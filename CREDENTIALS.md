# Blueprint Corpus: The Credential Map

Canonical map of the five credentials and the principles that give each its ethical voice. This is the credential map the Constitution's Article 6 cites ("Which principle voices which credential is held in the credential map"). It serves two readers: the runtime gate, resolving which credential a principle's verdict needs, and the author, deciding how a principle states its credential content. Load it before authoring or revising any principle's credential content, or any appendix that consumes a credential.

It is a sibling to `CROSS_PRINCIPLE.md` (co-requisites), `LEXICON.md` (action), and `CONSENT.md` (the consent fabric, expanding this map's Consent row). It sits beneath `CONSTITUTION.md` (the invariants) and above the principles (the content).

## 1. What this document is

The credential layer is the cross-cutting concern that runs beneath the principles: every consequential action is gated on five credentials, and this map says which principle gives each credential its ethical voice. The altitude is **ethics-first**: a principle owns a *duty*; a credential names the *enforcement concern* that duty implies; the Machine is the *canonical reference implementation* of that concern, and other implementations may meet it (Constitution, Article 11; invariants fixed, implementations open). The map states concerns and homes; it recites no patented construction.

## 2. The two-corpus frame

The corpus has two halves. The **Blueprint** is the content: the thirty-four principles, the human-readable Layer 1. The **Machine** is the enforcement substrate: the gate, the receipt, the five credentials, specified in the four papers and the filed portfolio. Layer 1 compiles to Layer 2 (YAML), anchored by Layer 3 (the policy hash), activated at Layer 4 (runtime).

Two readers consume a credentialed principle. An AI running the Machine compiles the credential to a real receipt at the gate. A compliant ally that does not run the Machine still owes the same duty and meets it by some other compliant means. The **duty is the invariant; the Machine is the reference implementation.**

## 3. The five credentials

The picture beneath the map: the three **information credentials** (Identity, Provenance, Consent) are the carried truth, the Composite Trust Proof an asset or person carries (Trust Machine diagrams 1–6). **Authorization** is the pulse, present at every governed action. **Liability** is bound to each pulse. The gate is the heart that checks them; the receipt is the trace each beat leaves.

- **Identity** asks *who acts, and who is affected.* Ethical home: **P2.4 (Human Identity Is Inviolable)**. It is the anchor the other four bind to, and it is co-required by P1.4, P2.5, P3.4, and P2.8. Standards anchor: First Person Project.
- **Provenance** asks *where the content or thing came from.* Homes: **P1.4 (Trust)** for the checkability of a claim, **P3.2 (Property)** for the attribution of a work, **P2.4** for the origin of identity markers. Requires Identity. Standards anchor: C2PA.
- **Consent** asks *who permitted it.* It has no single home; it circulates, and its fabric is mapped in **`CONSENT.md`**, the corpus-root consent map and the **single source for the validity bundle** (seven legs, including the necessity-void at 3.5's toll boundary), the three consent domains, the lifecycle, the circulation, and delegation. Its deepest scope specification is **P2.4 (the Consent Matrix)**; a consent failing any bundle leg is void at the gate (see `CONSENT.md` §2, the bundle's single statement, which this row only summarizes). Standards anchor: CAWG Consent Assertion.
- **Authorization** asks *whether the agent is in scope.* It is mostly operational, the throttle on a delegated task, and it is subordinate to the priority stack (Constitution, Article 4). Its thin ethical voice lives at **P2.8 (Self-Governance)** for collective authority and **P2.2 (Freedom)** for the individual's; **P2.5** carries the legitimate-authority (warrant) form. No standards body yet (a gap the action layer must close).
- **Liability** asks *who answers if it harms.* Ethical home: **P3.4 (Algorithmic Decisions Require Transparent Accountability)**. It resolves into the receipt (Constitution, Article 7), requires Identity to name the party and Authorization to carry the challenge pathway. No standards body yet (a gap).

**The initiator of an inbound action (the membership rule).** Where the AI is the counterparty to an action a non-human agent initiates, Identity, Authorization, and Liability are evaluated for that initiator on the same terms as for the acting agent (Constitution, Article 6); Provenance and Consent attach to content and rights-holders and are evaluated where the inbound action carries them. An ungoverned agent, a non-human actor presenting no verifiable credentials and leaving no receipt, is an unmet credential: a consequential action it initiates is blocked or escalated, and the verdict is receipted. A person is never an ungoverned agent.

**Findings the AI consumes.** Identity, Authorization, and Liability often rest on a finding signed by an external authority (a court's guardianship order, a license status, a conviction). Per Constitution Articles 3 and 8, the AI verifies and consumes such a finding. Adjudicating the legal status is the authority's alone, and absent a finding the AI presumes capacity and treats no person as carrying an adverse fact.

## 5. The cross-cutting enforcement objects (not credentials)

The gate, the receipt and its four conditions, the profile bar (person-exclusion), the priority stack, and the sphere-fractal are the Constitution's invariants (Articles 5, 7, 8, 4) realized in the Machine. None of them is a credential. This map does not restate them; it points up to the Constitution for the invariant, to `SUBSTRATE.md` for the register, the authority chain and the sphere fractal, and to the filed papers for the Machine specification.

## 6. How a principle states its credential (the write-rule)

State the duty mechanism-independently in Theoria (the *condition*: a person may contest a consequential decision; a use requires consent). Name the Machine as the canonical reference implementation in Phronesis or the appendix (the *construction*: the receipt, the consent assertion). The open corpus states conditions. It states no unfiled construction; the constructions live in the papers and the portfolio.

## 7. Method: capture incrementally, reconcile globally

Every principle's credential edge is declared here, marked **firm** (both ends canonical, the necessity clean). An amendment to a principle re-declares its edge, and a reconciliation pass verifies reciprocity, completeness, and clean Layer-2 compilation. This mirrors `CROSS_PRINCIPLE.md` §4, and the two graphs are siblings: the Five Credentials are the generator of most co-requisites.
