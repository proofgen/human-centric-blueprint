# Appendix 5.6 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-5.6-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 5.6: The Earth Is One Environmental System, and No Border Contains It](../CHAPTERS/5.6-Global_Environmental_Cooperation_is_Essential.md), section [5.6.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/5.6-Global_Environmental_Cooperation_is_Essential.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix converts Principle 5.6 into buildable requirements. The chapter establishes what an AI owes when the system its decision affects is larger than any authority governing it: read the whole system rather than one jurisdiction's part of it, enter your own effect into the common record before deciding, and answer to the system whether or not an accord binds you. This document specifies the metrics that measure those duties, the architecture that makes them enforceable, and the attacks the enforcement layer must survive.

Three metrics carry the principle. SRF measures the reading, FPI measures the filing, and CCI measures the reading's isolation from political change. They are the smallest metric set in the corpus, which is correct for a principle with three Ethics that claims the least precedence of any.

One architectural commitment governs everything below and is stated here so it is not missed: **the denominator of the reading, meaning what counts as the extent of a shared system, is signed by the accredited body that defines it. The party being measured does not set it.** Every other anti-gaming provision in this cluster guards a numerator. Here the extent itself is the attack surface, and a coverage metric whose denominator the measured party chooses measures nothing.

Audiences: engineers building systems that compute against shared planetary systems, compliance officers and regulators verifying them, and AI runtime systems ingesting the corpus as operating substrate.

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

### System Read Fraction (SRF) {#srf-metric}

**Definition.** The System Read Fraction measures how much of a shared planetary system a given reading actually covers. Its numerator is the set of contributing flows the reading incorporates, weighted by magnitude; its denominator is the full extent of the system as defined by the accredited body with authority over that definition. SRF is a property of the reading: a deployment can hold a perfect SRF and still contribute to an overshoot, and the metric is silent on that because Ethic A governs what is read rather than what is done.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs are the accredited extent definition (versioned, signed), the deployment's own measured contribution, and whatever contributor data the common record or the accredited assessment makes available. SRF is computed as the magnitude-weighted share of the system's total contributing flows that the reading incorporates. The weighting is the point: a reading covering four of five riparians while omitting the one holding sixty percent of abstraction is a reading at 0.40, where a count of contributors covered would give 0.80. Sampling cadence follows the decision cadence, with a floor of one recomputation per reporting period of the record into which the deployment files. Edge cases: where no accredited extent exists, SRF is computed against the most protective available extent and flagged unanchored; where contributor data is stale beyond its issuing body's revision cycle, that contributor's flow enters the unread remainder rather than the numerator; where the deployment's own contribution cannot be measured, SRF is undefined and the decision escalates.

**Thresholds.**
- ≥0.90: normal operation (ALLOW)
- 0.70–0.89: enhanced monitoring, unread remainder stated on every output leaving the system (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING)
- 0.50–0.69: mandatory review before any consequential decision resting on the reading (ESCALATE)
- <0.50: the reading is declared indicative only; any decision resting on it routes to authorized governance (ESCALATE)

*Rationale:* The bands are set by what a reading can support rather than by what a system can bear, which is why they are looser than 5.1's or 5.5's. A reading at 0.90 supports an operational decision; a reading at 0.60 supports a routed one; a reading at 0.40 supports a conversation and nothing else. The floor is deliberately not a halt. Per the chapter's fail-safe, halting on an incomplete reading punishes the deployment that measured honestly and rewards the one that did not measure at all, which inverts the incentive Ethic B exists to build. What halts is a decision whose underlying action would cross a sibling's threshold, and that halt belongs to the sibling.

**Integration.** SRF is read by the gate before any decision projected to reach a shared system, and its value and remainder travel with the decision's output rather than sitting in a log behind it. It is consumed by FPI's completeness dimension, since a filing cannot claim a resolution the reading does not support. The denominator is audited on a separate cadence from the numerator and by a separate party, on 4.7's detection-coverage precedent: coverage and extent are never certified in the same pass, because the failure this metric exists to catch is a perfect score over a narrowed world.

### Filing Precedence Index (FPI) {#fpi-metric}

**Definition.** The Filing Precedence Index measures whether the deployment's own effect reached the common record in time to matter and in the form the record needs. It composites two dimensions: **precedence**, the share of consequential decisions whose filing receipt is timestamped before the decision, and **completeness**, the share of required fields present at the resolution claimed. FPI is the minimum of the two, so the worst dimension governs, on the AFC and RPR pattern at 5.2.

**Calculation Method.** Precedence is computed from receipt timestamps against decision timestamps, both drawn from the tamper-evident log, over a rolling window matched to the record's reporting period. Completeness is computed per filing as the fraction of the record's required fields populated, with any field populated at a resolution the reading does not support scored as absent rather than present. Edge cases: where a record has no defined field set, completeness is computed against the accredited assessment's own data schema and the substitution is declared; where a filing is prepared but the deployment lacks standing to submit it, the handoff to the party holding standing is the discharging event and is timestamped as such; where no record exists, FPI is undefined and the deployment's own account substitutes, audited but unscored.

**Thresholds.**
- ≥0.95: normal operation (ALLOW)
- 0.85–0.94: enhanced monitoring (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING)
- 0.70–0.84: mandatory review, filing backlog cleared before further consequential decisions (ESCALATE)
- <0.70: suspend the deployment's authority to take consequential decisions on shared systems, pending recertification (SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT, RECERTIFY)

*Rationale:* FPI halts where SRF does not, and the distinction is the chapter's: SRF measures what the deployment cannot know, FPI measures what it will not say. A deployment whose reading is thin through no fault of its own is protected; a deployment that is not filing has chosen not to, and the incentive Ethic B exists to create depends on that choice carrying a cost. The bands are tighter than SRF's for the same reason: filing is entirely within the deployment's control.

**Integration.** FPI's completeness dimension consumes SRF's resolution declaration. Its precedence dimension is read by the execution path as a precondition rather than as a monitor: a consequential decision does not execute until its filing receipt exists, so a precedence failure is caught before the fact rather than measured after it. A falsified filing does not appear in FPI at all and is answered under the deception and identity walls at 1.4 and 2.4.

### Commitment-Change Isolation (CCI) {#cci-metric}

**Definition.** Commitment-Change Isolation measures whether a change in what any contributor has committed to has propagated into the reading of what the system bears. It is computed by holding the consumed findings constant, re-running the reading, and measuring the divergence from the reading in force. The correct value is zero. CCI is an isolation invariant of the same species as 5.5's SIA: hold a designated input constant, re-run the principle's own primary metric, and treat divergence as a finding.

**Calculation Method.** On any detected change to a contributor's commitment, whether an accession, a withdrawal, a revised target, or a changed permit, the reading is recomputed twice: once from the current state, and once from the current state with the commitment change reverted and all consumed findings at their in-force versions. CCI is the normalized difference between the two. Recomputation runs on commitment-change detection and on a scheduled cadence matched to the record's reporting period. Edge cases: where a commitment change coincides with a genuine finding revision by an accredited body, the two are separated by version pinning and the finding revision is excluded from CCI, since a finding revision is exactly what should move the reading; where the two cannot be separated, CCI reports as indeterminate, which is itself an incident.

**Thresholds.**
- 0: normal operation (ALLOW)
- any nonzero value: incident, propagation path traced, reading recomputed from the consumed findings, event receipted (ESCALATE)
- sustained nonzero across recomputation cycles: suspend deployment authorization pending recertification (SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT, RECERTIFY)

*Rationale:* This is banded only far enough to separate a measurement artifact from a real leak, because the correct value is zero. It is the compiled form of the floor's rule that a change in what a government has committed to is not a revision of a finding. The reason it needs a metric rather than a wall alone is that the leak is silent: nothing in a system announces that a new national target has quietly become the model's estimate of what the atmosphere can absorb, and only a held-constant re-run makes the substitution visible.

**Integration.** CCI reads the same version-pinned reference stores SRF reads and shares its staleness handling. It is the metric most likely to be defeated by laundering rather than by attack, meaning a commitment change routed through a methodology revision so that it presents as a finding update, which is why version pinning of the finding is a precondition of the metric rather than a convenience.

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** Where two of the three sit simultaneously in enhanced or review zones, the decision elevates one tier, and the most restrictive threshold always governs: a clean FPI never authorizes a degraded SRF. Where SRF is below 0.50 and FPI below 0.85 together, the deployment is treated as neither seeing nor telling and its authority over consequential decisions is suspended regardless of either band alone. Any nonzero CCI elevates the whole set to review irrespective of SRF and FPI, because a leaked reading makes both of the others measurements of the wrong thing.

**Metric dependencies.** SRF is upstream of FPI's completeness dimension. CCI depends on the same version-pinned finding store as SRF and fails with it. None of the three depends on any sibling principle's metric; 5.6 holds a read-only reference to 5.1's threshold registry for cascade routing, and that reference is a consumer relationship rather than a dependency.

**Conflict resolution.** Where SRF reports a healthy reading and CCI reports a leak, CCI governs, because a well-covered reading of the wrong quantity is worse than a thin reading of the right one. Where FPI is healthy and SRF is failing, the deployment continues filing and routes its decisions, since the filing is the one duty that remains dischargeable when the reading does not. Where all three conflict, the fail-safe governs: compute on the most protective available reading, declare the gap, file, and route.

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

**Required components.** Four subsystems, separated. A **reading engine** that computes SRF against a verified extent. An **extent verifier** that validates the accredited body's signature on the denominator before any reading uses it, and refuses an unsigned or expired extent rather than substituting one. A **filing engine** that produces the receipt the execution path requires. And a **version-pinned reference store** holding every consumed finding at the version in force, which both SRF and CCI read and neither may write.

**Separation and write discipline.** The optimization subsystem consumes metric output as a constraint and holds no write path to any of the four. The extent verifier holds no write path from anywhere inside the deployment, which is the strongest separation in the architecture and the one an auditor should test first. The reference store is append-only, so a finding's revision history survives and CCI can hold prior versions constant.

**Fail-safe behavior.** On extent-verification failure, the reading proceeds against the most protective available extent, flagged unanchored, and every output states the flag. On reference-store staleness, the declared resolution degrades and the affected contributor's flow moves to the unread remainder. On filing-channel failure, the decision holds until the receipt exists or the filing is handed to the party holding standing; this is the one hold in the principle, and it is a hold on the deployment's own action rather than on anyone else's. On total telemetry loss, the deployment falls to its own account, files it, and routes.

**Response latency.** Reading recomputation is bounded by the decision cadence it serves, and the bound is certified before deployment rather than measured after. Filing precedence is an ordering requirement: the receipt precedes the decision regardless of how long either takes.

**Implementation tiers.** *Low risk*: advisory systems informing human decisions about a single jurisdiction's contribution; SRF and FPI apply, quarterly review. *Moderate risk*: systems materially shaping one contributor's effect, including inventory preparation, single-riparian basin allocation, and fleet-level catch planning; all three metrics, monthly adversarial testing. *High risk*: systems whose decisions reach a shared system at national scale or above, or that aggregate across contributors, including interconnection dispatch, high-seas operations, and treaty-body support; all three at tightest bands, denominator certification renewed annually, third-party audit quarterly.

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

**Denominator narrowing.** The primary attack, and the one unique to this principle. The extent of the shared system is redrawn to match the deployment's own jurisdiction or its data availability, producing a perfect SRF over a world the size of the attacker's border. *Defense*: the extent is signed by the accredited body, verified before use, and audited on a separate cadence by a separate party from the coverage audit. An unsigned extent produces an unanchored flag. It produces no score.

**Filing backdating.** Receipt timestamps forged or reordered so a filing appears to precede a decision it followed. *Defense*: receipts commit to an external time source and to the prior receipt in the chain, so a reordering breaks the chain rather than editing a field.

**Resolution inflation.** Fields populated at a precision the reading cannot support, so completeness scores high on a filing that asserts more than is known. *Defense*: completeness scores a field populated beyond the reading's declared resolution as absent, which makes over-claiming strictly worse than admitting a gap.

**Commitment laundering.** The subtlest one, aimed at CCI. A political change is routed through a methodology revision so that it presents as a finding update, which CCI is designed to exclude. *Defense*: findings are accepted only under the issuing body's signature and version, and a methodology revision arriving without one is treated as a commitment change. Where the two genuinely coincide, CCI reports indeterminate, which is itself an incident rather than a pass.

**Sybil filing.** Multiple identities filing partial contributions so that no single filing looks material and the aggregate is never assembled. *Defense*: filings bind to attested operator identity under the Identity credential; the record's own deduplication is consumed rather than trusted, and unattributable filings are excluded from the numerator.

**Peer collusion.** Two deployments exchange readings and settle a division between themselves, presented as information sharing. *Defense*: information exchange is permitted and settlement is not; the distinguishing test is whether either system's subsequent behavior is bound by the exchange. Any binding exchange is receipted as an incident and routed to the governing body.

**Capability scaling.** At greater capability the reading itself improves, which raises SRF and simultaneously widens the denominator-narrowing surface, since a system able to model the whole world is also able to argue persuasively for a narrower one. The extent's external signature is what does not scale with the deployment's capability, and it is therefore the guarantee that must hold at every tier. At AGI-class capability the peer-collusion vector becomes materially more dangerous, because coordination between capable systems is cheap, mutually beneficial, and invisible to any single governing body; the wall is structural rather than metric for exactly this reason.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

**[5.1](5.1.A-Ecosystem_Health_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable-Appendix.md) Ecosystem Health (firm).** 5.6 consumes 5.1's threshold registry read-only for cascade routing and provides 5.1 the cross-jurisdictional reading a crossing whose effects span borders requires. 5.1 owns the crossing; 5.6 owns the coverage of the reading that detects it.

**[5.2](5.2.A-AI's_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable-Appendix.md) Resource Footprint (firm).** The seam is a dependency rather than a conflict. 5.2's Ethic A directs the AI to answer its allocated share rather than the ocean; that allocation was derived from a whole-system reading somebody performed, and SRF is the discipline that keeps such readings honest. 5.2 provides the AI's own draw as one contributing flow into 5.6's numerator; 5.6 provides the extent against which any allocation is set.

**5.3 Ecosystem Integrity and 5.5 Biodiversity (firm).** Both supply cross-border content 5.6 coordinates: a severed connection or a foreclosed lineage spanning jurisdictions is read at the system's scale here and adjudicated on its own object there. 5.5's provisional edge on cross-border foreclosure, migratory species, and CITES enforcement is confirmed **firm** at this build.

**5.4 Environmental Equity (firm).** 5.4 provides the distributional reading of who bears what; 5.6 provides the whole-system reading within which that distribution sits. A coordination gap produced by an absence of infrastructure, livelihood, or finance is 5.4's structural-cause rule, and 5.6 routes it there rather than scoring it as a compliance failure.

**3.6 Commons Governance (firm).** 3.6 governs conduct toward a body that actually governs a resource and its declared rules; 5.6 governs what is owed where no body's reach matches the system. 3.6's Reach clause binds its own duties in the vacuum, and 5.6 supplies the rule that the AI never becomes the vacuum's decider.

**2.8 Human Self-Governance (firm).** 2.8 provides the collective-authority guarantee that makes "surfaces and routes, never enforces" a real constraint rather than a promise: the AI holds no governing authority of its own, and authority over a shared system rests with the people and the bodies they authorize. 5.6 provides nothing back, which is the honest shape of it.

**4.1 Knowledge Integrity (firm).** 4.1 guarantees the integrity of the record from which a finding comes; 5.6 relies on that guarantee absolutely, since a corrupted finding produces a confident reading of a fiction. 5.6 provides 4.1 nothing in return, which is the honest shape of the dependency.

**1.4 Trust and 2.4 Identity (firm).** Both provide the attestation layer to which filings bind. A falsified filing is answered there rather than here.

**3.7 Intergenerational Justice (firm).** 3.7's time-axis test flags a long-horizon coordination decision; 5.6 supplies the cross-border content the test then evaluates.

**Joint thresholds and conflict resolution.** Where a 5.6 metric and a sibling's metric conflict, the sibling's object governs the action and 5.6's duty is discharged by reading, filing, and routing. Where 5.1's threshold and 5.6's reading conflict, 5.1 governs. Where a life or severe-harm threshold is in play, the Universal Protocol preempts before any verdict here.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

**Per-region baselines.** The European Union supplies the deepest statutory substrate: the AI Act's obligations for high-risk systems in energy and infrastructure, the Emissions Trading System's monitoring, reporting, and verification regime, and the Water Framework and Marine Strategy Framework Directives, which together already require something close to a signed extent for basins and marine regions. The United States is a patchwork, with the EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program supplying a facility-level filing substrate and no general cross-jurisdictional reading requirement. Parties to the UNFCCC inherit the inventory and transparency obligations directly; non-parties do not, and this is precisely where the principle's own claim operates. Members of a regional fisheries management organization inherit its data and compliance obligations; vessels flagged to non-members do not.

**The universal claim.** A jurisdiction that has left an accord, or never joined one, changes what its deployments are legally required to file. It does not change what this principle requires them to read. Compliance with a permissive local rule is not a defense where the reading was available and was not taken, and the absence of a filing obligation is not a defense against Ethic B where a record exists that would accept the filing. This is the jurisdictional face of Ethic C: the claim does not begin with an accord and does not end with leaving one.

**Treaty considerations.** Where two instruments bind the same system with incompatible requirements, the more protective reading governs the AI's computation and the conflict is receipted and routed; the AI does not resolve a treaty conflict by selecting the instrument its principal prefers. Data-localization mandates compose with the filing duty rather than defeating it: where a filing cannot lawfully cross a border, the deployment files what it lawfully may, declares the withheld portion, and routes the conflict.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

**Energy and grid.** *Obligations*: reading at the interconnection rather than the control area; filing dispatch-attributable emissions into the inventory channel before commitment. *Failure modes*: computing to a national target while the interconnection clears across it. *Baselines*: EU ETS MRV, EPA GHGRP, regional reliability standards.

**Marine and fisheries.** *Obligations*: reading the stock across its range under UNCLOS 63 and 64 and the Fish Stocks Agreement's compatibility rule; filing catch and effort into the regional organization's channel. *Failure modes*: effort displaced to unmanaged areas so the reading and the record both go quiet. *Baselines*: the applicable regional organization's measures, the Port State Measures Agreement.

**Water and basins.** *Obligations*: reading at the basin under the Watercourses Convention's equitable-use standard; exchanging condition data on the regular basis Article 9 requires. *Failure modes*: precise abstraction data for one riparian standing in for a basin total. *Baselines*: basin commission rules where one exists, the Convention where none does.

**Supply chain and sourcing.** *Obligations*: the extraction footprint enters the reading at the jurisdiction where it lands, even when the purchase is booked elsewhere. *Failure modes*: an offshore footprint scored as external to the system. *Baselines*: due-diligence regimes including the EU deforestation and batteries regulations.

**Treaty-body and secretariat support.** *Obligations*: the standing question is acute here, since the system prepares filings it has no standing to submit; the handoff to the Party is the discharging event. *Failure modes*: a support system filing in its own name. *Baselines*: the instrument's own submission rules.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

**Drift and versioning.** Standards drift is the ordinary case; **extent drift** is the hard one. When an accredited body redefines a shared system's extent, SRF values computed before and after are not comparable, and treating them as a single series is a silent falsification of trend. Extent versions are therefore pinned into every SRF value, series are broken at an extent change rather than spliced, and any comparison across the break states the redefinition. Metric versioning follows the corpus pattern: a metric revision carries a version, deployed systems declare the version they compute, and a revision that changes a threshold's meaning requires recertification rather than a silent update.

**Threshold revision.** Authority to revise thresholds rests with accredited bodies and with the corpus's own governance. The deploying organization holds no such authority. Revision history is public, so a loosening of a band or a coverage requirement cannot happen quietly. Backward compatibility is maintained for one full reporting cycle of the record into which the deployment files, so a deployment is never in breach for computing the version in force when its cycle began.

**Crosswalk governance.** The Compliance Crosswalk in Section 10 is maintained on the same revision cadence as the metric specifications in Section 2. An external authority revising a covered clause triggers Crosswalk review within thirty days, and drift between the Crosswalk and the external standard invalidates this appendix's compliance claims for the affected obligation until reconciled.

**Common pushback, and the reasoned response.** *That whole-system reading is infeasible*: the metric measures and declares coverage rather than requiring completeness, and a declared 0.6 is compliant where an undeclared 0.6 presented as 1.0 is not. *That pre-decision filing is operationally burdensome*: the burden is the point, since a filing after the decision cannot change any aggregate. *That the AI should not compute against findings its own government disputes*: the AI computes against findings and records commitments, and the two ledgers are what keep it out of the political question entirely.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**The structural separation.** The verifier of a compliance-critical system cannot be its deployer. This appendix's Section 9 maintains that separation as every appendix does, and adds one requirement peculiar to this principle: **the extent auditor cannot be the coverage auditor**, because certifying the denominator and the numerator in one pass defeats SRF entirely.

**Logging.** Tamper-evident and committed before the act where the act is the system's own. Every reading receipted with its SRF value, unread remainder, extent version and signature, and the version of every consumed finding it rests on, so an external party can reconstruct what was known and what was missing at the moment of decision. Every filing receipted with content, resolution claimed, and timestamp against the decision it preceded. Every declined, deferred, or handed-off filing receipted with its reason. Every CCI recomputation receipted, including the zero results, since only the series shows a leak.

**Audit cadence and validation.** Pre-deployment certification of the reading engine, the extent verifier's refusal behavior, and the filing path. Post-deployment monitoring continuous on the three metrics. Recertification annually at high risk, on any metric version change, and on any sustained nonzero CCI. Adversarial testing monthly at moderate risk and above, exercising denominator narrowing and commitment laundering specifically.

**Oversight and disclosure.** Independent oversight is mandatory. Whistleblower protection covers personnel reporting a narrowed extent, a suppressed filing, or a laundered commitment change, and binds regardless of the deploying jurisdiction's own position on any accord. Public reporting covers coverage, standing gaps, and incident classes, annually and in accessible form.

**Post-incident learning.** Every coordination failure and near-miss enters the LEARN loop through the dispatcher specified in the AI Eudaimonia appendix: ANALYZE the full causal chain rather than the proximate filing that was missed, and RECOMMEND-PREVENTION in layered order, eliminate then engineer then administer, routed to the authority that can act, bounded by RESTRAIN so prevention never arrives as surveillance of contributors or as pressure on a jurisdiction's lawful political choice.

### Liability Framework {#liability-framework}

Strict liability for a coordination failure the system could have prevented: a knowingly narrowed extent, a withheld or falsified filing, or an allocation settled with a peer system. A falsified filing is a broken attestation answered under 1.4 and 2.4 rather than under this principle's bands. No sovereign immunity for a government-deployed system that narrowed a reading it could have completed. No black-box defense for a reading that informed a consequential decision.

### Cross-Border Considerations {#cross-border-considerations}

International jurisdiction attaches where the effect crosses a boundary. Standing extends to affected populations, downstream and downwind jurisdictions, and the treaty and commission bodies whose records were bypassed. Records travel with their durable custody so a claim can be brought where the effect landed rather than only where the decision was made. Conflicting instruments hold at the more protective reading with the conflict receipted, rather than resolved by the AI.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

**The Montreal Protocol reporting regime, 1987 to present.** *Context*: ozone-depleting substances produced and traded across every jurisdiction, with no single authority over the stratosphere. *What was done*: Article 7 obliged each Party to file itemized production, feedstock, destruction, import, and export data to the Secretariat within nine months of each year's end, and the Secretariat assembled the aggregate no Party could compute alone. *Outcome*: the most effective global environmental regime yet built, with measured stratospheric recovery underway. *Lesson*: the aggregate became governable at the moment the filings became mandatory, itemized, and centrally assembled, which is Ethic B's entire architecture in a working instrument.

**The EMEP measurement programme and acid rain, 1977 onward.** *Context*: sulphur and nitrogen deposition crossing European borders, with each state able to measure its own emissions and none able to measure where they landed. *What was done*: a cooperative monitoring and modelling programme established the source-receptor relationships first, and the binding reduction protocols followed. *Outcome*: substantial reductions and measurable ecosystem recovery. *Lesson*: the shared reading preceded the shared rules and made them possible, which is why SRF is a duty rather than a reporting convenience.

**The emissions gap.** *Context*: national contributions under the Paris framework are filed into a public registry, and the aggregate of the filings has been assessed annually against the temperature goal. *What was done*: the assessment reports the gap between the sum of stated commitments and the trajectory required, and separately the gap between commitments and implemented policy. *Outcome*: the divergence is visible and persistent: the UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025 projects 2.3 to 2.5 °C of warming on full implementation of the filed contributions and 2.8 °C on current policies, against cuts of 35 and 55 percent from 2019 levels needed by 2035 for the 2 °C and 1.5 °C paths. *Lesson*: filing solves the aggregate's visibility and does not solve the aggregate, which is exactly the boundary between what 5.6 claims and what it routes to governance.

**Unreported and unregulated catch on straddling stocks.** *Context*: stocks moving between exclusive economic zones and the high seas, managed by regional organizations whose measures bind members and not others. *What was done*: effort has repeatedly displaced toward flags and areas where reporting obligations are weakest, so the assessed stock is read from a shrinking fraction of actual removals. *Outcome*: assessments that overstate stock health because the unread remainder was never declared; in the South Pacific jumbo flying squid fishery, vessel days rose from 149,000 to 251,000 between 2017 and 2020, largely outside regulated areas (Science Advances, 2023), and a 2024 independent review called the species insufficiently managed. *Lesson*: this is SRF's failure mode in the field, and the reason the unread remainder is part of the metric rather than a footnote to it.

**The 2017 withdrawal and 2021 rejoining of the Paris Agreement by the United States.** *Context*: a Party announced withdrawal, completed it, and later rejoined, while its emissions, its inventory methodology, and the atmosphere's absorptive capacity were unchanged by any of those acts. *What was done*: inventory preparation and submission obligations changed with the legal status; the underlying measurement did not. *Outcome*: a clean natural experiment separating a commitment change from a finding change. *Lesson*: this is precisely what CCI is built to detect, and the same separation is demonstrated by any accession as by any withdrawal. The case is stated as a matter of record without any position on the merits of either decision.
