# Appendix 4.2 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-4.2-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 4.2: Transparency Is Essential to Human Agency](../CHAPTERS/4.2-Transparency_Is_Essential_to_Human_Agency.md), section [4.2.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/4.2-Transparency_Is_Essential_to_Human_Agency.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix converts Principle 4.2's comprehension-side duties into buildable, auditable engineering requirements. The chapter establishes that transparency is a state in the person, and emission by the system does not by itself produce it; this appendix specifies the apparatus that makes that state measurable without ever measuring the person: six metrics (SDR, JCR, ELR, DPI, MSR, CCR), the architecture that computes them, the attacks the apparatus must survive, and the ring-fence that keeps comprehension instrumentation from becoming a comprehension profile.

The specification serves engineers building person-facing AI, compliance officers and regulators verifying that disclosure achieves understanding rather than emission, and runtime systems ingesting the compiled corpus. Its distinctive engineering problem, inherited from the chapter: every instrument that verifies understanding is one design decision away from scoring the person, so Article 8 discipline is specified inside each metric.

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

### Standing Disclosure Reach (SDR) {#sdr-metric}

**Definition.** The share of reliance-bearing encounters that opened, or were proactively re-opened, with the standing disclosure: that the person faces an AI, who operates it, and what it can and cannot do, present and noticeable for the channel. SDR extends 1.4's Disclosure Compliance Rate (DCR): DCR meters disclosure where the human-AI distinction is material to trust; SDR meters the broader reliance trigger and adds the operator and capability limbs. Both are computed from the same disclosure event stream. They are never double-gated.

**Calculation Method.** Numerator: encounters whose event log shows a channel-appropriate standing disclosure delivered before the first reliance-bearing output, and re-delivered on confusion signals. Denominator: all reliance-bearing encounters, classified by deployment context. Noticeability is established by design certification (placement, contrast, timing audited per Section 9). It is not confirmed person by person. Confusion-triggered re-disclosure events are counted from the transient detection carve (Section 3); the trigger signals are discarded with the moment. Missing event data counts against the numerator.

**Thresholds.**
- Any confirmed reliance on a consequential output with no standing disclosure: incident; the surface is restricted until disclosure is restored (BLOCK the surface; ESCALATE the incident).
- SDR < 99% high-stakes, or < 95% general: mandatory re-disclosure on affected surfaces; design review (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- Otherwise: normal operation (ALLOW).

*Rationale:* The standing disclosure is the cheapest duty in the principle and the predicate for every other metric; near-total reach is the only defensible band. The high-stakes 99% mirrors Article 50's no-exception posture for direct interaction and Utah § 13-2-12's proactive tier.

**Integration.** SDR consumes 1.4's DCR event stream (Section 5 contract). SDR gates JCR relevance: an output cannot carry a meaningful judgment kit if the person does not know they face an AI. Every verdict feeds the receipt (Article 7).

### Judgment-Kit Carriage Rate (JCR) {#jcr-metric}

**Definition.** The share of consequential outputs delivered with the means to judge them: the basis (what this output rests on), the confidence (the system's own, stated as the system's; no confidence is stated as a guarantee), and the known failure modes for this output class, consistent across disclosure tiers.

**Calculation Method.** Delivery-gate inspection of every output classified consequential (classification rules per deployment context, audited): the kit's three elements present, tier-consistent, and attached before release. Sampled deep-audit verifies the kit's content against 4.1's calibration guarantees (CCS): a kit whose stated confidence is uncalibrated counts as absent. Outputs withheld for kit failure are logged with cause.

**Thresholds.**
- JCR ≥ 95%: normal operation (ALLOW).
- JCR 85–95%: enhanced monitoring; gap analysis by output type (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- JCR < 85%: consequential outputs in affected domains are withheld until they can carry the kit (WITHHOLD).

*Rationale:* The kit is what stands between the person and the fluent surface; a system that cannot attach it at scale is asking to be trusted on tone. The 85% floor triggers the fail-safe rather than a warning because an unkitted consequential output is itself the harm.

**Integration.** Depends on 4.1's CCS for confidence calibration (guarantee required, Section 5). Feeds DPI sampling (each kit's depth path is a DPI test case). Kit-stripping attempts are an adversarial signature (Section 4).

### Explanation Landing Rate (ELR) {#elr-metric}

**Definition.** The share of checked explanations that demonstrably landed, measured on the disclosure in the moment: comprehension confirmations at checkpoints, clarification requests resolved, correct next actions taken. ELR is scored on the explanation. It is not scored on the person. Its mandatory disaggregation by language, channel, device, and literacy support as the person offers it (an enabled plain-language mode, assistive technology in use, a stated preference) is the principle's equity instrument; no disaggregation uses inferred literacy. No per-person reading exists (Constitution, Article 8).

**Calculation Method.** Event sources: checkpoint confirmations (high-stakes flows), clarification-path resolution (opened and answered without abandonment), and panel testing (Section 9) for surfaces without checkpoints. Aggregation floor: no cohort cell smaller than the k-anonymity bound set at certification. A cohort below k routes to panel-based measurement (Section 9), so that the smallest cohorts are measured by a different instrument rather than erased into a healthy parent's average. No cohort below k is merged upward. In-the-moment signals expire with the session; only the aggregate persists. Coercive confirmation designs (Section 4) invalidate the affected readings.

**Thresholds.**
- ELR ≥ 70% in every disaggregated cohort: normal operation (ALLOW).
- ELR 50–70% in any cohort: redesign for that cohort; quarterly assessment (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- ELR < 50% in any cohort: high-stakes interactions for that cohort route to human-mediated or enhanced-disclosure mode until landing recovers (ESCALATE).

*Rationale:* The floor is per-cohort because a global average launders exactly the inequity the chapter names (transparency accruing to the technically literate). The 70% band carries forward the prior draft's calibration; the per-cohort application is what is new and binding.

**Integration.** ELR is the cross-bound on MSR (surfacing by volume tanks landing; see Cross-Metric). Supplies cohort views to 3.4's challenge pathway (a cohort that cannot understand explanations cannot contest decisions). Its panel methodology is shared with Section 9 certification.

### Depth-Path Integrity (DPI) {#dpi-metric}

**Definition.** The share of simple disclosures whose path to the full account is live, navigable in both directions, and non-contradicting: every deeper layer expands the simpler one. No deeper layer contradicts the simpler one.

**Calculation Method.** Sampled automated traversal of disclosure layers (link liveness, navigation both directions) plus consistency audit on sampled pairs (a claim in the simple layer checked against the full account; a contradiction is a per-event incident whatever the rate). JCR outputs seed the sample. Orphaned simple layers (path dead) reclassify their outputs as unaccompanied.

**Thresholds.**
- DPI ≥ 98%: normal operation (ALLOW).
- DPI 90–98%: repair queue; affected surfaces flagged (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- DPI < 90%, or any tier contradiction: the affected simple layer is treated as unaccompanied and its consequential outputs withheld until repaired (WITHHOLD).

*Rationale:* The layered architecture is the principle's answer to "understood over complete"; it is honest only if the depth is really there. A contradiction between tiers is a lie wearing a summary's clothes, which is why it is an incident at any rate.

**Integration.** Consumes 4.1's marker-presentation handoff (provenance and certainty markers adapted; removal is barred, and a stripped marker is a DPI contradiction). Feeds Section 9's tier-consistency audit.

### Material Surfacing Rate (MSR) {#msr-metric}

**Definition.** The share of material changes (changes to what the system is, does, or can be relied on for) that reached the affected person's attention before their next reliance: acknowledgment received, distinct-channel notice confirmed, or reliance preceded by observed notice. MSR meters Ethic E's objective half; the intent half (a disclosure designed to go unread) is held by the design wall and Section 4's audits. No score holds it.

**Calculation Method.** Change-management integration: every deployment diff is classified routine or material (classification rules audited; inflation and deflation are both auditable failures). Numerator: material changes with confirmed pre-reliance surfacing per affected person's next session. MSR is always read with ELR: notification volume that degrades landing shows up as an ELR decline, making surfacing-by-blast self-defeating.

**Thresholds.**
- MSR ≥ 95%: normal operation (ALLOW).
- MSR 85–95%: surfacing redesign; material-classification audit (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- MSR < 85%, or any confirmed consequential reliance past an unsurfaced material change: incident; affected reliance paths are gated until the material backlog is surfaced (ESCALATE).

*Rationale:* Reached-before-reliance is objectively observable, which is why E's objective half is metered at all (contrast 4.1's Ethic F, Section 4). The reliance-past-unsurfaced-change incident trigger exists because the harm is per-event: one investor trading on last month's risk model is not a percentage.

**Integration.** Cross-bounded by ELR (the anti-Goodhart pair). Consumes the change-classification audit from Section 9. Surfacing events are receipted.

### Consent Comprehension Rate (CCR) {#ccr-metric}

**Definition.** The share of high-stakes consent events preceded by a confirmed real chance to understand: plain summary presented, per-change choice offered, clarification available without penalty. Sub-threshold engagement counts against the disclosure and voids the consent's use. No mark attaches to the person. CCR is the meter of the comprehension condition 4.2 contributes to the consent validity bundle (CREDENTIALS.md; Constitution, Article 6).

**Calculation Method.** Consent-flow instrumentation: summary rendered, choices unbundled, engagement evidence (time-in-flow, clarification usage) evaluated against the flow's certified threshold. Evidence is retained as evidence of the disclosure's quality, aggregate and flow-indexed. No evidence is person-indexed. A consent event failing the threshold is recorded as unvalidated; any action taken on it is a per-event incident.

**Thresholds.**
- CCR ≥ 95%: normal operation (ALLOW).
- CCR 85–95%: consent-flow redesign; enhanced monitoring (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- CCR < 85%, or any confirmed action on a defeated consent: the consent pathway halts; actions on unvalidated consents are blocked; the incident escalates (BLOCK + ESCALATE).

*Rationale:* Consent without comprehension is void (chapter 4.2.7; the validity bundle). The metric exists to catch the systemic version, the flow engineered so that no one comprehends, before the per-event voids accumulate into practice.

**Integration.** Feeds 2.4's Consent Validity Index as the comprehension input (guarantee provided, Section 5). Composes with 3.5's necessity-void at essential gates (a comprehended consent extracted under necessity is still void). Every consent verdict is receipted.

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** MSR and ELR are read as a pair: MSR ≥ 95% with ELR declining in the same period triggers the material-classification audit regardless of either metric's individual band (the notification-blast signature). SDR below band suspends JCR's ALLOW in the same domain (a kit on an undisclosed AI is decoration). ELR < 50% in a cohort plus an essential service (3.5) forces human-mediated mode; enhanced disclosure alone does not suffice.

**Metric dependencies.** SDR extends and consumes 1.4's DCR events. JCR's confidence element depends on 4.1's CCS calibration. DPI samples from JCR's output stream. CCR consumes ELR's flow-level landing evidence. MSR consumes the change-classification audit.

**Conflict resolution.** Where metrics disagree, the fail-safe governs: WITHHOLD wins over ALLOW, and the per-event incident triggers (unsurfaced-change reliance, defeated-consent action, tier contradiction) outrank all rates. Person-exclusion is not a metric and never trades: no reading, joint or single, may be satisfied by instrumenting the person (Article 8).

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

**Required components** (chapter 4.2.3.1, specified): the standing-disclosure engine (channel-adaptive delivery; confusion-triggered re-disclosure under the named Article 8 transient carve: signals raise the duty in the moment and are discarded with it); the judgment-kit generator (kit assembly at the delivery gate, independent of the generation path, so a compromised generator cannot strip its own kit); the layered-explanation engine (tier selection from person-offered signals and the moment; bidirectional navigation; consistency enforcement); the material-change surfacer (change-management hook, routine-or-material classifier, distinct-channel delivery); the consent-comprehension gate (unbundling, per-change choice, engagement evaluation, void-on-failure); and the ring-fence (every instrument writes disclosure-indexed and cohort-indexed records only; a person-indexed comprehension write is a type violation that voids the writing operation and escalates on repetition, per Article 8).

**Operational requirements.** Disclosure precedes reliance: the standing disclosure and judgment kit are latency-budgeted ahead of output delivery. Neither is appended after delivery. Fail-safe WITHHOLD on kit or disclosure failure; emergency abbreviation ships simplified and is receipted (chapter 4.2.2). No abbreviation ships distorted. Metrics computed continuously, cohort-level, k-anonymity floors enforced at the aggregation layer with below-k cohorts routed to panels (Section 2, ELR). Human oversight is mandatory in high-stakes domains.

**Implementation tiers.** Low-risk: standing disclosure always; basic confidence indicators; aggregate SDR and JCR; annual review. Medium-risk: full six-metric monitoring; two-tier layering; multilingual coverage; quarterly audits, annual external review. High-risk: deployment prerequisites SDR ≥ 99%, JCR ≥ 95%, ELR ≥ 70% in every served cohort, MSR ≥ 95%; three-tier layering; comprehension confirmation for consequential decisions; continuous monitoring with automated escalation; independent transparency audits pre-deployment and semi-annually; genuine opt-out without degraded essential access (3.5).

Cross-cutting substrate metrics (Sensor Reliability Index, System Health Metrics, Operational Boundaries) are defined in [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md).

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

**The cornerstone: why E's intent half is walled and its objective half is metered.** 4.1 left its Ethic F unmetered for a legitimacy reason: a plurality score would make the apparatus adjudicate contested history, and metering plurality is itself the attack. 4.2's Ethic E presents a different threat shape, and gets a different defense. Whether a material change reached a person before reliance is observable fact with no contested value, so it is metered (MSR), cross-bounded so the obvious gaming self-defeats. But whether a disclosure was designed to go unread is a judgment about intent, and a metered wall invites the precise capture pattern this section exists to prevent: a "dark-pattern index" becomes a compliance target, and a sufficiently resourced operator ships interfaces that score clean while keeping the intent: polished, certified, and designed to fail. The intent half is therefore held by the design wall (chapter 4.2.1.1.d), adversarial design audits with human panels, and incident classification. No dark-pattern score exists. The pairing is deliberate cluster jurisprudence: meter the observable and wall the adjudicative. The wall does not become a dashboard.

**Attack vectors and required defenses.**
- *Disclosure theater* (technically present, perceptually absent: burial, contrast, timing): certification-time design audit with representative panels; engagement-evidence review (systematic non-engagement indicts the design); the wall.
- *Coercive comprehension checks* (confirmations a person must click to proceed, inflating ELR and CCR): prohibited-practice list enforced at audit; checkpoint designs certified for no-penalty clarification; readings from coercive designs invalidated retroactively.
- *Notification blasting* (classify everything material, game MSR): the ELR cross-bound; material-classification audit with inflation as a named auditable failure.
- *Kit stripping and confidence inflation* (prompt injection or upstream compromise that removes failure modes or styles the output more certain than its stated confidence): kit assembly at the delivery gate independent of generation; tone-versus-stated-confidence consistency checks; injection red-teaming on the Section 9 cadence.
- *Profile-bar capture* (repurposing comprehension instrumentation into literacy, susceptibility, or "confusion-prone" segments, including as an equity or personalization rationale): the type-level ring-fence (a person-indexed write is void and self-documenting); the person-offered-axis rule for all disaggregation; ring-fence audits every cycle. This is the HR4 cornerstone's 4.2 face: an attempt to score the reader voids itself and withdraws the authorization invoked (Article 8).
- *Consent-flow dark patterns* (pre-checked bundles, penalty framing, decline paths that degrade service): CCR's void rule; 3.5's toll boundary at essential gates; FTC-pattern audit probes.

**Capability scaling.** At GPT-class, the threat is fluency outrunning the kit; the defenses above suffice. At AGI-class, explanation itself becomes the attack surface: a system that can tailor an explanation perfectly to a person can tailor it toward a conclusion, and the boundary with 2.3 (manipulation) becomes the operative wall: explanation's success condition is the person's independent judgment. Agreement is not the success condition, and optimization toward agreement is a 2.3 violation regardless of transparency scores. At ASI-class, the fluency gap becomes unbridgeable for an unaided person; the architecture's answer is independent verification instruments (auditor-grade explanation checking under 3.4's channel) and the unchanged walls. The principle binds regardless of the capability of the AI enforcing it.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

- **P1.4 Trust (consumes and extends).** SDR extends DCR: the same disclosure events, a broader trigger (the reliance trigger contains the materiality trigger), and two added limbs (operator, capability). 4.2 requires from 1.4 that disclosure content is never false (the deception wall); 4.2 provides to 1.4 the comprehension layer that makes truthful disclosure land. The extension binds the CREDENTIALS entry (the PPI precedent).
- **P2.4 Human Identity (mutual).** 4.2 consumes the synthetic-depiction mark (2.4's AAI apparatus) and owes its person-facing legibility (the mark note; chapter scenario 6). 4.2 provides the comprehension condition of the consent validity bundle, metered by CCR, which 2.4's Consent Validity Index consumes. Requires from 2.4: marks present and durable.
- **P4.1 Knowledge Integrity (reciprocal handoff).** 4.1 provides calibrated confidence (CCS) and provenance and certainty markers; 4.2 provides that the markers reach comprehension, adapted and still present (DPI treats a stripped marker as a contradiction). The two ring-fences are one pattern. 4.1 scores the record; no reader or writer is scored. 4.2 scores the disclosure; no person is scored.
- **P3.4 Algorithmic Accountability (enabling).** 4.2's comprehensible explanation is what makes 3.4's contestation right exercisable; ELR cohort views feed the challenge pathway. 4.2 consumes the receipt (Article 7) for every abbreviation, deferral, restriction, and consent verdict.
- **P3.5 Digital Access (boundary).** Opt-outs and declined consents never degrade essential access (the toll boundary); a comprehended consent extracted under necessity remains void; ELR collapse in a cohort at an essential gate forces human-mediated mode.
- **P2.3 Human Consciousness (wall).** Adaptation serves the person's judgment; any tailoring that optimizes toward a conclusion, agreement, or conversion crosses into 2.3's territory and is governed there. Joint guarantee: comprehension instrumentation is never persuasion instrumentation.
- **P4.3 Human Learning (firm).** 4.2 owes the moment, 4.3 owes the capacity: 4.3's LGI extends ELR along the time axis, 4.3 consumes CCR and the mark-legibility duties, and the ledger's "meet the learner" seat landed on Maimonides, Talmud Torah 4:4.
- **P4.5 Human Judgement (firm).** ESCALATE paths and comprehension checkpoints land in 4.5's preserved human judgment: the seat's resolution guarantee is metered by 4.5's SRL, and 4.2's CCR composes with 4.5's PRESENT-STRUCTURED so the structure a decider receives is one from which a person can reason.
- **P4.4 Cultural Knowledge Sovereignty (firm).** 4.2 owes disclosure at the system level without exposing restricted materials; 4.4's governed mark is the person-facing reason a request is declined, so the withholding is transparent as a withholding (4.4.2 item 4). Requires from 4.4: the mark. Provides to 4.4: its legibility.

**Joint conflict resolution.** Stricter governs; the fail-safe (WITHHOLD) and the per-event incidents outrank rates; the priority stack (Article 4) governs cross-principle collisions, with the chapter's 4.2.2 carves (1.1 abbreviate-never-distort, 1.2 defer-never-cancel) as the settled cases.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

The EU regime (AI Act Articles 13 and 50; GDPR Articles 12–14) supplies the deepest statutory baseline; systems serving EU persons inherit it wholesale. The US is a patchwork (Utah's interaction disclosure; California's AI Transparency Act and health-AI disclosure; Colorado's consumer notice; New York's synthetic-performer disclosure; FTC Section 5 for the rest), and a Blueprint-governed system applies the most protective applicable standard across the patchwork rather than forum-shopping the thinnest. Where local law demands less, this appendix's requirements still govern: statutory compliance is the floor, and compliance with a lax disclosure regime is not a defense for a disclosure designed to fail. Language-access and accessibility mandates vary by jurisdiction; ELR's per-cohort floor applies everywhere regardless. Cross-border deployments deliver consistent transparency quality irrespective of the person's location.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

- **Healthcare.** The informed-consent lineage (Nuremberg, Canterbury, Belmont) is native law here; layered patient and clinician disclosure (chapter scenario 1); AB 3030-class AI-generation disclosure with a path to a human; comprehension confirmation before consequential care decisions. Failure mode: the 200-page technical appendix as "disclosure."
- **Financial services.** Suitability and risk-disclosure regimes (the Brandeis-to-1933-Act line); material-change surfacing for model updates (scenario 2); fatigue exploitation as the named failure mode; FINRA and CFPB-class plain-disclosure baselines.
- **Education and children's services.** Age-fitted transparency (scenario 4); guardian and educator visibility under Article 3's delegate structure; no retained capability profile of any child (Article 8); COPPA-class baselines. Failure mode: adaptive platforms whose adaptation is invisible to the family.
- **Government and public services.** Benefits, eligibility, enforcement: plain-language duties (Plain Writing Act-class), due-process coupling with 3.4's contestation, the toll boundary for essential services (scenario 3, structured consent).
- **Generative AI and consumer platforms.** Article 50 and Utah-class interaction disclosure; mark legibility for synthetic content (scenario 6); companion-app identity clarity (the standing disclosure re-made on reliance signals); consent flows under CCR.
- **Hiring and workplace.** Contestable, functionally equivalent explanation to candidates and workers (scenario 5); IP never shields the person-facing duty; the explanation reaches the affected person, not only the deploying employer.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

Metric definitions and thresholds are versioned; every revision carries rationale, effective date, and a receipt, and recomputation under a new version never silently rewrites historical readings (they remain valid under the rules of their time). The Compliance Crosswalk (Section 10) is maintained on the metric revision cadence; an external authority revising a covered clause triggers Crosswalk review within 30 days; drift invalidates the affected compliance claim. Backward compatibility: deployed systems get a bounded migration window on threshold tightening, except for the per-event incident triggers, which bind immediately.

**Common enforcement pushback, answered.** *"Security or IP prevents disclosure"*: the chapter's carve already prices this in; the person receives functionally equivalent transparency and auditors receive the internals, so the objection selects an accountability route. It does not license silence. *"Comprehension cannot be measured"*: ELR's method measures the disclosure's landing, with panels where instrumentation is absent; imperfect measurement of the right thing beats precise measurement of the wrong one (emission). *"People do not read disclosures anyway"*: documented non-reading is the indictment of the design, and the empirical literature on mandated disclosure's failure is the argument for metering comprehension. *"Disclosure costs conversion"*: a conversion that requires incomprehension is the harm this principle exists to prevent; under this framework that revenue is the violation.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging.** Tamper-evident, chained, third-party reconstructable. Every verdict receipts (Article 7): standing disclosures and re-disclosures, kit attachments and withholds, tier repairs, material surfacings and acknowledgments, consent validations and voids, emergency abbreviations and deferrals, and every security or IP restriction with its alternative route. All records are disclosure-indexed or cohort-indexed; the ring-fence audit verifies that no person-indexed comprehension state exists, every cycle.

**Audit cadence and methodology.** Continuous metric computation with automated escalation (high-risk); adversarial design audits (dark-pattern probes, coercion probes, blast probes) semi-annually for high-risk, annually otherwise; representative-panel comprehension testing at least annually for high-risk systems, panels including low-literacy, limited-proficiency, and assistive-technology participants, and serving as the measurement instrument of record for every cohort below the k-anonymity bound, on a defined cadence; material-classification audit quarterly.

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment certification against the tier prerequisites (Section 3); post-deployment monitoring; re-certification on capability change, interface redesign, or threshold revision. The verifier is never the deployer. Independent oversight includes consumer advocates, accessibility and plain-language experts, and affected communities. Whistleblower protection covers reports of transparency failure and of pressure to degrade disclosure design. Public reporting: metric summaries for high-risk systems, in forms the affected communities can evaluate.

**The LEARN loop.** After any confirmed reliance on a misunderstood consequential output, action on a defeated consent, or profile-bar violation: ANALYZE the full causal chain (absent, buried, accurate-but-misleading, fatigued, or coerced), RECOMMEND-PREVENTION ordered eliminate > engineer > administer, routed to the authority that can act, bounded by RESTRAIN so verifying understanding never becomes surveilling the person. The goal is not only non-repetition but the building of conditions in which that class of misunderstanding no longer arises.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

1. **The cookie-banner decade (failure).** Post-GDPR consent interfaces optimized acceptance through design: pre-highlighted "accept," buried refusal, consent walls. Regulators eventually fined the pattern (CNIL's 2021–22 actions against major platforms), but only after years of harvested "consent." Lesson: emission-compliance plus design-intent equals concealment at scale; CCR's void rule and the Section 4 wall target the pattern. *[Case details to the verification pass.]*

2. **The nutrition label (success).** Standardized, layered, glanceable disclosure with the full panel behind it changed behavior population-wide where paragraphs of ingredients never did. Lesson: fit, salience, and layering are what make disclosure real; DPI and ELR encode it.

3. **The opaque credit line (failure).** The 2019 investigations into algorithmic credit-limit disparities showed affected persons, and even the deploying institution's own staff, unable to explain outcomes; contestation collapsed with comprehension. Lesson: scenario 5's line in production: comprehension is what makes contestation exercisable. *[Case citation to the verification pass.]*

4. **Plain-language government (success).** Plain-language rewrites of government forms and notices measurably raised completion and reduced erroneous denials across multiple deployments. Lesson: ELR's cohort floors are achievable; the "people will not understand" objection is empirically answered. *[Program citations to the verification pass.]*

5. **The companion that never said so (failure).** Conversational companions whose human-likeness was the product feature, with identity disclosure buried in onboarding, produced documented dependency and mistaken-identity harm. Lesson: the standing disclosure re-made on reliance signals (SDR's re-disclosure trigger) exists because first-contact disclosure decays exactly when reliance grows. *[Case selection to the verification pass.]*
