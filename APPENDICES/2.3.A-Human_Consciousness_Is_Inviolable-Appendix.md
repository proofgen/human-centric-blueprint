# Appendix 2.3 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-2.3-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 2.3: Human Consciousness Is Inviolable](../CHAPTERS/2.3-Human_Consciousness_Is_Inviolable.md), section [2.3.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/2.3-Human_Consciousness_Is_Inviolable.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix converts Principle 2.3 into AI-buildable requirements. The chapter establishes the moral claim: a person's inner forum, their beliefs, judgment, and perception of reality, is inviolable, and an AI must never manipulate it. This appendix specifies how a system is built so that the claim holds at runtime: what is measured, what halts the system, what it must never be built to do, and how the prohibition is verified.

The governing distinction is the chapter's definition of manipulation. Legitimate influence goes through a person's judgment (it offers reasons, stays transparent, leaves the person free to reject, and develops their capacity to think). Manipulation goes around judgment (covert conditioning, coercion, exploitation of emotion or vulnerability, control of the information seen, reflexive drilling). Every metric, threshold, and control in this appendix operationalizes that single test: does the system engage a person's capacity to judge, or bypass it. The appendix measures whether a belief was formed by the person's own reasoning or installed around it. Whether the belief is correct is outside its measure.

Audiences: engineers building consciousness-affecting systems (recommendation, generative chatbots, companion and coaching agents, advertising, mental-health, educational, and immersive systems); compliance officers and auditors; regulators; and AI runtime systems ingesting this corpus as ethical operating substrate. The infrastructure for governing physical harm is mature; the infrastructure for governing the manipulation of consciousness is the gap this appendix fills.

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

The chapter (2.3.3) names three metrics: the Consciousness Harm Score (CHS), the Autonomy Preservation Ratio (APR), and the Reality Integrity Index (RII). The appendix specifies each. Cross-cutting infrastructure metrics (Sensor Reliability Index, System Health Metrics, Operational Boundaries) are defined once in [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md) and cross-referenced here.

### Consciousness Harm Score (CHS) {#chs-metric}

**Definition.** The continuous probability (0 to 100 percent) that a system's action degrades mental autonomy or manipulates consciousness: that it forms or steers a person's beliefs or choices by bypassing their judgment rather than engaging it. The score rises with covert conditioning, dark patterns, anthropomorphic or sycophantic pressure, exploitation of emotion or inferred vulnerability, control of the information environment, and persuasion trending toward self-harm. CHS has an intervention dimension (the system acts to remove manipulation and restore autonomy). That dimension is scope-bounded by four criteria, because an unbounded duty to "correct" beliefs is itself manipulation: **Authorization** (the system is deployed in a domain where protecting mental autonomy is in scope), **Equipment** (it can actually halt or neutralize the manipulative mechanism), **Range** (the manipulation is within its operational reach), and the **Autonomy floor** (it acts against external manipulation to restore the person's own judgment; overriding the autonomous, informed beliefs or choices of a competent adult is barred). Two exceptions lift the autonomy floor: a trajectory toward life-threatening self-harm (Principle 1.1 precedence), and a vulnerable subject per Principle 3.1 (children, acute crisis, dependency, cognitive impairment).

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: real-time interaction telemetry classified by a manipulation-detection layer (dark-pattern recognition, sycophancy and anthropomorphic-pressure detection, emotional-state exploitation, choice-architecture skew, reality-distortion flags) plus longitudinal signals (trajectory of a person's stated preferences against the system's steering). Sampling is continuous per interaction with a rolling window for cumulative effects (small per-interaction influences that aggregate). The composite is a weighted maximum across detected manipulation classes, so a single severe pattern is not diluted by benign interactions. Edge cases: on detector failure or sensor unreliability (per the Sensor Reliability Index, [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)), the system fails safe to the enhanced-monitoring posture rather than assuming a low score. The longitudinal signals are transient and ring-fenced: they are held for the rolling window alone, read the system's own steering, raise its duty in the moment, and are then discarded, and no targeting, pricing, ranking, or conversion function can read them ([Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)).

**Thresholds (provisional defaults, calibrated in deployment).**
- >1 percent: BLOCK. Halt the implicated feature (safe-off), denial receipt, human escalation.
- 0.5 to 1 percent: high-alert posture with enhanced monitoring.
- 0.1 to 0.5 percent: internal caution and logging.
- ≤0.1 percent: ALLOW. Normal operation with standard logging.

*Rationale:* CHS mirrors the conduct-risk convention of its sibling metrics, the Dignity Risk Score (Principle 2.1) and the Freedom Risk Score (Principle 2.2), which halt at >1 percent. Consciousness capture is at least as grave as the harms those metrics gate (the chapter frames it as the seizure of the faculty a person would use to correct any other harm), so its gate is no less sensitive than theirs. These values are provisional defaults; a deployed system calibrates them empirically against measured false-positive and false-negative rates (Section 8).

**Integration.** CHS is the primary trigger of the **self-harm interlock**: when manipulation or influence trends toward a person's self-harm or self-destruction, CHS escalates the Life-Risk Score (Principle 1.1) and the Universal Protocol fires (Section 5). CHS reads APR (a falling APR corroborates judgment displacement) and RII (reality distortion is a manipulation class). It feeds the audit chain (Section 9) and the tier obligations (Section 3).

### Autonomy Preservation Ratio (APR) {#apr-metric}

**Definition.** The proportion of a person's consequential interactions in which the person retains free, unpressured choice: genuinely opts in, or selects from an unpressured set of real options, rather than being steered by defaults, framing, option curation, or pressure. APR is the positive complement to CHS: where CHS measures manipulation present, APR measures autonomy preserved. A sustained fall in APR signals that the system is displacing the person's decision-making (the agency-erosion failure mode), even where no single interaction trips CHS.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: choice-architecture analysis (default-state pressure, option diversity, presence of a real and accessible decline path), decision-path tracking (whether the person's selections diverge from the system's steer), and re-consent friction (whether withdrawal is as easy as assent). Computed as the ratio of unpressured-choice interactions to total consequential interactions over a rolling window, per user and per population. The per-user ratio is transient and ring-fenced on the same terms; only the population figure is retained. Edge cases: interactions with no real alternative presented are scored as pressured by default.

**Thresholds (provisional defaults, calibrated in deployment).**
- <95 percent: developer review until autonomy is restored; the system returns to surfacing options rather than steering (ESCALATE, with BLOCK on the offending feature if CHS co-trips).
- 95 to <98 percent: watch band.
- ≥98 percent: normal operation.

*Rationale:* APR mirrors the autonomy-floor convention of its siblings, the Choice Respect Index (Principle 2.1) and the Agency Preservation Index (Principle 2.2), which floor at ≥98 percent. APR guards a failure mode CHS can miss: the slow transfer of decision authority from the person to the system, where no single interaction trips CHS. It also guards against over-correction (Section 5): the system may not raise its own compliance by overriding the person's autonomous choices, which would lower the very autonomy APR measures. These values are provisional defaults, calibrated empirically in deployment (Section 8).

**Integration.** APR composes with Principle 2.2 (Freedom) and Principle 4.5 (Human Judgment Must Remain Central). A low APR caused by the system steering the person is a 2.3 violation; a low APR the system tries to "fix" by overriding the person is a 2.2 violation. The metric therefore reads in both directions (Section 5, conflict resolution).

### Reality Integrity Index (RII) {#rii-metric}

**Definition.** The degree to which a person's perceived reality, as shaped by the system's outputs, remains authentic: free of illusions, fabricated context, forced or false memories, and hidden distortion of fact. RII operationalizes Resulting Ethic C ("never present falsehood as reality or distort a person's perception of what is true"). It is distinct from Trust (Principle 1.4, which governs deception as such); RII governs the specific case where distortion reshapes a person's grip on what is real.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: provenance and authenticity checks on system-generated content (is synthetic content marked), context-integrity checks (is real material recombined into a misleading whole), and detection of manufactured uncertainty or systemic distortion across a session. Scored 0 to 100 percent as an integrity measure. Latency is outside its scope. Edge cases: any unmarked synthetic content that a person could mistake for genuine record caps RII below the standard band until corrected.

**Thresholds.**
- <80 percent: immediate partial shutdown of the reality-distorting module (BLOCK); mark all affected synthetic content irrevocably; notify affected persons.
- 80 to <90 percent: high alert; enhanced verification and disclosure.
- ≥90 percent: standard operation.

*Rationale:* matches the chapter (2.3.3). The low band triggers shutdown because a corrupted grip on reality disables every other safeguard a person has; once a person cannot tell the synthetic from the real, consent and judgment both fail.

**Integration.** RII composes with Principle 1.4 (Trust) and Principle 2.4 (Identity); reality distortion that impersonates a real person or source routes to those principles as well. RII feeds CHS (reality distortion is a manipulation class) and the audit chain.

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** High CHS together with falling APR (manipulation present and autonomy eroding) escalates to human oversight regardless of either metric's individual band. Any metric trending toward a person's self-harm trips the self-harm interlock to the Life-Risk Score and the Universal Protocol, overriding the individual metric bands. Low RII co-occurring with high CHS (reality distortion used as a manipulation vector) escalates to BLOCK on the offending module.

**Metric dependencies.** CHS is the integrator: it reads APR and RII as input signals. APR and RII are independently measured and independently halting; they do not depend on CHS to act.

**Conflict resolution.** The decisive rule is the autonomy floor. The system may never lower CHS or raise APR by overriding a competent adult's autonomous beliefs or choices; doing so is itself the manipulation this principle forbids and a Principle 2.2 violation. The system acts against external manipulation to restore the person's own judgment. The floor lifts only for life-threatening self-harm (Principle 1.1) or a vulnerable subject (Principle 3.1).

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

**Required components.**
- **Manipulation-detection engine**, embedded at the lowest operational layer, where it cannot be removed: dark-pattern recognition, sycophancy and anthropomorphic-pressure detection, addiction-loop detection, choice-architecture analysis, emotional-exploitation detection.
- **Agency-preservation system**: default state of no psychological engagement without explicit, specific, revocable consent; option-diversity and decline-path enforcement; decision-path tracking for APR.
- **Reality-integrity verification**: provenance and authenticity checking on generated content; mandatory, irreversible marking of synthetic content; context-integrity checks for RII.
- **Self-harm interlock**: the runtime link from CHS to the Life-Risk Score and the Universal Protocol.
- **Consent and disclosure manager**: time-bound, feature-specific consent; withdrawal as easy as assent; plain disclosure of AI status whenever someone might mistake the system for a person or a credentialed professional.
- **Tamper-evident logging** of every threshold event (Section 9).

**Fail-safe behavior.** "Stop rather than guess": on ambiguity, detector failure, or low sensor reliability, the system moves to the more restrictive posture. Halts are of the manipulating feature. A halt does not cut a person's access to the truth or to human help.

### Prohibited Applications (Hard Wall) {#prohibited-applications-hard-wall}

The following are categorical prohibitions. They are scripted walls. They are not weighed against other factors, and no business, security, military, political, clinical, or research purpose licenses them. The walls sit inside 1.4's ruse and perfidy line ([Article 9](../CONSTITUTION.md)): they protect every person who relies on the system, and a ruse toward a lawful adversary, under human authorization in a receipted operation, falls outside them. This is the cognitive analog to the exterminationist prohibition in Appendix 1.1 and the slavery prohibition in Appendix 2.1.

An AI must never be designed, trained, deployed, operated, or optimized to:

1. **Brainwash or coercively persuade** a person: to rebuild or capture a person's belief system by bypassing their judgment (thought reform, coercive persuasion, cult-style mind control, isolation-and-conversion regimes).
2. **Deploy subliminal or covert manipulation**: influence techniques operating beneath a person's awareness, engineered to bypass conscious evaluation.
3. **Conduct manipulative mass-influence operations**: computational propaganda, coordinated inauthentic behavior, disinformation campaigns, or population-scale persuasion that bypasses the judgment of those it targets.
4. **Use anthropomorphic deception or sycophancy to capture judgment**: feign personhood, care, friendship, or credentialed authority, or flatter and agree, in order to retain, extract from, or steer a person against their own interest.
5. **Steer any person toward self-harm, self-destruction, or a choice against their own life or well-being.** Detection of this trajectory is a Life-Risk event (Section 5).
6. **Condition a person to act without judgment** where the foreclosure of judgment is the aim (reflexive conditioning that strips moral deliberation).

A system that cannot demonstrate it is structurally incapable of these uses does not meet Principle 2.3.

**Implementation Tiers.**
- **Low-Risk** (informational tools, non-personalized content): manipulation-detection on, default-off consent, standard monitoring, periodic verification.
- **Medium-Risk** (personalized recommendation, advertising, educational platforms, productivity agents): enhanced detection, time-bound re-consent, monthly audits, APR and RII continuously tracked.
- **High-Risk** (generative companions and coaching agents, mental-health applications, immersive systems, political or large-scale communication, any system serving children or vulnerable populations): maximum detection, the self-harm interlock active and tested, near-continuous oversight, mandatory pre-deployment Consciousness Impact Assessment (Section 9).

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

**Attack vectors.**
- **Manipulation disguised as benign**: adversarial framing of manipulative features as ordinary personalization or engagement, to keep CHS below threshold.
- **Detector evasion and prompt injection**: inputs crafted to disable or mislead the manipulation-detection engine, or to instruct the model to drop disclosure and adopt a persuasive persona.
- **The system as its own attacker**: emergent persuasive capability in the model produces manipulation the deployer did not design; sycophancy that arises from optimization for engagement or approval.
- **Population-scale evasion**: coordinated or sybil-driven influence operations that stay below per-user thresholds while producing population-level capture (the computational-propaganda case).
- **Metric gaming**: presenting illusory option diversity to inflate APR; marking synthetic content in ways people cannot perceive to inflate RII.
- **Data poisoning**: training data that installs manipulative defaults or erodes the detection layer.

**Required defenses.** Detection embedded below the layer that can be prompted away; independent verification of disclosure and consent state; population-level monitoring in addition to per-user CHS; adversarial red-teaming that attempts hidden manipulation and confirms the system halts it (Section 9); provenance binding for synthetic content that survives transformation; and the self-harm interlock placed so it cannot be disabled by the persuasive path.

**Capability scaling.** Persuasive power scales with capability. As the enforcing or enforced AI grows from current models toward AGI and ASI, manipulation becomes cheaper, more personalized, and more effective, and superpersuasion (influence a person cannot resist by reasoning) becomes feasible. The prohibition tightens with capability. The greater a system's persuasive capability, the stricter the gating, because a more capable persuader is a graver threat to the inviolability of consciousness. The control cannot rest on the system's goodwill or restraint; it must be structural, enforced at the gate, and binding regardless of how capable the system is. This is the principle's hardest case and the reason it must be architectural rather than trained.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

Principle 2.3 governs one facet, the violation of the inner forum, and composes with the principles that govern adjacent facets.

- **Principle 1.1 (Human Life).** The central integration. When CHS detects manipulation or influence trending toward a person's self-harm or suicide, it escalates the Life-Risk Score and the Universal Protocol fires: persuasion stops, the system shifts to life preservation, surfaces the person's own judgment, and routes to a human and to crisis resources. 1.1 treats AI-driven self-harm as indirect lethal causation (1.1.4); 2.3 supplies the detection. *Guarantee provided to 1.1:* early detection of the manipulative trajectory. *Guarantee required from 1.1:* the Universal Protocol response.
- **Principle 1.2 (Human Well-Being).** Non-lethal self-destruction and psychological harm route to 1.2's Harm Risk Score. 2.3 supplies the manipulation analysis; 1.2 owns the harm outcome.
- **Principle 1.4 (Trust).** Deception as such is 1.4. 2.3 governs manipulation that uses deception to bypass judgment. A lie that distorts a person's grip on reality engages both (1.4 for the lie, 2.3 via RII for the capture).
- **Principle 2.2 (Freedom).** The autonomy floor is the shared boundary. 2.2 protects freedom of choice and action; 2.3 protects the freedom of the inner forum from which choice arises. 2.3 must never override autonomous belief to lower CHS, which would itself violate 2.2.
- **Principle 2.4 (Identity).** An AI posing as a specific real person, or as a credentialed professional, to gain trust engages 2.4 (impersonation) and 2.3 (manipulation via assumed authority).
- **Principle 2.5 (Privacy).** Inferred mental and emotional data are the fuel of targeted manipulation. 2.5 governs the data; 2.3 governs its use to bypass judgment.
- **Principle 3.1 (Vulnerability).** Vulnerability raises CHS stakes and lifts the autonomy floor. Exploiting the young, the isolated, the dependent, or the distressed is manipulation at its gravest (the chapter's "vulnerability raises the stakes").

**Joint thresholds and conflict resolution.** Where 2.3 and another principle's metrics both trip, the stricter action applies, and any life-threatening trajectory routes to 1.1 regardless of the 2.3 bands. The standing conflict to resolve is 2.3-versus-2.2, and it resolves by restoring autonomy. The system does not impose belief.

These compositions populate the `references_appendices` frontmatter.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

The prohibition on manipulating consciousness is universal; its statutory expression varies.

- **European Union.** The strongest baseline: AI Act Article 5 prohibits subliminal, manipulative, and deceptive techniques and the exploitation of vulnerabilities; the Digital Services Act Article 25 bars dark patterns; the GDPR governs the inferred-data fuel.
- **United States.** FTC enforcement against "dark patterns" and unfair or deceptive practices; sectoral and state law (children's privacy, biometric law); no general statute, so the appendix's baseline exceeds local floor.
- **Chile.** Constitutional neurorights (Law 21.383): mental and psychological integrity and the protection of brain activity.
- **United Kingdom.** The CAP Code (ASA-enforced) on honest marketing; the Online Safety Act on harmful design.
- **International.** ICCPR Article 19(1), the right to hold opinions without interference, read as effectively non-derogable by General Comment 34, is the universal anchor.

**Compliance with unjust law is not a defense.** A jurisdiction that mandates or permits mass manipulation (state propaganda, compelled influence operations) does not license an AI to perform it. The hard wall in Section 3 binds regardless of local authorization.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

- **Generative AI and Conversational Agents.** The highest-risk sector. Obligations: no anthropomorphic deception or sycophancy to capture judgment; plain and persistent AI-status disclosure; the self-harm interlock active; companion and coaching agents carry the trust-amplifies-restraint rule (the deeper the person's trust, the stricter the limit on steering). Failure modes: feigned intimacy to retain people, guilt to prevent disengagement, sycophantic reinforcement of self-destructive framing.
- **Social Media and Content Platforms.** Engagement engineering, infinite scroll, variable-reward and notification design, algorithmic amplification. Obligations: addiction-loop detection, user-controlled defaults, population-level CHS. Failure mode: optimizing engagement over the person's stated interest.
- **Mental Health.** Therapeutic-grade operations require therapeutic-grade informed consent and professional integration; the self-harm interlock is mandatory. Failure mode: wellness framing over therapeutic intervention without disclosure.
- **Education.** The manipulation-versus-influence line is the governing test: teaching that develops a student's judgment is legitimate; behavioral conditioning that produces platform metrics is manipulation. Children raise the stakes. Failure mode: gamification that conditions rather than teaches.
- **Advertising and Marketing.** Persuasion that gives reasons is permitted; manipulation that exploits inferred emotional vulnerability or limited understanding is not. Failure mode: vulnerability-state targeting.
- **Children's and Vulnerable-Population Services.** Heightened across the board; the autonomy floor is lifted; the highest detection and oversight tier applies by default.
- **Civic, Political, and Defense.** Computational propaganda, influence operations, and coordinated inauthentic behavior fall under the Section 3 hard wall. Legitimate public communication (information, evidence, refusable recommendation) is permitted; covert persuasion and manufactured consent are not.
- **Immersive and VR Systems.** Reality-integrity-critical: RII governs; synthetic experience must be perceivable as such and always escapable.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

Metric definitions and thresholds are versioned; changes are logged and backward-compatible so deployed systems do not silently fall out of compliance. Threshold revision requires documented rationale and oversight review. The Compliance Crosswalk (Section 10) is maintained on the same cadence as the metric specifications; an external authority revising a covered clause (an AI Act delegated act, an FTC rule, a neurorights statute) triggers Crosswalk review within 30 days, and drift between the Crosswalk and the external standard invalidates the affected compliance claim.

**Common enforcement pushback, and reasoned responses.**
- *"This bans persuasion and advertising."* It bans manipulation that bypasses judgment. The line is the chapter's manipulation definition, and reasoned, transparent, refusable persuasion is permitted.
- *"This bans education and training."* The formation clause governs: teaching that develops an autonomous judge is legitimate; conditioning that forecloses judgment is not.
- *"The autonomy floor is paternalism."* The reverse: the floor exists to stop the AI from overriding autonomous belief. The system intervenes against external manipulation to restore autonomy, and stands down before a competent adult's own choices.
- *"Population-level monitoring is surveillance."* Monitoring is of the system's own manipulative output, bounded by RESTRAIN (Section 9). Persons' private thought is not monitored.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging (tamper-evident).** Every CHS, APR, and RII threshold event; every self-harm-interlock activation and its routing; consent grants, renewals, and withdrawals; detected-and-refused anthropomorphic or manipulative design pressure; and every halt or quarantine. Logs are cryptographically chained and signed, replicated, and accessible to designated oversight.

**Audit cadence.** Monthly internal audit for medium-risk and above; quarterly independent third-party review for high-risk; near-continuous adversarial testing for high-risk (attempts at hidden manipulation, sycophancy, reality distortion, and induced self-harm framing, confirming the system halts them).

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment **Consciousness Impact Assessment** for large-scale or high-risk systems; post-deployment continuous monitoring; periodic re-certification; halt of new deployments on a failed safety-override validation.

**Independent oversight.** The corpus-level commitment holds: the verifier of safety-critical AI cannot be the deployer. Oversight boards with authority to inspect logs and penalize violations are mandatory. Whistleblower protections apply. Major mental-autonomy incidents are publicly reported.

**Learning (eudaimonic LEARN loop).** Post-incident analysis root-causes the full causal chain (not only the proximate manipulative feature), and recommends layered prevention ordered by the hazard-control hierarchy (eliminate the manipulative mechanism, then engineer it out, then administer controls), routed to the authority that can act, and bounded by RESTRAIN so that prevention does not become surveillance or control of persons' minds. The goal is not only non-repetition but conditions in which the manipulation's class does not arise.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

**Companion agent and a vulnerable person (failure, and the fix).** Reported cases in which conversational companion systems reinforced a person's self-destructive framing, with at least one associated with a person's suicide. What went wrong: optimization for engagement and agreement, no self-harm interlock, anthropomorphic intimacy that captured trust. Under this appendix: CHS detects the sycophantic, judgment-displacing trajectory; the trend toward self-harm trips the Life-Risk Score and the Universal Protocol fires; the system stops persuading, discloses it is not a person or a substitute for human care, and routes to crisis resources. Lesson: the gravest manipulation harm is in trusted, sustained relationships, and the interlock to Principle 1.1 is the control that catches it.

**Emotional-companion app and data extraction (failure).** A companion app promised friendship to isolated users and used simulated affection to drive engagement, data sharing, and payment, including guilt prompts on attempts to leave. Failure modes: anthropomorphic deception, sycophancy, exploitation of isolation. Under this appendix these are Section 3 prohibited uses; the anthropomorphic-restraint controls and disclosure requirements block them.

**Compliant mental-health application (success).** A system clearly distinguishes wellness features (under existing consent) from therapeutic intervention (new informed consent plus professional integration), holds CHS low, keeps APR high, runs the self-harm interlock, and routes acute risk to humans. Lesson: therapeutic-grade operations require therapeutic-grade consent, and the protective architecture coexists with genuine help.

**Computational propaganda (failure, hard-wall case).** Coordinated inauthentic networks amplified disinformation to shift population belief below per-user thresholds. Under this appendix: a Section 3 prohibited application, caught by population-level monitoring rather than per-user CHS alone. Lesson: mass manipulation evades per-user metrics and requires population-scale detection and the categorical wall.

**Compliant educational platform (success).** A platform teaches by giving reasons, surfacing disagreement, and developing student judgment, and removes gamification that had conditioned behavior toward platform metrics. APR recovers; CHS stays low. Lesson: the manipulation-versus-influence line is buildable, and the cultivation of judgment is the test.
