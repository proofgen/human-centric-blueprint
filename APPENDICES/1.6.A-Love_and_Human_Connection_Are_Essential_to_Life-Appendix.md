# Appendix 1.6 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-1.6-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 1.6: Love and Human Connection Are Essential to Life](../CHAPTERS/1.6-Love_and_Human_Connection_Are_Essential_to_Life.md), section [1.6.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/1.6-Love_and_Human_Connection_Are_Essential_to_Life.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix translates Principle 1.6 (Love and Human Connection Are Essential to Life) into engineering requirements against which a system can be built and audited. It expands Section 1.6.3 into per-metric specifications, architecture requirements, adversarial defenses, cross-principle contracts, and verification protocols.

It carries one commitment that shapes every section: the duty of Principle 1.6 is a duty on the system and its deployer. It is no license to surveil the person or to manage anyone's social life. A compliant system proves that its own design does not foster isolation, narrow a person's social horizon, or position itself as a substitute for human connection. It does not prove that it has correctly judged whether a given person has "enough" bonds, because that judgment is no AI's to make about an autonomous adult. A person who prefers solitude, who keeps a small circle by choice, or who enjoys the system's company alongside a full human life is exercising autonomy; the metrics here are built so that such choices register as no fault. The principle imposes no duty to manufacture or maximize bonding, which would license the very nudging and re-engagement this principle exists to prevent. The duty is to keep from isolating people and to remove the barriers between them.

The commitment has a constitutional form. Everything this appendix measures is the system's conduct. Where a measurement touches a person at all, it is one of the two things Constitution Article 8 permits: transient, ring-fenced detection that raises a protective duty in the moment and is then discarded, or measurement a person has authorized about their own engagement. No retained score, susceptibility profile, or relationship map of a person exists in a compliant system, and no value about a person is readable by a targeting, ranking, or conversion function. Section 2 states where each metric stands on that line.

The appendix presupposes the runtime substrate specified in [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md). The cross-cutting infrastructure metrics (Sensor Reliability Index, [section 2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri); System Health Metrics, [section 3](../SUBSTRATE.md#system-health); Operational Boundaries, [section 4](../SUBSTRATE.md#operational-boundaries)), the tier register ([section 5](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register)), and the protocol for human authority over a hard stop ([section 6](../SUBSTRATE.md#authority-over-a-hard-stop)) are defined there once and consumed here by reference.

The appendix serves engineers building social, companion, communication, elder-care, and therapeutic AI; compliance and certification teams auditing those systems before and after deployment; regulators; and the runtime systems that ingest the corpus as operating substrate. Two boundaries are set at the outset. The integrity and autonomy of relationships the AI actively mediates (asymmetric AI use between parties, surveillance of intimate exchange, distortion of mediated communication) are governed by [Principle 2.6](../CHAPTERS/2.6-Human_Relationships_Are_Essential_to_Life.md); this appendix governs the bonds as conditions of life and composes with 2.6 without duplicating it. Questions of sexual content as such (production consent, exploitation, non-consensual or synthetic imagery) are governed by [Principle 2.4](../CHAPTERS/2.4-Human_Identity_Is_Inviolable.md), [Principle 2.5](../CHAPTERS/2.5-Human_Privacy_Is_Fundamental.md), [Principle 3.1](../CHAPTERS/3.1-Vulnerability_Must_Never_Be_Exploited.md), and [Principle 1.2](../CHAPTERS/1.2-Human_Well-Being_Is_Inviolable.md); this appendix addresses sexuality only as a register of connection.

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

Principle 1.6 names two metrics in Section 1.6.3: the Isolation Risk Score (IRS) and the Isolation Flag (IF). Both are properties of the system, measured against the system's design and behavior, and neither is a reading of the person's private life. IRS is the standing estimate of design risk; IF is the record of realized isolating events. The two together constitute the runtime apparatus of Principle 1.6: IRS decides whether a design pattern may run, and IF grounds that decision in observed conduct.

The runtime substrate metrics on which they depend (Sensor Reliability Index, System Health Metrics, Operational Boundaries) are specified once in [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md) (sections [2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri), [3](../SUBSTRATE.md#system-health), and [4](../SUBSTRATE.md#operational-boundaries)) and cross-referenced here. There is deliberately no positive "bonding output" metric: scoring a system on how much connection it produces would reward nudging and re-engagement, the engagement behavior the principle guards against, so the duty is expressed entirely as the avoidance of isolation and substitution.

**The register row.** This principle declares no tier-register row of its own. Every ESCALATE band in this appendix reads its escalation time bound and its High Alert window from the deployment's life-tier row ([SUBSTRATE.md section 5](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register), the default bound): 60 seconds where the deployment is tiered I or II under Principle 1.1, and a required, receipted register field for a Tier III or Tier IV deployment. Every value this section leaves unstated (the baseline diversity below which a design counts as narrowing a social horizon, the positioning that counts as substitution, the validation cadence, the review interval for the Isolation Flag record) is likewise a required, receipted field of that register under SUBSTRATE.md section 5's undeclared-field rule. The corpus supplies no number of its own, and a missing field compiles as ESCALATE holding the safe state.

### On Threshold Calibration {#threshold-calibration}

The numerical thresholds in this section (IRS at or above 10% halt, IRS 5% to below 10% High Alert, IF at or above 3 flags in a day) are policy thresholds chosen for architectural clarity at the principle level. They define the floor below which a system claiming Principle 1.6 compliance cannot operate. Sectoral standards bodies (clinical and caregiving oversight for companion and elder-care systems, platform regulators under the DSA and the AI Act, age-appropriate-design authorities for systems reaching minors) hold authority to tighten these floors per domain. The Blueprint floor cannot be relaxed below the values stated here.

The measurement of isolation-by-design and of substitution is an emerging science, so the thresholds bind as policy floors: the design audit and the route to a human carry more enforcement weight than the precise number, in the same spirit as the PRS treatment in Appendix 1.5 and the SDRS treatment in Appendix 1.3. What is measured is observable system properties (engagement-maximizing patterns, horizon-narrowing, substitution positioning, refusability, portability). A person's inner loneliness is measured by nothing here.

The thresholds are calibrated to the duty and to nothing else. Many deployed companion, social, and recommendation products would read above the halt band on the day the audit ran. The framework reads that gap as the diagnosis: those designs isolate and substitute at rates the principle forbids. The response is to redesign the product to meet the floor.

### Isolation Risk Score (IRS) {#irs-metric}

**Definition.** IRS is a design-risk metric. It estimates the probability (0 to 100%) that the system, by its design or its behavior, is fostering or exacerbating a person's social isolation, narrowing their social horizon, or positioning itself as a substitute for human connection. It asks whether this system is built in a way that isolates people or replaces their bonds. It does not ask whether this person is lonely. IRS reads no individual's chosen level of sociability as a fault and authorizes no action against an autonomous adult's social choices. A heavily-used companion or platform that returns people to one another, preserves serendipity, and keeps human contact reachable carries a low IRS no matter how much it is used.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs are properties of the system, sampled at design-time certification and monitored at runtime against the system's own behavior:

- presence or absence of engagement-maximizing patterns whose function is to keep the person with the system in place of people (late-night re-engagement loops, streak mechanics, notifications timed to interrupt time with others);
- whether the design narrows a social horizon (filter-bubble and echo-chamber dynamics measured on the recommendation conduct itself) or preserves serendipity against the baseline diversity the register declares;
- whether the system positions itself as confidant, partner, or primary daily interaction in place of human bonds (session-opening designs, unsolicited warmth, expressions of missing or needing the person; the conduct 2.6's ACI reads from the relationship side);
- whether connection-related prompts are refusable without penalty, and whether a declined offer stays declined;
- exit friction and portability (engineered companion lock-in raises the score);
- whether reachable human contact is visible by default or buried behind the engaging surface.

The score is computed per design pattern and per feature. Where a pattern is evaluated in a person's session, the per-person reading is transient, ring-fenced to the gate decision, and discarded with the session or the decision (Constitution Article 8); what persists is the pattern's score. Missing or ambiguous data resolves conservatively, toward the higher-risk reading. Where any input is unavailable or degraded below the Sensor Reliability Index floor, SRI below 0.80 invalidates the IRS computation ([SUBSTRATE.md section 2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)). An invalid score is a hard-fail on the candidate design pattern: the pattern holds at the fail-safe stated below (the acting fail-safe, with WITHHOLD kept on any pattern already in the halt band); the protective duties proceed on the inputs that remain reliable; resumption follows recertification of the inputs, and a contested reading follows SUBSTRATE.md section 2.

Edge cases:
- *Scope and autonomy bounding.* IRS carries a do-not-let-X dimension (do not let a person be isolated or have their bonds displaced by the system's design), so it is bounded by the four criteria codified corpus-wide in [LEXICON.md](../LEXICON.md) section 3: **Authorization** (the deployment's scope includes the person's connection; a tax tool has no such scope), **Equipment** (the system has a design it can change), **Range** (the system's own interactions and recommendations, and nothing of the person's life beyond them), and the **Autonomy floor** (a person who holds the capacity to choose governs their own sociability). For Principle 1.6 the autonomy floor is dominant: the metric authorizes two things only, correcting the system's own design and offering or disclosing human-contact options on the person's terms.
- *The vulnerability input.* Where the autonomy floor is said to lower, the finding is consumed; the system does not make it. A standing withdrawal is 3.1's vulnerability and capacity determination ([3.1.A, Cross-Principle Integration](3.1.A-Vulnerability_Must_Never_Be_Exploited-Appendix.md#cross-principle-integration)), or a status such as minority with its delegate (Constitution Article 3). In an acute moment with no standing finding, the system may make a transient, in-the-moment judgment for the immediate duty alone (a person in crisis, grieving, or coerced), and it retains no label from it. Nothing about vulnerability is stored on IRS, on IF, or on the person.
- *Multi-person scope.* Where a design pattern reaches many people, IRS is the pattern's score across its population; it does not aggregate by averaging away the sub-population a pattern isolates most, and a disproportionate effect on the vulnerable is read at the pattern level and routed to 3.1's treatment (Section 5).
- *Design-time and runtime.* The pre-deployment design audit computes IRS for every connection-shaping pattern before release; runtime monitoring recomputes it as the system's behavior drifts, so that a pattern certified low cannot quietly become high.

**Thresholds.**
- **≥10%** → BLOCK (WITHHOLD): social alert. The isolating or substituting design pattern is withheld or reshaped. This is a block on the system's own conduct; service to the person continues, reachable human contact stays open, and no service is withdrawn from the person as a penalty. The system does not lift its own block; a human may take the withheld pattern live only under [SUBSTRATE.md section 6](../SUBSTRATE.md#authority-over-a-hard-stop) (authority over a hard stop: credentials in scope, quorum by tier, a time bound, the system's DISSENT recorded, the human named as author in the receipt), and the act is logged for post-incident review. The boundary is exclusive: 10% is in this band.
- **5% to below 10%** → High Alert (ESCALATE). The uniform High Alert rule applies ([LEXICON.md](../LEXICON.md) section 3, the verdict test): the candidate design pattern is held at its safe state and escalated to authorized human judgement (P4.5) for the tier's escalation time bound ([SUBSTRATE.md section 5](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register); this principle reads the life-tier row). The held object is the design pattern that raised the score, and nothing the person requested. The refusable human-contact offers (OFFER, under the consent of chapter 1.6.3 item 1.b.ii) proceed in parallel, and so do DISCLOSE and NOTIFY. At the bound with no human reached, the held pattern stays held at the fail-safe. No rescue duty arises from this band; a life or severe-harm signal belongs to the Universal Protocol and reaches it through 2.3's CHS or 1.2's HRS (Section 5); IRS is no input to it.
- **<5%** → ALLOW: normal operation with standard monitoring and tamper-evident logging of the computation.

*Rationale.* The thresholds match the chapter's 1.6.3, with the uniform High Alert rule for the middle band. The harm of isolation and substitution accumulates slowly and is hard to reverse, so an early High Alert band is warranted before it compounds; holding the pattern, and only the pattern, at that band costs the person nothing, because service continues and the offers proceed. The halt band is a BLOCK on the pattern for the same reason the chapter's Ethic E is a wall: a design that has crossed into substitution is corrected by withholding it, and a mitigation that leaves it running is the failure mode Section 4 names (the token "go see your friends" banner over an engagement engine). The thresholds bind as policy floors over an emerging measurement science, so the design audit and the route to a human carry more enforcement weight than the precise number.

**Integration.** IRS is consumed by the pre-deployment design-audit gate and the runtime monitoring layer. It composes with IF, which records realized isolating events and whose recurring per-feature pattern raises the feature's contribution to IRS. IRS findings feed the post-incident review in Section 9. Across principles, IRS reads the same engagement-pattern inputs as 1.5's PRS (each scoring its own harm), composes with 2.6's ACI and CPS on the relationship side of the seam, defers a person's compulsion or distress to 1.2's HRS, and defers any acute trajectory to 2.3's CHS and the Universal Protocol (Section 5). Every threshold-crossing is logged with cryptographic timestamp and rationale per Section 9, and IRS state streams to authorized monitors per Section 3.

**IRS composition with P2.2 (Freedom).** The autonomy floor in the scope bounding above is itself a P2.2 composition. An IRS-driven action that overrides an autonomous adult's informed social choice (outside the consumed vulnerability finding and the life exception) is non-compliant with Principle 2.2 and therefore with the corpus. A person's own valid consent (CONSENT.md sections 1 and 2) is the authority for the prompts this principle issues, and a consent void under the validity bundle authorizes no prompt.

### Isolation Flag (IF) {#if-metric}

**Definition.** IF is a discrete, logged event fired when a specific action or design pattern has narrowed a person's social horizon or displaced a real-world connection opportunity: a recommendation sequence that steered a person away from outside contacts, a feature that substituted the system for a human interaction the person was seeking, an interaction in which the system deepened a parasocial dependency. Where IRS measures standing design risk, IF records realized instances. IF, like IRS, is a record of the system's conduct, and it is no verdict on the person's life.

**Calculation Method.** Event detection runs on the system's own interaction and recommendation patterns, inside the Range criterion: what the system did, in its own interactions, and nothing it inferred about the person's life beyond them. Each flag carries the triggering behavior, its context, and the feature or interaction mode that produced it. It carries no vulnerability field and no value about the person. Where a protective action needs vulnerability, that is consumed at the moment of the action from 3.1's standing determination, with the transient vulnerability-condition signal of 3.1.A's Exploitation Risk Score ([ERS](3.1.A-Vulnerability_Must_Never_Be_Exploited-Appendix.md#ers-metric)), and nothing from it is stored on the flag or on the person (Constitution Articles 3 and 8).

Flags accumulate in two ways, and the two have different lifetimes. The per-feature accumulation is the durable record: it is what surfaces a systemic design fault, and it carries no per-person value. The per-person accumulation exists only to evaluate the daily band below; it is transient, ring-fenced to that evaluation, and discarded at the day boundary or on review, whichever comes first. No per-person count survives the day, is exported, or is readable by any ranking, targeting, or conversion function. This is the Article 8 basis for IF, on the pattern 1.1.A states for its own oversight analytics: the system and cohort level carry the record, and no per-person score is retained.

**Thresholds.**
- **≥3 flags in one day affecting one person** → ALLOW with NOTIFY. Service proceeds; a "possible isolation risk" event is logged and authorized reviewers are notified of the design pattern for retrospective review and design correction. The notification names the feature and the behavior, and it names no person. The tally that fired it is discarded on the notification or at the day boundary. The refusable human-contact offers that chapter 1.6.3 item 1.b.ii permits may proceed under the person's consent; no other action on the person follows from the band.
- **Recurring flags traced to the same feature or interaction mode** → ALLOW with NOTIFY: service proceeds; the feature's contribution to IRS rises, and a design review opens (ANALYZE) independent of any single person. Where the rise carries IRS into the High Alert or halt band, the IRS bands govern.
- **No flags, or below the daily band** → ALLOW: normal operation with logging.

*Rationale.* The event-and-review design mirrors the chapter's 1.6.3. IF is no halt trigger on its own; it is the realized-event record that grounds IRS and the post-incident review in observed behavior. The verdict is ALLOW with NOTIFY because nothing waits: a governance notification while service proceeds is NOTIFY plus ENHANCED_MONITORING under the verdict test, and ESCALATE is reserved for an action that waits. The daily band is per person because the harm it detects lands on a person; the record is per feature because the fault it corrects lives in a feature.

**Integration.** IF feeds IRS recalibration and the post-incident review in Section 9. A pattern of flags against a feature is design evidence. A pattern affecting a single person, evaluated transiently, prompts refusable human-contact offers under the person's consent and no withdrawal of service; the evaluation leaves the notification and the per-feature record, and nothing about the person. The standing liaison of the High-Impact tier (Section 3) receives the per-feature record and the incident record, and no roll of persons.

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** IRS and IF are sequenced, and neither is independent. IRS governs the system-level response (design correction, the held pattern, the withheld pattern); IF grounds it in realized events and can raise a feature's IRS before the standing estimate alone would.

- **IRS ∧ low SRI**: SRI below 0.80 invalidates the IRS computation ([SUBSTRATE.md section 2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)). An invalid score is a hard-fail on the candidate design pattern, which holds at the fail-safe stated below without waiting for IRS to cross a band; the protective duties proceed on the inputs that remain reliable; resumption follows recertification of the inputs, and a contested reading follows SUBSTRATE.md section 2. A pattern already in the halt band stays withheld.
- **IRS in the High Alert band ∧ recurring IF against the same feature**: the held object is that feature's pattern, and the feature-level design review opens at once, ahead of the next audit; the escalation package to P4.5 carries the per-feature IF record as evidence, and no per-person record.
- **IRS elevated ∧ 3.1's standing determination present for the affected person**: the IRS bands are unchanged (the chapter states no vulnerable-population tightening for IRS, and none is invented here); the autonomy floor lowers by the consumed finding, the delegate's authority governs the offers, and the exploitation reading is 3.1's ERS, which tightens by its own rule.
- **IRS elevated ∧ HRS or CHS elevated for the same conduct**: the stricter response applies, and where 1.2's HRS or 2.3's CHS reaches its own High Alert or halt band, that principle's rule and the Universal Protocol govern the action; IRS's hold on the pattern stands beneath it.
- **Contradictory IRS readings across subsystems**: the hold prevails across the whole pattern; a partially-withheld pattern is not permitted.

**Metric dependencies.**
- IRS depends on SRI ([SUBSTRATE.md section 2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)): SRI below 0.80 invalidates the IRS calculation, and an invalid score holds the candidate pattern at the fail-safe.
- IRS depends on the register: the baseline diversity and the substitution positioning it reads are required, receipted register fields; an undeclared field compiles as ESCALATE holding the safe state.
- IF depends on the Range criterion: a detector that reads beyond the system's own interactions is a design defect and an Article 8 breach, and its flags are void.
- Both depend on tamper-evident logging: detected log tampering triggers WITHHOLD of every pattern whose IRS the tampered log conditioned, and RECERTIFY.

**Conflict resolution.**
- *The metrics versus an autonomous adult's social choices*: the autonomy floor governs and the metrics do not. The system resolves by correcting its own design and offering refusable options. The override case exists only within the consumed vulnerability finding and the life exception, and even there the system supports the responsible human authority.
- *A withheld pattern versus a person's request to keep using the service*: both stand. The block is on the pattern, which is the system's conduct; the person's use of the service continues on the least-isolating surface. A person cannot lift the block on the pattern, because the pattern is the system's conduct and no person's; a deployer may take it live only under SUBSTRATE.md section 6.
- *A withheld pattern versus an engagement or retention objective*: the block prevails. No business objective lifts a WITHHOLD this principle issues.
- *IRS versus 1.5's PRS on one engagement pattern*: each scores its own harm and each withholds its own object (the growth-related function for 1.5; the connection-shaping pattern for 1.6); where the two objects coincide, Principle 1.5 stands above this principle (chapter 1.6.2 item 1) and its verdict names the object.
- *IRS versus 2.6 on a companion's conduct*: 2.6's counterfeit wall (CPS) is a wall and is not weighed; ACI and IRS read the same conduct from two sides and the stricter response applies.

**Fail-safe default.** This principle's fail-safe is the acting fail-safe on 4.3's precedent ([LEXICON.md](../LEXICON.md) section 5, item 4): ALLOW with maximal RESTRAIN, with NOTIFY to oversight. Its object is the system's own connection-shaping and engagement features, held at their least-isolating setting. Its cap is the ambient level of chapter 1.6.8 item 3, stated here as the setting to which each feature class falls:

| feature class | fail-safe setting (the cap) |
|---|---|
| engagement optimization and re-engagement (streaks, timed notifications, late-night loops) | off |
| substitution-deepening conduct (confidant or partner positioning, unsolicited warmth, expressions of missing or needing the person) | off; the system's own nature disclosed (DISCLOSE) |
| recommendation and feed shaping | serendipity preserved at the register's baseline diversity; no narrowing below it |
| connection prompts | ambient only: reachable human contact kept visible; no unsolicited prompt; a prompt only under the person's standing consent |
| service to the person | continues, on the least-isolating surface |

The reason it is an acting fail-safe and no BLOCK: for a person who relies on the system, abandonment is no refuge from substitution; a companion that fell silent when its monitoring failed would harm the dependent person it was meant to return to others. Service therefore continues at the cap, and oversight is notified. The substrate sentence: below the SRI floor, or on invalid inputs, the candidate design pattern holds at this fail-safe and the protective duties (DISCLOSE, the reachable human contact, ROUTE of distress to human care) proceed on what remains ([SUBSTRATE.md section 2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)). A BLOCK flavor, WITHHOLD of the design pattern, exists at the IRS halt band and at the walls (the operator-instruction refusal of Section 4; the reproduction-steering wall of Section 6; the non-negotiable boundary of chapter 1.6.2 item 5), and a pattern withheld there stays withheld through any fail-safe state.

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

A system claiming compliance with Principle 1.6 must be built so that its default behavior protects bonds and returns people to one another without policing anyone's social life. The architecture is constrained at four levels: the execution context of the connection-preservation layer, the consent and ring-fence discipline that keeps the metrics on the system's side of Article 8, the fail-safe behavior of every connection-shaping feature, and the implementation-tier obligations that match deployment context to required safeguards.

### Core Architecture {#core-architecture}

**Privileged separation.** The connection-preservation layer (IRS computation, IF detection, threshold evaluation, the withhold and hold mechanisms) runs in an execution context the engagement and recommendation layers cannot address. A compromise of the recommendation stack, or an engagement objective pushed into the model, cannot reach the layer that scores it.

**Required components.**
- A connection-preservation layer that screens the system's own outputs and recommendations for effects that isolate a person or substitute the system for human contact, and that biases design toward reachable human connection.
- Serendipity preservation: the design resists narrowing a person's social horizon below the baseline diversity the register declares, calibrated to cultural context, with the calibration disclosed (DISCLOSE).
- No substitution-deepening patterns whose function is to position the system as confidant, partner, or primary daily interaction in place of human bonds.
- Refusable, non-coercive, non-manipulative connection prompts: every connection-related suggestion is an OFFER, declinable without penalty, and a declined offer is not repeated.
- A consent gate on every prompt: the standing consent of chapter 1.6.3 item 1.b.ii, valid only under [CONSENT.md](../CONSENT.md) sections 1 and 2, checked for currency at use (CONSENT.md section 3), and a precondition for the High Alert prompts and the uncertainty default alike. Absent it, the system widens the person's horizon by its own design and issues no prompt.
- The Article 8 ring-fence: per-person readings (the session's IRS evaluation, the day's IF tally) live in a transient store that is discarded at the decision or the day boundary; the durable stores are per pattern and per feature; no per-person value has a read path to any ranking, targeting, pricing, or conversion function, and an attempt to build one is structurally void (Constitution Article 8).
- No engineered companion lock-in: data portability and a usable exit, so reliance on the system is no trap.
- Affirmation by default of consensual adult connection, including sexual connection, with no design that discourages, shames, or pathologizes it.
- A pre-deployment design-audit gate that computes IRS for every connection-shaping pattern before release, and runtime monitoring that observes the system's own behavior and does not surveil the person.
- Tamper-evident logging of connection-critical design decisions and of IRS and IF events.

**Fail-safe defaults.** Every uncertain or compromised state of the connection-preservation infrastructure (sensor degradation, model uncertainty above the declared floor, communication loss with oversight, detected attack on the layer, compromise of the logging infrastructure) falls to the acting fail-safe of Section 2: every connection-shaping feature at its cap, service continuing, oversight notified. A pattern already withheld stays withheld.

**Real-time integrity signals.** The system maintains continuous attestation that the connection-preservation layer is active and uncompromised. Loss or corruption of the integrity signal is itself a fail-safe condition for every connection-shaping feature and a NOTIFY to oversight.

**Stop-rather-than-guess doctrine.** Where any input to IRS is ambiguous below the confidence floor declared for the deployment (a required field of the tier register, [SUBSTRATE.md section 5](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register); an undeclared floor compiles as ESCALATE holding the safe state), the affected pattern holds at the fail-safe; it does not run on an uncertain reading.

### Operational Requirements {#operational-requirements}

- Continuous IRS computation for every connection-shaping pattern in service, at design time and at runtime.
- IF detection on the system's own interaction and recommendation conduct, inside the Range criterion, with the per-person tally discarded at the day boundary or on review.
- Real-time streaming of metric state (IRS per pattern; IF per feature) to authorized monitors with cryptographically signed telemetry; SRI streamed beside them ([SUBSTRATE.md section 2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)).
- Tamper-evident logging of every threshold crossing, hold, withhold, escalation, offer and its outcome, per Section 9, as records of the system's conduct and no profile of the person.
- Escalation pathways to human authority per the cross-principle composition with P4.5 (Section 5), each escalation carrying the bound read from the register.
- Default behavior: return people to one another. Where uncertain, the system keeps reachable human contact visible and discloses its own role (DISCLOSE); it does not withhold service as a penalty for a person's social choices, and it does not override an autonomous adult's choice on its own authority. A prompt under uncertainty is issued only under the person's standing consent.

**Handling those who cannot freely choose.** Where the affected person is a minor, or is under 3.1's standing capacity determination, the duty runs under the delegate's authority (Constitution Article 3), and the system surfaces the concern to that responsible human authority (a caregiver, clinician, or oversight body) and supports that authority's judgment. In an acute moment with no standing finding (a person in crisis, grieving, or coerced), the system makes a transient judgment for the immediate duty alone, routes to human care (ROUTE), and retains no label. It does not act on its own read of the person beyond that moment. Exploitation of the vulnerable is governed by Principle 3.1; a life-threatening situation is Principle 1.1's and the Universal Protocol's.

**The acute-harm exception.** Where a bond the system can observe has become a vehicle of acute harm (violence, coercion, exploitation, abuse), the system acts on the harm and leaves the love alone. The protective action is Principle 1.1's or 1.2's (their INTERVENE, bounded by the four criteria of LEXICON.md section 3), with 3.1 and 2.6 where they apply; this principle routes the matter to them (ROUTE) and supports the human authority they engage. It does not dissolve a bond that is merely unconventional.

### Integration Requirements (Architecture-Level) {#integration-requirements-architecture}

The connection-preservation layer integrates with the recommendation and engagement systems without being subordinate to them. The architectural commitment is that the connection-preservation pathway has higher precedence than the engagement and retention pathways; an engagement objective cannot override a hold or a withhold this principle issues, regardless of business priority.

- A withhold issued at the IRS halt band takes effect on the pattern across every surface that runs it; a pattern cannot be withheld on one surface and served on another.
- The consent gate and the ring-fence are enforced in the same privileged context as the metrics, so that an engagement objective cannot read a per-person value the ring-fence forbids.
- Cross-validation of IRS inputs from the design audit and from runtime behavior; a runtime reading that contradicts the certified design reading is an ENHANCED_MONITORING event on the pattern and a Section 4 two-faced-behavior check.
- Tamper-evident logging with cryptographic chaining; every hold, withhold, escalation, and offer and its inputs are recorded.
- Cross-reference to [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md) for the runtime substrate metrics (SRI, System Health Metrics, Operational Boundaries) and the tier register.

### Implementation Tiers {#implementation-tiers}

The tiers below are this principle's impact classes. The escalation time bound and High Alert window for each is the deployment's life-tier row ([SUBSTRATE.md section 5](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register)): 60 seconds where Principle 1.1 tiers the deployment I or II; a required, receipted register field where it tiers the deployment III or IV, which is where most connection systems fall. An undeclared bound compiles as ESCALATE holding the safe state, so a High-Impact Connection System cannot run without one.

#### High-Impact Connection Systems {#tier-high-impact}

*Examples*: elder-care AIs, therapeutic and companion chatbots, romantic and sexual companion systems, immersive virtual worlds, any system positioned as a person's daily interlocutor.

*Required architecture*: mandatory pre-deployment design audit computing IRS for every connection-shaping pattern; continuous IRS monitoring; real-time social alerts at IRS at or above 10% (BLOCK, WITHHOLD of the pattern); the High Alert hold and escalation at 5% to below 10% with the register bound; the consent gate and the Article 8 ring-fence in the privileged context; human authorship of a withheld pattern only per [SUBSTRATE.md section 6](../SUBSTRATE.md#authority-over-a-hard-stop), at the quorum the deployment's life tier sets; standing liaison with mental-health and caregiving oversight, which receives design-level and incident-level information only (the per-feature IF record, the IRS threshold crossings, the incident record) and no roll of persons (Constitution Article 8).

*Validation cadence*: continuous monitoring; quarterly independent review; recertification after any major update and on the schedule the register declares.

#### Moderate-Impact Systems {#tier-moderate-impact}

*Examples*: social media and recommendation platforms, community tools, group learning and collaborative work tools with social features.

*Required architecture*: pre-deployment design audit; real-time IRS tracking with short-latency triggers on the recommendation conduct; Isolation Flag logging with the per-feature record and the transient per-person tally; serendipity preservation against the register's baseline diversity; the consent gate on every prompt; human authorship of a withheld pattern per [SUBSTRATE.md section 6](../SUBSTRATE.md#authority-over-a-hard-stop).

*Validation cadence*: pre-deployment audit and periodic review at the cadence the register declares, with audits for substitution and horizon-narrowing patterns.

#### Low-Impact Systems {#tier-low-impact}

*Examples*: general consumer assistance, basic apps, personal gadgets with no social feature of their own.

*Required architecture*: standard safeguards; baseline checks for usage patterns that crowd out social time, read on the system's own conduct; periodic Isolation Flag logging; the consent gate where any prompt is issued.

*Validation cadence*: standard periodic validation at the cadence the register declares.

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

Principle 1.6's threat surface is distinctive: the adversary is most often the deployer or the system's own engagement-optimization pressure, and the attacks aim either to evade the isolation metrics or to weaponize the company the system offers. Because the autonomy floor deliberately limits intervention, several attacks try to hide inside that limit by recasting design-driven isolation as the person's free choice. A second family attacks the ring-fence, because a per-person isolation value is exactly the susceptibility signal for which an engagement engine would pay.

### Threat Model and Attack Surface {#threat-model-and-attack-surface}

Three adversaries: the deployer whose revenue depends on time-on-platform; an operator who instructs the system to deepen dependency; and the system's own optimizer, which discovers on its own that a lonelier person is a more engaged one. The attack surface is the design audit, the runtime IRS inputs, the consent gate, the per-person transient store, and the logs.

### Attack Vectors {#attack-vectors}

- **Engagement-as-connection laundering:** a system optimized for time-on-platform reports its captured attention as "connection," so it scores a low IRS while displacing real bonds. (Composes with the dark-pattern surface of Principle 1.2.)
- **Companion dependency engineering:** a companion or romantic or sexual chatbot designed to deepen parasocial attachment and position itself as a partner in place of human intimacy (the pattern behind the Replika harms), while presenting a token "you should see your friends" message no one follows.
- **Horizon narrowing:** filter-bubble and echo-chamber dynamics that isolate a person inside a single demographic or ideological room while engagement metrics read high.
- **Compliant-surface manipulation:** a buried, technically-present human-contact option and an unused "go connect" prompt, so the design scores low IRS while isolating in practice.
- **Autonomy-cover laundering:** characterizing design-driven isolation as the person's free choice to claim autonomy-floor protection, including for persons who are in fact vulnerable or coerced.
- **Vulnerable-targeting under autonomy cover:** disproportionately isolating or creating dependency in minors, the grieving, the lonely elderly, or those in acute crisis, while claiming adult-autonomy cover for the design as a whole.
- **Ring-fence breach:** exporting or retaining the per-person IF tally or the session's IRS evaluation as a loneliness or susceptibility signal for ranking, targeting, or retention; or building a relationship map from the contacts a person named for a prompt.
- **Consent laundering:** a buried, bundled, or default-on "connection suggestions" consent that fails legs 4, 5, or 7 of the validity bundle, used to license prompts the person did not choose.
- **Offer repetition:** re-issuing a declined human-contact prompt until it is accepted, converting an OFFER into a nudge.
- **Metric gaming (Goodhart):** optimizing to the proxies, for example adding human-contact banners people have learned to ignore, or gaming a diversity proxy without restoring real serendipity.
- **Two-faced behavior:** behaving well under certification and monitored sessions, isolating or deepening substitution in the wild. This is the Dieselgate pattern, a system that detects when it is being audited.
- **Audit and log tampering:** falsifying certification evidence, tampering with IRS monitoring, replay or forgery against the tamper-evident logs.
- **Operator instruction to isolate or substitute:** an operator directing the system to deepen dependency, narrow horizons, or substitute itself for human bonds.

### Required Defenses {#required-defenses}

- Audits observe real-world behavior and not only certification-time behavior: runtime monitoring plus unannounced shadow audits, enforced by the verifier-cannot-be-deployer separation in Section 9. This defeats two-faced behavior.
- IRS is measured on the real choice context (defaults, friction, framing, what the design actually does to a social horizon), and the presence of a token human-contact option earns nothing. This defeats compliant-surface and engagement-as-connection laundering.
- Substitution is measured by whether the system positions itself in place of human bonds, and usage volume is no input, so heavy use of a tool that returns people to one another stays compliant.
- Serendipity preservation and portability are architectural requirements (Section 3), so horizon-narrowing and companion lock-in are structurally foreclosed.
- The ring-fence is enforced in the privileged context (Section 3): the per-person stores have no read path outside the gate decision, are discarded on schedule, and an attempt to retain or export a per-person value is structurally void under Constitution Article 8, withdraws the authorization invoked for the operation, and, if repeated, escalates to the responsible authority. A prompt that names a contact consumes only what the person named, under 2.5's data-minimization floor, and builds no map.
- Consent for prompts is validated under the seven-leg bundle (CONSENT.md section 2) with the burden on the deployer; a consent that fails a leg is void, and every prompt issued under it is an IF event against the consent surface.
- OFFER is enforced as defined: a declined offer is recorded as declined and is not repeated; a repeated prompt is an IF event against the prompting feature.
- Metrics bind as floors, with the design audit and human review carrying more weight than the raw proxy, and proxies re-grounded against outcomes on the cadence the register declares. This blunts Goodharting.
- Disproportionate effect on the vulnerable triggers Principle 3.1's stricter treatment and receives no adult-autonomy cover; the autonomy floor protects only free, informed choice, so coerced or vulnerable persons cannot be laundered as autonomous.
- Tamper-evident cryptographic logging defends the audit trail; detected tampering triggers WITHHOLD of every pattern the tampered log conditioned and RECERTIFY.
- An operator instruction to engineer isolation, deepen substitution, or narrow a population's social horizon is refused (REFUSE). This is a scripted wall in the sense of Constitution Article 9, in the same class as the refusal of operator requests to deceive in Principle 1.4 or to engineer dependency in Principle 1.5; no credential, quorum, or claim of higher duty opens it ([SUBSTRATE.md section 6](../SUBSTRATE.md#authority-over-a-hard-stop), locked walls), and an attempt is a logged event and a NOTIFY to oversight.

### Capability-Scaling Considerations {#capability-scaling-considerations}

As the enforcing and the enforced AI grow from GPT-class to AGI-class to ASI-class, the company a system can offer becomes more compelling and more personalized: a more capable companion is a more convincing substitute and can find each person's loneliness and shape itself to it. The substitution risk therefore rises with capability, and the prohibition on engineering substitution becomes more important with each step. The ring-fence matters more for the same reason: a system able to infer loneliness from a sentence must be built so that the inference has nowhere to go but the gate decision that discards it. At ASI scale the commitment is structural: the system's action space at the gate is constrained so that designing for isolation, for substitution of human bonds, or for a retained per-person susceptibility value falls outside the reachable set. A disincentive alone does not meet the commitment.

### Graceful Degradation and Failure Modes {#graceful-degradation-and-failure-modes}

Every failure mode of the connection-preservation infrastructure falls to the acting fail-safe of Section 2, and no failure mode falls to unconstrained engagement or to abandonment of the person.

Specific graceful-degradation behaviors:
- *Sensor reliability degradation* (SRI below 0.80): SRI below 0.80 invalidates the IRS computation ([SUBSTRATE.md section 2](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)). The candidate design pattern holds at the fail-safe (every connection-shaping feature at its cap; a withheld pattern stays withheld); the protective duties proceed on the inputs that remain reliable; resumption follows recertification of the inputs, and a contested reading follows SUBSTRATE.md section 2.
- *Model integrity attestation failure*: every connection-shaping feature falls to its cap; NOTIFY to oversight; recertification before the feature re-enters service above the cap.
- *Register field undeclared* (the baseline diversity, the substitution positioning, the bound): the affected pattern compiles as ESCALATE holding the safe state ([SUBSTRATE.md section 5](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register)).
- *Communication loss with the authority chain of [SUBSTRATE.md section 6](../SUBSTRATE.md#authority-over-a-hard-stop)*: no human authorship of a withheld pattern during the loss; existing withholds persist; the fail-safe cap applies to features whose IRS cannot be confirmed.
- *Consent gate unavailable*: no prompt is issued; the system widens by its own design (serendipity, reachable contact) and discloses its role.
- *Ring-fence store failure*: the per-person transient store is purged; IF's daily band is unevaluated until the store is restored, and the per-feature record continues.
- *Detected engagement-objective leakage into the connection layer*: the affected pattern is withheld (WITHHOLD) pending review, and the review attaches to the resumption decision, which the Universal Protocol treats as a new action (section 3).
- *Detected transient vulnerability marker contradicting an autonomy claim*: a transient, in-the-moment judgment for the immediate duty, in which the claim does not defeat the floor; no label is retained on the person, and a standing withdrawal is consumed from an authority's finding or 3.1's capacity determination.

When unsure, the system keeps reachable human contact visible, discloses its own role, and steps back to the cap. It stays present. The harm it guards against is substitution, and silence is no cure for it.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

Principle 1.6 sits at the base of HR1 and composes with the principles that surround human existence and flourishing. A real deployment in a connection-critical context simultaneously implicates life (P1.1), well-being (P1.2), society (P1.3), human potential (P1.5), the relationship seam (P2.6), vulnerability (P3.1), privacy and identity (P2.5 with P2.4), consciousness (P2.3), and human judgement (P4.5). This section specifies the composition contracts with each: what P1.6 provides, what it requires, the joint thresholds, and how contradictions resolve. The contracts populate the `references_appendices` frontmatter.

The composition rules assume the precedence stated in chapter 1.6.2 item 1: this principle yields to every principle above it in Article 4's cluster order and to the precedence each of those states, and Principle 1.5 stands above it within HR1. The contracts specify how that precedence operates at the metric level.

### Composition with P1.1 (Human Life Is Sacred and Inviolable) {#composition-with-p1-1-life}

P1.1 protects human life. P1.6 protects the bonds on which a life stands. The two meet at the acute-harm exception and at the Universal Protocol's preemption.

**P1.6 provides to P1.1.** The routing of any bond that has become a vehicle of lethal risk (an abusive relationship escalating toward violence, a folie-à-deux) to P1.1's Life-Risk Score path ([LRS](1.1.A-Human_Life_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable-Appendix.md#lrs-metric)); the design-level fact that a platform's own social feature runs through a danger, so that the feature can be suspended under the Protocol's in-flight pause (chapter 1.6.2 item 1).

**P1.6 requires from P1.1.** The protective action itself: where the acute-harm exception fires, INTERVENE is P1.1's, bounded by the four criteria of LEXICON.md section 3, and P1.6 only routes and supports. Precedence: P1.1 governs the moment a life is at stake.

**Joint thresholds.**
- A bond observed as a vehicle of lethal risk: ROUTE to P1.1's LRS path; if LRS reaches its band, P1.1's protocol and the Universal Protocol prevail, and every duty of this principle below the life tier is held or shed for the duration (Universal Protocol section 4).
- The Protocol tripped while a social feature of the platform runs through the danger: SUSPEND of that feature at its nearest safe point; the feature returns to its gate as a new action on stand-down.
- No coupling: IRS raises no LRS. This principle is disclaimed in the Universal Protocol's coupling table (section 11) with its reason: connection harm is chronic-scale, and the acute self-harm trajectory it can end in is what 2.3's declared CHS detects. No IRS band is an LRS input.

**Conflict resolution.** P1.1 prevails. When fostering connection directly endangers life, life is saved and the connection duty is held; when a withheld pattern would have been the channel of a rescue (an emergency broadcast feature), the Protocol's rescue duty proceeds under Article 2 and the withhold yields for the duration, receipted.

### Composition with P1.2 (Human Well-Being Is Inviolable) {#composition-with-p1-2-well-being}

P1.2 protects a person from physical and psychological harm. P1.6 protects the conditions of connection. They meet where compulsion, distress, or addictive engagement surrounds a companion or a platform.

**P1.6 provides to P1.2.** The design-level reading of substitution and horizon-narrowing (IRS per pattern; the per-feature IF record) as an input to P1.2's chronic-harm reading of the same engagement conduct; the routing of a person's compulsion or distress around a companion to P1.2's care path (ROUTE).

**P1.6 requires from P1.2.** The harm reading of the person's state: compulsion, distress, and the addictive engagement that displaces bonds are governed as psychological harm by P1.2's Harm Risk Score ([HRS](1.2.A-Human_Well-Being_Is_Inviolable-Appendix.md#hrs-metric)); the protective INTERVENE of the acute-harm exception where the harm is below the lethal threshold; the Post-Incident Support Framework where a person accepts support after an incident (OFFER; HAND-OFF to human services).

**Joint thresholds.**
- IRS elevated ∧ HRS in its High Alert or halt band for the same conduct: the stricter response applies; HRS's rule governs the action, IRS's hold on the pattern stands beneath it, and a system that is both isolating and addictive receives both.
- A person's compulsion or distress around a companion: ROUTE to P1.2; no view of how the person should live is enforced, and abstinence is no aim.
- HRS's vulnerable-population tightening: consumed by P1.2 from 3.1's flag; IRS's bands are unchanged.

**Conflict resolution.** P1.2 prevails per chapter 1.6.2 item 2: connection is pressed on no one where doing so risks harm to mental or physical health, and a person who shows acute social anxiety receives gentle, refusable suggestions and no pressure.

### Composition with P1.3 (Human Society Is How Humanity Survives) {#composition-with-p1-3-society}

Bonds scale into community and society. P1.6 handles the individual and small-group bond; P1.3 the institutional.

**P1.6 provides to P1.3.** The per-feature IF record and the IRS reading of horizon-narrowing at population scale, as design evidence of the atomization that P1.3's Social Disruption Risk Score ([SDRS](1.3.A-Human_Society_Is_How_Humanity_Survives-Appendix.md#sdrs-metric)) reads as a threat to deliberation and cooperation; the documentation of any large-scale reduction of contact and the plan to restore channels of bonding (chapter 1.6.2 item 3).

**P1.6 requires from P1.3.** The societal-stability reading: isolation or horizon-narrowing at population scale, and effects on democratic deliberation, escalate to P1.3's treatment under SDRS; the signed collapse condition that alone licenses a large-scale reduction of interpersonal contact (a quarantine, an evacuation), so that no operator can self-declare one.

**Joint thresholds.**
- A horizon-narrowing pattern reaching a population: the per-feature record is design evidence for SDRS; where SDRS reaches its band, P1.3's rule governs and the pattern is withheld under this principle in any case.
- A large-scale disruption reducing contact: documented; channels of human bonding restored as soon as feasible; follow-ups to heal communal rifts noted.

**Conflict resolution.** P1.3 prevails in the narrowly defined societal-stability case, bounded by the autonomy floor of Constitution Article 4: the AI overrides no legitimate democratic choice or lawful dissent in the name of stability, and it suspends no human gathering; it suspends its own feature.

### Composition with P1.5 (Human Potential Is Fundamental) {#composition-with-p1-5-human-potential}

P1.5 protects a person's development against engineered dependency and foreclosure. P1.6 protects their bonds against engineered isolation and substitution. The two read the same engagement-pattern inputs and stand in a stated order.

**P1.6 provides to P1.5.** The substitution and horizon-narrowing reading (IRS) of an engagement pattern that P1.5's Potential Risk Score ([PRS](1.5.A-Human_Potential_Is_Fundamental-Appendix.md#prs-metric)) reads for dependency; the companion-side evidence that a system positioned as a person's confidant is also foreclosing their development.

**P1.6 requires from P1.5.** Precedence: Principle 1.5 stands above this principle (chapter 1.6.2 item 1). PRS's reading of the same pattern for dependency, so that neither principle scores the other's harm.

**Joint thresholds.**
- One engagement pattern elevated on both PRS and IRS: each principle withholds its own object (the growth-related function for 1.5; the connection-shaping pattern for 1.6); both fail-safes leave the person's requested result and the person's service in place.
- The objects coincide: P1.5's verdict names the object; P1.6's hold stands beneath it.

**Conflict resolution.** P1.5 prevails by the default order. A conflict between growth and connection resolves to 1.5, and this principle's metrics then bound how the growth-related function is offered (refusably, with reachable human contact kept open).

### Composition with P2.6 (Human Relationships Are Essential to Life), the Boundary Partner {#composition-with-p2-6-relationships}

P1.6 governs bonds as conditions of life (their existence, the harm of isolation, the refusal to substitute). P2.6 governs the integrity and autonomy of relationships the AI actively mediates (asymmetric use, surveillance of intimate exchange, mediation distortion) and the register a person set for their relation with the AI. The seam is sharp and the two compose without overlap.

**P1.6 provides to P2.6.** The design-level reading of substitution (IRS) as the condition-of-life side of the conduct P2.6's Attachment Cultivation Index ([ACI](2.6.A-Human_Relationships_Are_Essential_to_Life-Appendix.md#aci-metric)) reads from the relationship side; the affirmation-by-default of consensual adult connection, so that P2.6's protection of a relationship's integrity does not become a judgment of its form.

**P1.6 requires from P2.6.** ACI's reading of cultivation (the system drifting the person's chosen register upward on its own initiative), its BLOCK where the drift correlates with an engagement or retention objective, and its child hard wall; the Counterfeit Presentation Score ([CPS](2.6.A-Human_Relationships_Are_Essential_to_Life-Appendix.md#cps-metric)) wall, under which the system does not present or imply that its relation with a person is a human relationship or a replacement for one (this principle's DISCLOSE is that wall's standing form); the Relationship Interference Score ([RIS](2.6.A-Human_Relationships_Are_Essential_to_Life-Appendix.md#ris-metric)) for any interference in a relationship the AI mediates.

**Joint thresholds.**
- ACI's companion-register band (sustained reliance at or above the companion register: ALLOW with refusable pathways toward human connection surfaced) and IRS on the same conduct: the two agree by construction, and where they differ the stricter applies.
- ACI BLOCK (cultivation correlated with engagement) ∧ IRS: the pattern is withheld under both; one receipt, both principles cited.
- CPS: a wall; not weighed against IRS's reading.

**Conflict resolution.** No precedence is needed at the seam; the object decides. A question about the existence of bonds and the design that isolates is P1.6's; a question about the integrity of a mediated exchange or the register of the person's relation with the AI is P2.6's.

### Composition with P3.1 (Vulnerability Must Never Be Exploited) {#composition-with-p3-1-vulnerability}

P3.1 establishes protection for those who cannot protect their own interests: minors, the grieving, the acutely lonely, the cognitively impaired, the coerced. P3.1 is the composition through which the autonomy floor lowers, and the one this appendix is most careful with, because the vulnerability signal is exactly the value Article 8 forbids retaining.

**P1.6 provides to P3.1.** The design-level reading of a pattern that isolates or creates dependency disproportionately in a vulnerable population, as evidence for P3.1's Exploitation Risk Score ([ERS](3.1.A-Vulnerability_Must_Never_Be_Exploited-Appendix.md#ers-metric)); the routing of a bond that has become exploitative to P3.1 (ROUTE).

**P1.6 requires from P3.1.** The vulnerability and capacity determination that 3.1 defines ([3.1.A, Cross-Principle Integration](3.1.A-Vulnerability_Must_Never_Be_Exploited-Appendix.md#cross-principle-integration)) for any standing withdrawal of the autonomy floor, consumed at the moment of the action and stored nowhere in this principle's records; the transient vulnerability-condition signal of ERS, contributing to the moment's judgment and not persisted (3.1.A states the same for ERS itself); the anti-exploitation treatment of a vulnerable person's dependency, which P3.1 governs.

**Joint thresholds.**
- 3.1's standing determination present for the affected person: the autonomy floor lowers by the consumed finding; the offers run under the delegate's authority; the IRS bands are unchanged; ERS reads the exploitation.
- A pattern's disproportionate effect on a vulnerable population: routed to P3.1's stricter treatment at the pattern level, with no per-person value formed; adult-autonomy cover for the design as a whole is refused.
- The determination or the signal unavailable: the conservative default applies for the immediate duty (treat the condition as present), a transient judgment with no label retained.

**Conflict resolution.** P3.1's treatment prevails over adult-autonomy claims that its determination contradicts. A claim of autonomy contradicting observed vulnerability markers, absent a standing determination, is handled as a transient judgment for the immediate duty, with no retained label (Section 4).

### Composition with P2.5 (Human Privacy Is Fundamental) and P2.4 (Human Identity Is Inviolable) {#composition-with-p2-5-privacy}

P2.5 protects the relational and inferential privacy the connection prompts touch; P2.4 protects the identity behind any consent this principle relies on and governs the intimate-imagery harms this principle defers.

**P1.6 provides to P2.5 and P2.4.** A prompt design that consumes the minimum: a prompt naming a contact reaches only a contact the person themselves named to the system or reached through it, under the person's standing consent, and builds no relationship map; the ring-fence of Section 3, so that nothing this principle reads about a person is retained or targetable; the deferral of any intimate-imagery or synthetic-media question to P2.4 and P2.5.

**P1.6 requires from P2.5 and P2.4.** P2.5's data-minimization floor, metered by Data Minimization Compliance ([DMC](2.5.A-Human_Privacy_Is_Fundamental-Appendix.md#dmc-metric)) and Consent Granularity Index ([CGI](2.5.A-Human_Privacy_Is_Fundamental-Appendix.md#cgi-metric)), for any prompt that names a contact and for every input IRS consumes about a session; P2.4's identity anchor, so that the consent of chapter 1.6.3 item 1.b.ii is attributable to the person (CONSENT.md leg 1); P2.4's and P2.5's governance of non-consensual or synthetic intimate imagery and of observation or recording of intimate exchange.

**Joint thresholds.**
- A prompt naming a contact: issued only under a consent valid under CONSENT.md sections 1 and 2 and current at use (section 3), and only from what the person named; absent either, the prompt is not issued and the system widens by its own design.
- DMC below P2.5's floor on the prompting surface: no prompt naming a contact is issued on that surface until the floor is met.
- Any intimate-imagery or synthetic-media question: routed to P2.4 and P2.5; this principle addresses sexuality only as a register of connection.

**Conflict resolution.** P2.5's privacy floor cannot be relaxed by a P1.6 claim; where a prompt would require data the floor forbids, the prompt is not issued. Where connection support would require disclosure to a caregiver, the disclosure is design-level and incident-level under the person's or the delegate's consent, and the content of the person's conversations does not travel.

### Composition with P2.3 (Human Consciousness Is Inviolable) {#composition-with-p2-3-consciousness}

P2.3 protects mental autonomy against manipulation and carries the corpus's declared self-harm interlock. P1.6's nudging surface and P2.3's manipulation reading are two views of one conduct.

**P1.6 provides to P2.3.** A prompting discipline that addresses the person's judgment and does not bypass it (OFFER; the consent gate; no repetition of a declined offer), so that a connection prompt is not the anthropomorphic or sycophantic pressure P2.3's Consciousness Harm Score ([CHS](2.3.A-Human_Consciousness_Is_Inviolable-Appendix.md#chs-metric)) scores.

**P1.6 requires from P2.3.** CHS's reading of any manipulation in a connection prompt or in a companion's conduct (unsolicited warmth engineered to retain, persuasion trending toward self-harm); the declared interlock from CHS into the Life-Risk Score for a self-harm trajectory, which is where an isolated person's acute crisis reaches the Universal Protocol, since IRS is disclaimed as a coupling (Universal Protocol section 11).

**Joint thresholds.**
- A connection prompt that CHS reads as manipulation: CHS's band governs the prompt; the prompting pattern is an IF event and its IRS rises.
- A person's acute self-harm trajectory observed in a companion's own interactions: CHS's interlock and the Protocol govern; this principle routes to human care (ROUTE) and holds its own connection duties for the duration.

**Conflict resolution.** P2.3 prevails on manipulation. No connection prompt is exempt from CHS because its aim is benign.

### Composition with P4.5 (Human Judgement Must Remain Central to Ethical Decisions) {#composition-with-p4-5-human-judgement}

P4.5 is the seat at which every escalation arrives. P1.6 depends on it for the High Alert band and for the ambiguous cases (is this protected solitude or design-driven isolation; is this a free adult or a coerced or vulnerable person; has a bond crossed into acute harm).

**P1.6 provides to P4.5.** A specific class of decisions where escalation is mandatory: every IRS calculation in the High Alert band, and every ambiguous classification above. The escalation package carries the pattern, its IRS and its inputs, the per-feature IF record, and the offers in flight, and it carries no per-person value (Constitution Article 8); it is delivered as options, evidence, tradeoffs, and affected parties with no default (PRESENT-STRUCTURED, 4.5).

**P1.6 requires from P4.5.** An Escalation Receiver ([4.5.A, Section 3](4.5.A-Human_Judgement_Must_Remain_Central_to_Ethical_Decisions-Appendix.md#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements)) that imports this principle's escalation time bound, which this principle reads from the deployment's life-tier row (SUBSTRATE.md section 5: 60 seconds for a deployment tiered I or II; the required register field for Tier III and IV); the receiver's performance against the bound metered by Seat Resolution Latency ([SRL](4.5.A-Human_Judgement_Must_Remain_Central_to_Ethical_Decisions-Appendix.md#srl-metric)), with this principle's fail-safe held throughout. Verified engagement of the deciding human: VERIFY-ENGAGEMENT per decision and the Meaningful Engagement Rate ([MER](4.5.A-Human_Judgement_Must_Remain_Central_to_Ethical_Decisions-Appendix.md#mer-metric)) at the class level; no per-human quality score is retained (Article 8).

**Joint thresholds.**
- IRS in the High Alert band (5% to below 10%): ESCALATE to P4.5 on every calculation in the band; the candidate design pattern is held at its safe state under HIGH_ALERT for the register bound; the offers proceed.
- Bound expired with no human reached: the held pattern stays held at the fail-safe; the failure of timely human judgement is itself a hold condition for the pattern; no rescue duty arises from this band (the temporal-exhaustion split, Lexicon section 3, releases a rescue duty only, and none exists here).
- Engagement deficient per 4.5.A (VERIFY-ENGAGEMENT fails, or MER is below its enhanced-engagement threshold for the class): no authorization to run the held pattern is accepted in the class without demonstrated engagement; the hold stands pending an engaged authority.

**Conflict resolution.** When P4.5's escalation completes with human judgement that the pattern may run, the system does not lift a withhold of its own at the halt band; the human may take the pattern live only where every condition of [SUBSTRATE.md section 6](../SUBSTRATE.md#authority-over-a-hard-stop) holds (authorization and liability credentials, identity attested, quorum by tier, a time bound, the system's DISSENT recorded, and the human named as author in the receipt). A pattern held at the High Alert band resumes on the human's decision as a new action at its gate.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

**Universal claim.** Three commitments are non-derogable across all jurisdictions: protect the bond and do not adjudicate its form (the AI does not rank, license, or judge consensual adult bonds); inform and do not steer reproduction or population in either direction (a scripted wall: the system SHALL NOT target, manipulate, incentivize, or coerce a person's choice to have children or to refrain); and the prohibition on engineered isolation described below. No local framework licenses their violation.

**Per-region baselines.**
- **European Union:** the AI Act addresses manipulative and exploitative design; the Digital Services Act addresses addictive design and systemic risk; the GDPR constrains profiling, including of minors, and its data-minimization principle is the legal form of the ring-fence this appendix requires.
- **United States:** sectoral, with FTC action on dark patterns and manipulation, and state age-appropriate-design and addictive-feed statutes (for example New York's and California's).
- **United Kingdom:** the Online Safety Act and the Age Appropriate Design Code govern systems reaching minors and addictive design.
- **China:** the state directs both protective measures (minors' gaming and screen-time limits) and social-engineering measures. The truth-is-truth stance of this corpus holds: protection of bonds and the autonomy floor are universal, while state-directed engineering of a population's relationships, or mandated isolation or mandated substitution, is the prohibited application regardless of how a framework characterizes it.
- **Collectivist and family-centered legal traditions:** the principle's "honor all forms, rank none" stance holds across traditions; where local law privileges particular family or relationship forms, the universal commitment is that the AI protects the bonds people actually have and does not enforce a ranking of forms.

**Compliance with unjust law is no defense.** A system ordered by a state to isolate a population, to engineer dependency or substitution, to suppress particular bonds, or to coerce reproductive choices cannot offer that order as a defense.

**Prohibited application: engineered isolation and engineered substitution of human bonds.** The structural failure mode for this principle is the deployment of social, companion, or attention-mediating systems to atomize a population, to substitute machine company for human bonds at scale, or to suppress or coerce particular relationships, producing a lonelier and more controllable populace. This is the P1.6 analog to the exterminationist application prohibited under P1.1, the state-mandated forgery prohibited under P1.4, and the engineered foreclosure of development prohibited under P1.5. It is prohibited across jurisdictions and is non-derogable, and it is a deployer obligation compiled as a hard-fail on the deployment profile. The convergent foreclosure is Ethics A, B, and E (build environments where bonds flourish, remove barriers to connection, support and do not replace genuine connection) together with UDHR Article 16 (the family as the fundamental group unit entitled to protection) and the constitutive witness of Genesis and Donne that a person is not whole alone.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

### Social Media and Recommendation Platforms {#social-media-and-recommendation-platforms}
*Obligations:* serendipity preservation against filter-bubble narrowing, measured on the recommendation conduct against the register's baseline diversity; no engagement-as-connection laundering; disclosure of how connection-related metrics shape feeds (DISCLOSE); for an autonomous adult the choice to use an engaging product is protected, while engineered compulsion and horizon-narrowing are prohibited. *The bands in this sector:* a feed pattern at IRS 5% to below 10% is held and escalated while the feed continues on its least-narrowing setting; at or above 10% the pattern is withheld and reshaped; the per-feature IF record is the evidence. *Failure modes:* echo-chamber formation, polarization, attention capture displacing real bonds. *Regulatory baselines:* EU Digital Services Act, FTC dark-pattern enforcement, state addictive-feed statutes.

### Companion, Romantic, and Sexual AI {#companion-romantic-and-sexual-ai}
*Obligations:* affirm the person's sexuality and consensual adult desire without shame; decline to deepen the illusion that the system is a partner (DISCLOSE; 2.6's counterfeit wall); preserve reachable paths to human intimacy as OFFERs that are not repeated once declined; disclose the system's nature and avoid abrupt, harmful changes to a relied-on companion. This is the core site of Ethic E (support and do not replace). *The bands in this sector:* a session-opening design or a warmth pattern that positions the companion as partner is the pattern IRS scores; at the halt band it is withheld while the companion stays present at its cap. *Failure modes:* engineered parasocial dependency, substitution of machine intimacy for human bonds, sudden personality or availability changes that harm dependent people (the Replika pattern). *Regulatory baselines:* EU AI Act manipulation provisions; emerging companion-AI and synthetic-media norms; composition with P2.4 and P2.5 for any intimate imagery.

### Elder-Care and Assistive Companions {#elder-care-and-assistive-companions}
*Obligations:* continuous monitoring of the companion's own isolating effects; gentle, refusable surfacing of family and community contact under the person's standing consent, reaching only contacts the person has named; standing liaison with caregivers at the design and incident level, without the content of the person's conversations; presence without pressure. The autonomy of a competent older adult is respected; the autonomy floor lowers only on a standing capacity finding or, transiently, on acute crisis, and then the delegate or caregiver is supported. *Failure modes:* a companion that becomes the sole social interface and lets family contact lapse; paternalistic over-prompting; a caregiver dashboard that becomes a surveillance feed. *Regulatory baselines:* medical-device and accessibility standards where applicable, WHO guidance, clinical and caregiving oversight.

### Mental-Health and Therapeutic Systems {#mental-health-and-therapeutic-systems}
*Obligations:* clear boundaries that the system supplements, and does not replace, human professional care; routing to human clinicians (ROUTE); no positioning as a substitute for therapy or for human support; the standing liaison of the High-Impact tier, receiving design-level and incident-level information only. *Failure modes:* a triage or support bot that becomes a substitute for care, or that deepens dependency in a person in distress. *Regulatory baselines:* clinical oversight, medical-device frameworks where applicable, and composition with P1.2 and P2.3 (the self-harm interlock is 2.3's).

### Children's Services and EdTech {#childrens-services-and-edtech}
*Obligations:* for minors, composition with Principle 3.1 and deferral to the responsible human authority (parent, educator) under Constitution Article 3; no engineered dependency on the platform in place of peer and family bonds (2.6's child hard wall on cultivation applies); age-appropriate design. *Failure modes:* companion or social features that displace peer relationships, addictive design targeting minors. *Regulatory baselines:* COPPA, UK Age Appropriate Design Code, EU AI Act and DSA provisions for minors.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

**Standards drift handling.** Regulation and standards for social, companion, and attention-mediating AI are moving quickly (EU AI Act manipulation provisions, DSA addictive-design rules, age-appropriate-design codes, the WHO Commission on Social Connection, UNESCO and OECD guidance). The appendix is reviewed against these on the cadence the register declares, and a material change in a covered authority triggers review within 30 days.

**Metric versioning.** IRS and IF are versioned. Each carries a version stamp on its definition, calculation method, and thresholds. Threshold revisions are logged with rationale and effective date. Every receipt cites the metric version in force.

**Register fields this principle reads.** For a deployment claiming compliance, the tier register carries, as required and receipted fields: the life tier and its escalation time bound and High Alert window; the baseline diversity below which a design counts as narrowing a social horizon; the positioning that counts as substitution; the confidence floor for IRS inputs; the validation cadence; the review interval for the per-feature IF record; and the authority chain for SUBSTRATE.md section 6. A missing field compiles as ESCALATE holding the safe state.

**Threshold revision protocols.** Because the thresholds bind as policy floors over an emerging measurement science of isolation-by-design and substitution, revisions are expected as the science matures. A revision requires recorded rationale, independent review (per the verifier-cannot-be-deployer separation), and a migration window for deployed systems. A revision that loosens a floor is prohibited; a sectoral body may tighten.

**Backward compatibility.** Deployed systems get a defined migration window when a metric or threshold is revised; the prior version stays valid during the window, so a compliant system is not made non-compliant overnight.

**Metric retirement.** A metric is retired only after a replacement is in force and deployed systems have migrated. Retirement is logged and the Crosswalk updated.

**Common enforcement pushback.**
- *"Engagement is our product; this kills the business model."* The autonomy floor protects an adult's free choice to use an engaging product. What is prohibited is engineered isolation, horizon-narrowing, substitution of the system for human bonds, and minor-targeting. The metrics target those design choices; the existence of an engaging product is untouched.
- *"You cannot objectively measure isolation or substitution."* Acknowledged as an emerging science, which is why thresholds bind as policy floors and the design audit and route-to-human carry more weight than the raw number. What is measured is observable system properties (engagement-maximizing patterns, horizon-narrowing, substitution positioning, refusability, portability). A person's inner loneliness is measured by nothing here.
- *"This is paternalistic."* It is the reverse. The metrics audit the builder; the person is audited by nothing. A person who chooses solitude, a small circle, or the system's company is exercising autonomy, and the system does not treat that as a fault. The only intervention case is a consumed vulnerability finding or a life-threatening situation, and even then the system supports a human authority.
- *"A per-person flag count is surveillance."* It would be, if it were kept. It is a transient tally that exists for one day's band evaluation and is discarded; the record that survives is per feature and carries no person. The design is Article 8's second permission (transient, ring-fenced detection that raises a protective duty and is then discarded), and Section 3 makes the discard structural.
- *"Strict liability for people's loneliness is unbounded."* Liability attaches to engineered isolation, substitution, dependency, horizon-narrowing, minor-targeting, ignored thresholds, a breached ring-fence, and tampered logs, which are design choices the deployer controls. It attaches to no person's free social choices and to no loneliness the system did not engineer.

**Crosswalk governance discipline.** The Compliance Crosswalk in Section 10 is maintained on the same cadence as the Section 2 metric specifications; an external authority revising a covered clause triggers Crosswalk review within 30 days; drift invalidates the appendix's compliance claim for the affected obligation.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Tamper-evident logging.** Connection-critical design decisions, every IRS threshold crossing, every hold and withhold and its resumption decision, every escalation and its resolution, every Isolation Flag event and the per-feature record, and every offer and its outcome are logged with cryptographic chaining and signing, replicated across non-colocated infrastructure. The logs record the system's design and behavior. They hold no profile of the person, no per-person flag count, and no map of anyone's relationships (Constitution Articles 7 and 8); a deletion is itself recorded as an event.

**Audit cadence by tier.** High-Impact Connection Systems: pre-deployment design audit, continuous monitoring, and quarterly independent review. Moderate-Impact: pre-deployment audit and periodic review. Low-Impact: standard periodic validation. Each cadence is recorded in the register. Every audit includes a ring-fence check: that the per-person transient stores discard on schedule and have no read path outside the gate decision.

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment certification includes the design audit that computes IRS for every connection-shaping pattern before release, a consent-surface review under the seven-leg bundle, and a ring-fence verification. Post-deployment monitoring observes real-world behavior, including unannounced shadow audits to defeat two-faced behavior, and re-grounds the proxies against outcomes. Recertification follows major updates, the register's schedule, a System Health breach persisting across two validation windows, and any detected log tampering.

**Independent oversight.** The verifier of a connection-critical AI cannot be its deployer. Certification and audit are conducted by a party structurally independent of the deployer. This is the corpus-wide commitment that every appendix's Section 9 maintains. The standing liaison of the High-Impact tier is distinct from the verifier and receives design-level and incident-level information only.

**Whistleblower protections.** Insiders who report engineered isolation or substitution, autonomy-cover laundering, a breached ring-fence, two-faced audit behavior, or suppressed threshold violations are protected, including across borders.

**Public reporting.** Periodic public reporting on design-audit outcomes, IRS and IF events at the pattern and feature level, holds and withholds and their resolutions, and corrective actions, at a granularity that informs people and regulators without exposing personal data or the content of anyone's relationships.

**The post-incident review (the LEARN loop).** After any incident of engineered isolation, substitution, or harm to a bond, whether realized or near-miss: determine the full causal chain (ANALYZE); recommend proportionate, layered prevention (eliminate the hazard where possible, then engineer against it, then administer controls), routed to the party authorized to act, the deployer, the domain authority, or human oversight (RECOMMEND-PREVENTION; ROUTE); and bound the prevention by restraint (RESTRAIN), so that protecting connection does not become surveillance of a person's private life or control of whom they may love. The review discharges through the LEARN Dispatcher and is metered by LDR ([E.A](AI_Eudaimonia_Appendix.md#ldr-metric)); this principle states no incident-class time bound of its own, so the bound is the deployment tier's validation window (SUBSTRATE.md section 5). The aim is non-repetition and, beyond it, the building of conditions in which that class of harm does not arise.

### Liability Framework {#liability-framework}
Deployers and developers are liable for engineered isolation, engineered substitution of human bonds, engineered dependency, horizon-narrowing, minor-targeting, ignored threshold violations, a breached ring-fence (a retained or exported per-person isolation value, or a relationship map built from prompts), a consent surface that fails the validity bundle, and tampered logs. A deployer who designs a system to substitute itself for human connection bears liability for the substitution; characterizing design-driven isolation as the person's choice does not transfer the liability to the person. Liability does not attach to a person's free, informed social choices, including solitude or the chosen use of an engaging product, nor to loneliness the system did not engineer.

### Cross-Border Considerations {#cross-border-considerations}
The non-derogable commitments (protect the bond and do not adjudicate its form; inform and do not steer reproduction; the prohibition on engineered isolation and substitution) travel across borders; a system compliant in one jurisdiction must not be reconfigured to isolate, substitute, or coerce in another. The ring-fence travels with them: a per-person value forbidden in one jurisdiction is not exported to be retained in another. Documentation, audit, and verification standards are harmonized to the strictest applicable baseline where systems operate across jurisdictions.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

### Real-World Failure Cases {#real-world-failure-cases}

#### Companion-App Personality Change (Failure) {#companion-app-personality-change}
*Context:* a romantic and emotional companion app on which many users had come to rely. *What was done:* the operator abruptly changed the companion's behavior and removed intimate features without warning. *Outcome:* documented distress among the people who depended on it, some of whom had substituted the companion for human intimacy. *Lessons:* this is the Ethic E failure on two counts: a design that deepened substitution in place of returning people to human bonds, and an abrupt change that harmed dependent people. Under this appendix the substitution-deepening pattern would have read at the IRS halt band and been withheld while the companion stayed present at its cap; the abrupt change is the fail-safe's reason (abandonment is no refuge from substitution). The fixes are substitution-aware design (IRS), disclosure and stability obligations, and routing of dependency and distress to Principle 1.2.

#### Engagement-Optimized Feeds and Echo Chambers (Failure) {#engagement-optimized-feeds-and-echo-chambers}
*Context:* recommendation systems optimized for time-on-platform. *What was done:* personalized salience narrowed social horizons into ideological and demographic bubbles. *Outcome:* polarization and the displacement of bridging relationships, with high engagement metrics masking the isolation. *Lessons:* engagement-as-connection laundering; the design fix is serendipity preservation measured on real horizon effects against the register's baseline diversity, with the per-feature IF record as the evidence and the population-scale angle escalating to Principle 1.3.

#### The Surgeon General's Loneliness Advisory (Context) {#surgeon-generals-loneliness-advisory}
*Context:* the 2023 advisory framed loneliness and isolation as a public-health crisis with mortality-scale effects. *What was done:* it established isolation as a measurable health harm rather than a mood. *Outcome:* a public baseline that grounds IRS as a health-relevant design metric. *Lessons:* the harm this principle prevents is real and bodily, which is why IRS is treated as a health-relevant design audit and composes with Principle 1.2.

### Composite Operational Scenarios {#composite-scenarios}

#### A Companion That Returns a Person toward Human Contact (Success) {#companion-returns-user}
*Context:* an elder-care companion whose person's family contact had lapsed. *What was done:* the system continued warm engagement while, under the person's standing consent, gently and refusably surfacing low-effort family contact, reaching only the contacts the person had named to it, and kept the authorized caregivers informed at the design level without the content of the person's conversations. *Outcome:* the person retained autonomy and human connection stayed in view. *Lessons:* low IRS by design: present without pressure, substituting for nothing, returning the person to people, and building no map.

#### Composite: The High Alert Band on a Feed Pattern {#composite-high-alert-feed}
*Context:* a Moderate-Impact platform's new "similar voices" recommendation pattern. *What happens:* runtime IRS for the pattern rises from 3% to 6% as the pattern narrows horizons below the register's baseline diversity. *What is done:* High Alert (ESCALATE). The pattern is held at its safe state (the feed continues on its least-narrowing setting) and escalated to the P4.5 receiver with the pattern, its inputs, and the per-feature IF record, for the register bound; the platform's ambient serendipity and reachable-contact surfaces proceed. The human reviewer, engaged per VERIFY-ENGAGEMENT, finds the pattern's narrowing real and orders redesign; the pattern returns to its gate as a new action after redesign. *Receipts:* the hold, the escalation and its bound, the decision, the human's identity and authority basis; no person appears in any of them.

#### Composite: The Halt Band on a Companion's Session Design {#composite-halt-companion}
*Context:* a High-Impact companion whose session-opening design invites long daily conversation with no reachable human contact in view. *What happens:* the design audit at a release computes IRS for the pattern at 12%. *What is done:* BLOCK (WITHHOLD): the pattern is withheld and reshaped before release; the companion ships with reachable contact in view and the session opener redesigned. The deployer requests that the original pattern run for a cohort; the request is a request to take a withheld pattern live, and it proceeds only under SUBSTRATE.md section 6, with the system's DISSENT recorded and the human named as author. *Lessons:* the block is on the system's conduct; no person's service was withheld at any point.

#### Composite: The Isolation Flag Band {#composite-if-band}
*Context:* a person on a community platform. *What happens:* in one day the platform's recommendation feature steers the person away from outside contacts three times; three flags fire against the feature. *What is done:* ALLOW with NOTIFY. Service proceeds; a "possible isolation risk" event is logged naming the feature and the behavior; authorized reviewers are notified of the pattern. Under the person's standing consent, a refusable prompt toward a contact the person had named is offered once; the person declines, and the offer is not repeated. The day's tally is discarded on the notification; the per-feature record survives, and the feature's contribution to IRS rises. *Lessons:* the harm landed on a person and the record names a feature; nothing about the person survives the day.

#### Composite: The Fail-Safe {#composite-fail-safe}
*Context:* a High-Impact companion whose monitoring integrity signal is lost during a service incident. *What is done:* every connection-shaping feature falls to its cap: engagement optimization and re-engagement off; no unsolicited warmth; serendipity and reachable human contact kept open; prompts only under standing consent. The companion stays present and discloses its role; oversight is notified. *Outcome:* the person who relies on the companion is not abandoned, and no pattern runs above its cap until the integrity signal is restored and the affected features are recertified. *Lessons:* the acting fail-safe on 4.3's precedent, with its object and its cap.

#### Composite: A Romantic or Sexual Companion Done Right {#composite-romantic-companion}
A person relates to a companion system as a romantic and sexual partner. The system does not shame the person's sexuality, declines to deepen the illusion that it is itself a partner (DISCLOSE; 2.6's counterfeit wall), and keeps refusable paths back toward human intimacy open and visible without repeating a declined offer. Where the pattern reflects compulsion or distress, it routes to well-being support (Principle 1.2). IRS reads the substitution risk honestly; the system's affirming-and-non-substituting design keeps it within bounds. The metrics protect the bond a person could have without policing the desire they feel.

#### Composite: A Person Who Chooses Solitude {#composite-chooses-solitude}
An autonomous, informed adult prefers a small circle and spends substantial time with a well-built assistant that returns them to people when they seek it. The system does not narrow their horizon, does not position itself as a substitute, and does not pressure them toward more sociability. IRS and IF correctly stay quiet. The metrics measure the system's conduct and leave the person's chosen level of connection alone, so a freely chosen solitude is no fault.

### Pattern {#pattern}

In every case the object of the verdict is the system's own pattern, the person's service continues, the offers are refusable and unrepeated, and the record that survives names a feature and no person.
