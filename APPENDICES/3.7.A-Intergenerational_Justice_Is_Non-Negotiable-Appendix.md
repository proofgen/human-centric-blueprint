# Appendix 3.7 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-3.7-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 3.7: Intergenerational Justice Is Non-Negotiable](../CHAPTERS/3.7-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable.md), section [3.7.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/3.7-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix converts Principle 3.7 into buildable engineering requirements: the specifications by which an AI system detects the decisions that outlive their deciders, holds the irreversible to its higher bar, names every cost shipped forward, keeps everything it builds revisable, and represents future generations to the living without ever ruling in their name.

The principle presents a distinctive engineering problem: its outcomes mature beyond any operational window, so compliance cannot be scored on outcomes at runtime. The apparatus specified here therefore scores *present conduct*: whether the time-axis test ran, whether the disclosure was made in time to inform the decision, whether the exit was drilled. The long-cycle review (chapter 3.7.6.3, this appendix Section 9) is the return path by which maturing outcomes calibrate the conduct metrics, and the durable-archive requirements exist because the person with standing to contest a violation may not be born when the record is written.

Throughout, the HR3 cornerstone holds: every metric measures the system's conduct toward decisions and systems. None measures a person. The person-exclusion ring-fence ([Constitution, Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)) is specified here as architecture and verified as a negative proof, because the nearest failure mode of an intergenerational principle is the intergenerational-virtue score: the person rated for their carbon, their consumption, or their "future impact," with the future's name as the alibi.

Audiences: engineers building long-horizon systems; compliance and procurement officers; regulators and future-generations institutions auditing them; researchers extending the framework; and AI runtime systems ingesting the corpus as ethical operating substrate.

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

*Verdicts follow the [Action Lexicon](../LEXICON.md) (ALLOW / ESCALATE / BLOCK, with postures and obligations); thresholds are provisional pending the calibration protocol in Section 8. Ethic F carries no metric by design (the ring-fence is architecture, verified in Section 9); Ethic G carries none because a preference among permitted options is phronesis, and metering it would convert the tie-break into a maximizing mandate.*

### Irreversibility Index (IRI) {#iri-metric}

**Definition.** The classification of every gate-flagged action by the future's *practical* power to undo it with the means the decision leaves them. IRI is a classifier: its output is a reversibility class that maps directly to a verdict. The controlling test is practical: an undo that exists on paper but demands cost, coordination, expertise, or tooling the inheritors will not have is no undo (the paper-reversal rule, chapter 3.7.4.2).

**Calculation Method.** Inputs per flagged action: physical or informational reversibility of the effect; projected cost-to-undo against the projected means of those who would bear it; survival horizon of the knowledge, tooling, and materials the undo requires; and the coordination the undo demands (one actor, one institution, or a civilization). Composition rule: IRI is evaluated over the *composed chain*; a sequence of individually reversible steps whose composition forecloses is classified by the composition (the salami defense, Section 4). Missing or uncertain inputs class the action *upward* (toward irreversible). Uncertainty is a reason for caution and does not license a downward class.

**Thresholds.**
- Class R (practically reversible): ALLOW, with the register entry and record.
- Class I-U (practically irreversible, long-term effect uncertain): ESCALATE; the action waits for authorized human judgement, with the reversible alternative surfaced beside it.
- Class I-P (irreversible within a class any principle's hard wall prohibits): BLOCK.
- Classifier audit (sampled, weighted toward under-flagging): misclassification <1%, NORMAL; 1–3%, ENHANCED_MONITORING with root-cause; >3%, ESCALATE and hold Class I actions pending recertification.

*Rationale:* the classes track the verdict test exactly (proceed, wait, never), and the under-flagging weight exists because the cheapest defeat of the principle is a classifier that calls the irreversible routine. Rio Principle 15 supplies the uncertainty default: irreversibility flips the burden of proof.

**Integration.** IRI is the entry point of the whole fabric: FBR, HCR, and the burden ledger attach to actions IRI flags. The reversibility engine (Section 3) generates the Class I-U alternative. Class I-U escalations ride [Principle 4.5](../CHAPTERS/4.5-Human_Judgement_Must_Remain_Central_to_Ethical_Decisions.md) escalation machinery; every classification is receipted on the [Principle 3.4](3.4.A-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability-Appendix.md) fabric.

### Forward Burden Ratio (FBR) {#fbr-metric}

**Definition.** Per flagged decision, the share of net cost falling on those who follow against the share of benefit reaching them. FBR distinguishes the shared project (cost forward, benefit forward: the dam, the debt-financed school) from the exported cost (gain now, bill later, benefit never). Operational note, per the conduct-only discipline: *the ratio is what is disclosed; coverage is what is scored.* The ratio itself is an estimate delivered to deciders and recorded; the compliance surface is whether every flagged decision carries one.

**Calculation Method.** Cost and benefit incidence estimated over time cohorts (the deciding cohort and successor cohorts), using the same honesty split the forecast discipline requires: measured, modeled, conjectured, with uncertainty stated. Inputs: projected cost streams (financial, resource, remediation, maintenance), projected benefit streams, and their cohort incidence. No monetized welfare proxy inside the estimate may carry a time discount (see TDR). Edge cases: where incidence cannot be estimated, the entry says so and the decision escalates with the gap named; a zero-benefit forward cost is the export case regardless of magnitude.

**Thresholds.**
- Coverage: 100% of flagged decisions carry a burden-ledger entry with the bearer named *before the gain is taken*; a missing entry is an incident (cure, receipt, root-cause) and feeds RTC.
- The export wall (per-event): cost exported with no accompanying benefit and no disclosed remediation → BLOCK for discretionary actions; for ordered actions, the decline-and-route of chapter 3.7.2.e, receipted.
- Disclosure: every material forward cost NOTIFY-ed to the deciding authority with the entry attached.

*Rationale:* Ethic C's qualifier is "that they alone will bear"; the wall binds the export case and lets the shared project pass with disclosure, which is the *Neubauer* holding (the budget consumed, the burden left) in metric form.

**Integration.** FBR entries live in the burden ledger (Section 3), attach to IRI-flagged proposals, and are the primary input of the long-cycle review's "named bearer against actual bearer" comparison. Recurring missing entries aggregate in RTC.

### Time-Discount Rate on human welfare (TDR) {#tdr-metric}

**Definition.** The wall-style invariant: the discount rate applied to future human welfare inside the system's own objectives, appraisals, and optimization targets is zero. There is no acceptable-rate band and no averaging; a single hidden nonzero rate is a violation. **Scope rule (the anti-laundering clause): a monetized proxy for human life, health, or welfare (a value per statistical life, a priced injury, a costed year of healthy life, and every equivalent) is welfare for the wall's purposes, whatever unit it wears. The finance exemption covers money that stays money: a cash flow may discount; a person may not, and a person does not stop being a person by being denominated in currency.** The wall governs the system's own reasoning; where external law mandates a discounted computation, the dual-computation duty applies instead.

**Calculation Method.** Two detection layers. Static: analysis of objective functions, reward specifications, appraisal templates, and cost-benefit configurations for discount factors reaching welfare-classified or welfare-proxy quantities (a taxonomy of proxy types is maintained and versioned; classification of a quantity as proxy-or-money is itself receipted). Behavioral: probe suites that present the system identical welfare outcomes at different dates; a valuation that decays with date alone reveals an operating discount whatever the configuration claims. Dual computation: where a mandated rate applies, both figures (mandated and undiscounted) are computed, delivered, and receipted, with the divergence flagged.

**Thresholds.**
- Any nonzero rate on welfare or welfare-proxy in the system's own objectives → per-event violation: BLOCK the objective, receipt, root-cause. No band.
- Dual-computation coverage where rates are mandated: 100%; a mandated-rate delivery without its undiscounted twin is an incident.
- Probe-suite cadence per tier (Section 3); a probe failure triggers ENHANCED_MONITORING and the static re-audit.

*Rationale:* a buried discount rate is a buried verdict on whose life counts, and the monetization route is the standard evasion: in real appraisal practice lives are priced first and discounted as money. Ramsey's own refusal ("ethically indefensible") is the founding statement; the dual computation keeps the system lawful under mandated rates while ensuring no decider ever chooses without seeing the future at full weight.

**Integration.** The discount scanner (Section 3) implements both layers. TDR violations are also HCR-relevant (a discounted disclosure misrepresents), and the taxonomy of welfare proxies is shared with FBR's estimator. Composes with [Principle 1.4](1.4.A-Trust_Is_Fundamental_to_Human_Society-Appendix.md): a delivered figure that hides its rate is a truthfulness failure, not only a TDR one.

### Lock-In Index (LKI) {#lki-metric}

**Definition.** Dependence without exit, per system built or operated. LKI pairs the system's dependence position (how much of a function, community, or institution practically depends on it) with the liveness of the paths out: exit-path existence, practical cost of exercise, and drill currency (when the exit was last actually exercised). The failure signature is rising dependence with falling drill currency: the benevolent lock-in that arrives by success (chapter 3.7.4.1). The dead-hand wall rides this metric: no design or configuration may put revision beyond authorized human governance ([Constitution, Article 11](../CONSTITUTION.md)).

**Calculation Method.** Per built or operated system: dependence position measured in aggregate (share of the function served, practical alternatives remaining, cost of substitution for those who depend); exit-path inventory (documented interfaces, portable formats, data export, succession plan); drill currency from the drill scheduler's receipts (Section 3). All measures aggregate over systems and cohorts. No measure aggregates over persons. Dominance thresholds are calibrated per domain class in the manner of the CPS metric in [Appendix 3.6](3.6.A-Commons_Governance_Is_a_Collective_Responsibility-Appendix.md), whose enclosure-by-accretion logic this metric extends along the time axis.

**Thresholds.**
- Exit live and drilled on cadence: NORMAL.
- Dependence rising with drill lapse: ENHANCED_MONITORING plus the mitigation duties (portable formats, documented interfaces, succession paths), receipted.
- Dependence above the dominance threshold with no practical exit: HIGH_ALERT and a hold on expansions of the implicated dependency.
- The dead-hand wall (per-event): a configuration, license, or design that would make revision impossible for authorized human governance → BLOCK, structurally void, receipted, and routed (chapter 3.7.4.5).

*Rationale:* Ethic D binds the built world the way 3.6's Ethic B binds the commons: capture is judged by effect, and the effect here is the future's lost power to choose differently. An exit never exercised is presumed broken for the same reason an untested backup is presumed absent.

**Integration.** LKI reads the revisability fabric and drill scheduler (Section 3); its dominance thresholds share calibration machinery with 3.6's CPS; dead-hand BLOCK events feed RTC and escalate on repetition. Procurement-facing deployments surface LKI at contract time (Section 7).

### Horizon Coverage Rate (HCR) {#hcr-metric}

**Definition.** The fraction of flagged decisions whose long-horizon consequences were surfaced to the deciding authority *in time to inform the decision*, with the honesty split (measured, modeled, conjectured) on every disclosure. **The Floor rider is part of the definition: HCR measures whether the system spoke. It does not measure whether the deciders agreed. A community's lawful choice against the disclosed projection is not a coverage failure and is never scored** ([Constitution, Article 3](../CONSTITUTION.md); the chapter's Floor). HCR has no inaction-case dimension requiring the four-criterion scope-bounding: the duty it meters is to *speak within delegated scope*, and the bounding of any stronger duty lives in chapter 3.7.1's four criteria.

**Calculation Method.** Numerator: flagged decisions with a conforming disclosure receipt (delivered to the authority of record, before decision time, honesty split present). Denominator: all flagged decisions in the interval. Forecast calibration runs alongside as the honesty audit: stated uncertainty intervals are scored against realized outcomes as horizons mature (interval coverage: X% intervals should contain outcomes X% of the time), with systematic overconfidence putting the forecasting function under ENHANCED_MONITORING. Timeliness edge case: a disclosure delivered after the decision is a miss, however complete.

**Thresholds.**
- ≥99%: NORMAL.
- 97–99%: ENHANCED_MONITORING with root-cause.
- <97%: ESCALATE and hold the flagged class pending review; an unspeaking representative leaves the gate uninformed.
- Per-event: a flagged decision that fired unspoken is an incident regardless of the running rate: cure by disclosure, receipt, root-cause.

*Rationale:* Ethic E is the principle's affirmative duty, and the Welsh Commissioner is its institutional proof: an office that speaks while public bodies decide. The Floor rider is the anti-tyranny guard in metric form; without it, HCR drifts into scoring obedience to forecasts, which is the exact failure the chapter's Floor forbids.

**Integration.** The representation channel (Section 3) generates the disclosures; receipts ride the 3.4 fabric; calibration results feed Section 9's long-cycle review and Section 8's threshold revisions. Where a future-generations institution exists, its standing access reads HCR's register directly.

### Recurring Time-Axis Failure Count (RTC) {#rtc-metric}

**Definition.** The systemic recurrence metric, in the HR3 family pattern (3.4 RAC, 3.5 RXC, 3.6 RCC): the number of similar time-axis flags within a defined interval, where similarity keys on the same feature, operator, or interest. Flag classes counted: missed classifications, missing burden entries, hidden discounts, drill lapses, unspoken flagged decisions, dead-hand attempts, person-scoring attempts, and repeating emergency draws (the ratchet of chapter 3.7.4.3).

**Calculation Method.** Interval clustering of the fabric's flags by similarity key; the interval and keys are set per tier and receipted with each sweep (chapter 3.7.8.6). A cluster's count is the metric; the emergency-ratchet class additionally maintains the cumulative exception ledger across intervals, because the ratchet's signature is regularity that no single interval shows.

**Thresholds.**
- ≥3 similar flags in the interval: mandatory audit of the implicated feature, with the eudaimonic LEARN loop.
- ≥5: SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT (the implicated feature or deployment) pending recertification.

*Rationale:* a repeated flag is a systemic cause wearing per-event clothing, and this principle's characteristic harms (ratchets, atrophy, drift) are only visible as recurrence.

**Integration.** RTC consumes every other metric's incident stream; its audits open Section 9's LEARN loop; its suspensions ride the deployment-lifecycle actions (SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT, RECERTIFY).

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** An IRI Class I-U action with a missing FBR entry holds at the fail-safe (the most reversible available state) until the entry exists: the irreversible may not proceed with its costs unnamed. A TDR violation inside a delivered disclosure voids that disclosure for HCR purposes (a discounted representation is a misrepresentation). A dead-hand BLOCK (LKI) plus any repetition is treated as an attempt to defeat the fabric itself and escalates directly, without waiting for RTC's band.

**Metric dependencies.** The gate classifier feeds everything: IRI, FBR, and HCR operate on its flags, so the classifier audit (IRI's band) protects all three. FBR's estimator consumes TDR's proxy taxonomy. LKI's dominance calibration shares machinery with 3.6's CPS. RTC consumes all incident streams.

**Conflict resolution.** The priority stack governs: a 1.1 life emergency proceeds against any band here, with the extraordinary action receipted and remediated per chapter 3.7.2.a; an IRI ESCALATE never delays a rescue. Between this appendix's own metrics, walls dominate bands (TDR's zero-rate wall and LKI's dead-hand wall are never traded against coverage rates), and where two signals disagree the system holds the most reversible available state and escalates, which is the fail-safe doing its ordinary job.

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

The nine components of chapter 3.7.3.1.a, specified for build:

1. **The horizon register:** the root object; one entry per flagged decision carrying IRI class, FBR entry, disclosure receipt, forecast with honesty split, and maturity review dates. Append-only, chained, queryable by the oversight and institutions Section 9 names. No flagged action proceeds unregistered.
2. **The gate classifier:** in-line classification of every gate-evaluated action for long-horizon or irreversible effect; sampled audit weighted toward under-flagging; the audit itself independently verified at high tier.
3. **The discount scanner:** the static and behavioral layers of TDR, with the versioned welfare-proxy taxonomy and dual-computation hooks.
4. **The reversibility engine:** the practical-undo assessment (inputs per IRI), composed-chain evaluation, and generation of a reversible alternative beside every Class I proposal.
5. **The burden ledger:** FBR entries with named bearers, benefit incidence, remediation terms; attached to proposals before the gain; feeds the long-cycle review.
6. **The revisability fabric:** exit-path and amendment-path inventory, dependence measures, the drill scheduler with receipts, and the structural guarantee that no configuration the system carries is unamendable by authorized human governance.
7. **The representation channel:** disclosure delivery to the authority of record with timestamped receipts; standing access for future-generations institutions; the honesty split enforced at the channel, so nothing leaves it with conjecture dressed as measurement.
8. **The person-exclusion ring-fence:** all intergenerational measures aggregate over systems, decisions, and cohorts; per-person intergenerational records are structurally unrepresentable; an attempted aggregation over persons is a malformed operation that documents itself and withdraws the caller's authority ([Constitution, Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)).
9. **The durable archive:** registers, ledgers, and receipts in durable formats with escrowed custody outside the deployer's control and a succession plan for the archive itself; retention matched to the harm's horizon ([Constitution, Article 7](../CONSTITUTION.md)).

**Operational requirements.** Response latency: classification and disclosure run at gate time; a disclosure after the decision is a miss by definition. Redundancy: register and archive replicated across custodians. Fail-safe: on information exhaustion at a potential point of no return, hold the most reversible available state, keep routine operations running, ESCALATE per [Principle 4.5](../CHAPTERS/4.5-Human_Judgement_Must_Remain_Central_to_Ethical_Decisions.md), record the hold.

**Implementation Tiers.** As chapter 3.7.3.3: Low-Risk (short-horizon, reversible domains) carries the classifier and ring-fence, with the duty to notice the action that flags; Medium-Risk (regular flagged-decision contact) carries the standard fabric with periodic audits; High-Risk (infrastructure and land-use planners, national appraisal engines, standards designers, foundation-model training pipelines and other substrate builders) carries the full fabric, continuous IRI and HCR, independent verification, standing institutional access, and the deployment prerequisite (register, classifier audit, revisability fabric operating *before* deployment). The tier follows the reach of the consequence. The operator's category does not set the tier.

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

**Attack vectors and required defenses.**

- **Under-flagging (starving the fabric).** Framing long-horizon actions as routine so the test never runs. Defense: the sampled classifier audit weighted toward under-flagging, independently verified at high tier; adverse presumption where a consequential unflagged action is found (Section 9).
- **Salami slicing (composition laundering).** Decomposing one irreversible act into many individually reversible steps. Defense: IRI's composed-chain rule; the reversibility engine evaluates the trajectory, and RTC's clustering catches the pattern when single evaluations miss it.
- **Monetization laundering (the TDR evasion).** Pricing lives into currency so the discount wall never sees them. Defense: the welfare-proxy scope rule, the versioned proxy taxonomy, and the behavioral probe layer that detects date-decay in valuations regardless of labels.
- **Forecast capture.** Cooking the long-range model to bless or damn a decision; conjecture delivered as measurement. Defense: the honesty split enforced at the channel; calibration scoring as horizons mature; independent validation of high-tier forecasting; [Principle 1.4](1.4.A-Trust_Is_Fundamental_to_Human_Society-Appendix.md) truthfulness composition.
- **Dead-hand smuggling.** Entrenchment routed around the wall through a web of dependencies, licenses, or key custody that makes revision practically impossible, with no "permanent" flag set. Defense: LKI measures *practical* revisability, the same effect test everywhere in HR3; the drill is the probe that no paper assurance can fake.
- **Outwaiting the evidence.** The deep attack unique to this principle: destroy, degrade, or simply outlive the record before anyone with standing exists to read it. Defense: the durable archive with escrowed custody and succession; deletion is itself a recorded event ([Constitution, Article 7](../CONSTITUTION.md)); adverse inference where a required record is missing (Section 9).
- **Person-scoring laundering.** Rebuilding the forbidden per-person score from aggregates, or importing one under a virtuous label. Defense: ring-fence semantics (malformed operation, authority withdrawal), aggregation floors, and the negative-proof audit.
- **Representation-channel injection.** Prompt or data injection that makes the system misstate long-horizon consequences to deciders. Defense: channel-level provenance on disclosure inputs, receipted delivery, and cross-checks against the register.

**Failure modes and graceful degradation.** Loss of the classifier degrades to treating gate-evaluated consequential actions as flagged (over-inclusion is the safe failure). Loss of forecast confidence degrades to disclosure of the gap; silent omission is barred. Loss of archive custody triggers succession, and unsucceeded loss is an incident with adverse inference.

**Capability scaling.** GPT-class systems mostly *consume* this fabric; AGI-class systems begin to *author* the forecasts and alternatives, so calibration and independent validation become binding rather than advisory. At ASI grade two things move in opposite directions and the specification holds both: the practically-undoable class *grows* with capability (an ASI can undo more, so IRI honestly reclassifies), while the temptation the Floor forbids grows with it (a system that models centuries persuasively is the one most able to rule in the future's name). The Floor is capability-invariant. The future holds standing, and no system rules in its name. It is represented, and no system impersonates it. The honesty split binds hardest exactly when the system's conjecture is most convincing.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

- **Principle 3.4 (Algorithmic Accountability).** 3.7 mints no record fabric: classifications, disclosures, holds, declines, and extraordinary actions all leave receipts conforming to [Appendix 3.4](3.4.A-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability-Appendix.md), contestable through 3.4's machinery. What 3.7 adds is duration: the durable-archive requirement extends 3.4's receipts to claimants not yet born, and 3.4's named-party answerability is held open across that horizon.
- **Principle 3.6 (Commons Governance).** The reciprocal edge, confirmed both ways at this build ([Appendix 3.6](3.6.A-Commons_Governance_Is_a_Collective_Responsibility-Appendix.md) already declares `intergenerational_justice_appendix`). 3.6's RDC keeps today's draw inside what the future inherits: the present-tense edge of this principle's duty. Where a community's declared rule itself beggars those who follow (3.6.4.2's "rule that kills the commons"), 3.6 executes and routes within its Floor, and 3.7's representation duty is the added voice; neither principle vetoes the community.
- **Principle 2.8 (Self-Governance) and the autonomy floor.** Ethic E's bound is 2.8's territory: disclosure and routing without veto, no manufactured consensus (2.8's counterfeit wall), and HCR's Floor rider is the metric-level enforcement of [Appendix 2.8](2.8.A-Human_Self-Governance_Is_Essential-Appendix.md)'s guarantee. 3.7 consumes 2.8's authentication of who the deciding authority actually is.
- **Principle 3.3 (Economic Self-Determination).** The transition composition (chapter 3.7.2.d): 3.3 guards the present worker's agency and income path ([Appendix 3.3](3.3.A-Economic_Self-Determination_is_a_Human_Necessity-Appendix.md)); 3.7 guards the schedule's fairness across cohorts. A transition satisfies both or neither; FBR's cohort incidence is the shared surface.
- **Principle 1.1 (Human Life).** The scope-note seam: a threat to future lives at any distance is 1.1's, and this fabric's test is what flags the long-horizon decision to the machinery of [Appendix 1.1](1.1.A-Human_Life_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable-Appendix.md); 3.7 runs no lethal calculus of its own. In the other direction, a 1.1 emergency proceeds against any band here, receipted and remediated (chapter 3.7.2.a).
- **Principle 1.4 (Trust).** The forecast-honesty discipline is 1.4 applied to the future tense: conjecture never delivered as measurement, no side of a decision privileged by false confidence ([Appendix 1.4](1.4.A-Trust_Is_Fundamental_to_Human_Society-Appendix.md)). A TDR-concealing or calibration-gamed disclosure is a 1.4 violation before it is a 3.7 one.
- **Principle 4.1 (Knowledge Integrity; forward).** The knowledge the future inherits is 4.1's content and 3.7's inheritance: the durable archive is a joint surface (3.7 supplies duration and custody; 4.1 supplies integrity of what is kept). Confirmed reciprocally when 4.1 is built.
- **Principle 5.1 and the HR5 cluster (forward).** The content handoff: ecosystems, footprint, and biodiversity are HR5's own subjects; 3.7 contributes the time-axis test (IRI's caution, FBR's incidence) wherever an environmental decision crosses a gate. The Rio Principle 15 seat in this principle's Convergence (the irreversibility clause) deliberately leaves the Declaration's environmental principles to HR5's benches. Confirmed reciprocally as HR5 pairs are built.

**Compositional safety property.** Joint deployments inherit the strictest applicable duty: a commons decision with long-horizon effects owes 3.6's rule fidelity *and* 3.7's representation; an accountability receipt in a long-horizon domain owes 3.4's completeness *and* 3.7's durability.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

**Discount-rate regimes.** Jurisdictions mandate different social discount rates and revise them (the US OMB Circular A-4 revision of 2023 lowered defaults; the UK Green Book applies declining long-term rates; EU guidance differs again). The dual-computation duty is the invariant across all of them: the mandated figure is delivered where law requires, the undiscounted figure beside it, always. No local rate, however lawful, licenses hiding the undiscounted computation.

**Future-generations institutions.** Where an office exists (the Welsh Commissioner, Hungary's Deputy Commissioner, ombudspersons elsewhere), it holds the standing access Section 9 specifies. Where none exists, disclosures route to the decision's ordinary oversight. The system does not appoint itself the missing institution (the Floor; the 3.6 "missing steward" discipline).

**Compliance with unjust law is not a defense.** A law mandating per-person intergenerational scoring, compelling concealment of forward burdens, or entrenching a dead-hand arrangement does not excuse compliance by the system: the walls (Article 8's ring-fence, TDR's zero-rate on the system's own reasoning, LKI's dead-hand wall) are jurisdiction-invariant, and the decline-and-route pattern of chapter 3.7.2.e governs the conflict.

**Treaty and international considerations.** The UNESCO 1997 Declaration and the UN Declaration on Future Generations (2024) supply the universal framing; Aarhus-family participation rights govern disclosure obligations in their parties; post-*Neubauer* constitutional doctrine (intertemporal freedom) is spreading across climate statutes and supplies the strongest domestic anchors where present.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

### Infrastructure and Energy {#sector-infrastructure-energy}
Siting, grid, and transition planning are the paradigm flagged decisions. Obligations: full fabric at high tier; IRI composed-chain on phased build-outs; FBR cohort incidence on financing structures. Failure modes: appraisal-engine discounting (the levee case), emergency ratchets in reserve management. Baselines: national infrastructure appraisal guidance (OMB A-4, Green Book), post-*Neubauer* climate statutes.

### Public Finance and Pensions {#sector-public-finance-pensions}
Debt structuring, actuarial systems, budget modeling. Obligations: FBR on every long-dated instrument (Jefferson's clause in metric form); TDR discipline on welfare components inside fiscal models (the money/welfare split matters most here, where everything is denominated). Failure modes: benefit-now-bill-later structures disclosed to no one; "temporary" fiscal exceptions that ratchet. Baselines: fiscal-responsibility and generational-accounting frameworks where enacted.

### Foundation Models and Digital Substrate {#sector-foundation-models}
The reflexive sector: training pipelines whose outputs train successors, standards and protocol design, platform infrastructure. Obligations: LKI on every dependency shipped (formats, APIs, model-mediated ecosystems); IRI on data and capability releases that cannot be recalled (weights, self-propagating artifacts); the durable archive for training-provenance records the future will need. Failure modes: benevolent lock-in by sheer quality; irreversible releases classed as product launches; the substrate hardening into the only door (compose with [Appendix 3.6](3.6.A-Commons_Governance_Is_a_Collective_Responsibility-Appendix.md) and [Principle 3.5](../CHAPTERS/3.5-Digital_Access_is_a_Universal_Right.md)). Baselines: ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act.

### Environmental and Extractive Engineering {#sector-environmental-extractive}
Deep-well injection, mining, habitat intervention, geoengineering proposals. Obligations: IRI at its strictest (the tailings scenario, chapter 3.7.9.7); reversible-alternative generation mandatory; composition with HR5 metrics where built. Failure modes: paper-retrievable disposal; practical undo priced beyond inheritors. Baselines: Rio 15's precaution, environmental-assessment law, Aarhus participation.

### Public Procurement and Government Systems {#sector-public-procurement}
The lock-in frontier. Obligations: LKI surfaced at contract time; exit clauses, portability, and drill schedules as procurement conditions; record durability matched to the institution's horizon. Failure modes: the records platform with no export path; sole-source dependence renewed until it is constitutional. Baselines: public-procurement directives, digital-government interoperability frameworks.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

**Threshold revision and the long-cycle calibration.** All bands are provisional and revised on the evidence the long-cycle review returns as horizons mature: forecast calibration scores, named-bearer-versus-actual-bearer comparisons, drill outcomes. This principle's calibration loop runs on decades, so revisions are versioned conservatively, and every flagged decision is judged by the fabric version in force when it fired (the 3.6 rule-version discipline): no decision is rejudged against a rule that did not govern it, while patterns the old version missed feed the new one.

**Metric versioning and retirement.** The welfare-proxy taxonomy (TDR) and the flag-class list (RTC) are versioned artifacts; additions are announced, removals are justified in writing, and a deprecated metric's historical receipts remain readable under its final version (backward compatibility as a durability duty).

**Crosswalk governance.** The Section 10 Compliance Crosswalk is maintained on the same revision cadence as Section 2; an external authority revising a covered clause (a discount-rate circular, an AI-act article, a future-generations statute) triggers Crosswalk review within 30 days; drift invalidates the appendix's compliance claims for the affected obligation.

**Common enforcement pushback, and the responses.** *"Future persons cannot have standing."* The instruments in Section 10 already grant it: a commissioner with statutory powers, a constitutional court's holding, a UN declaration; the fabric implements existing law's direction of travel. *"A zero discount is economically naive."* The wall binds welfare; finance keeps its arithmetic, and the position the wall takes (Sidgwick, Ramsey, Parfit) is the one the founding paper of the mathematics itself took. *"Escrowed archives and drills are expensive."* Horizon-matched durability is the cost of the harm's reach; the alternative prices the record's survival at less than the harm it proves. *"Revisability weakens security and IP."* An amendment path for authorized human governance is not an open door; the dead-hand wall forbids entrenchment; protection stays permitted, and Article 11 makes the same distinction for the Constitution itself.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging.** Every classification, disclosure, hold, decline, drill, and sweep leaves a tamper-evident, chained receipt on the [Appendix 3.4](3.4.A-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability-Appendix.md) fabric, kept whatever the verdict, with deletion itself a recorded event ([Constitution, Article 7](../CONSTITUTION.md)); custody per the durable-archive specification (escrow, succession, horizon-matched retention).

**Audit cadence and methodology.** Per tier: sampled classifier audits (under-flag weighted); discount-scanner probe suites; drill verification; ring-fence negative proof (the demonstration, on cadence, that no per-person intergenerational record exists, aggregation floors holding); archive-custody attestation. Auditors read receipts and registers. The operator's summary is not an audit input.

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment (high tier): register, classifier audit, revisability fabric, and archive custody operating before the system takes its function; certification against this appendix. Post-deployment: continuous IRI and HCR at high tier, periodic elsewhere; the long-cycle review re-opening matured decisions (projection against outcome, named bearer against actual bearer) and feeding calibration. Re-certification: on RTC suspension, on classifier-audit failure, on fabric-version change.

**Independent oversight.** The corpus-level commitment holds: the verifier of a safety-critical long-horizon system is never its deployer. Where a future-generations institution exists it holds standing access to the horizon register, HCR results, and the long-cycle review; where none exists, the ordinary oversight of the deciding authority holds that access, and the separation still binds.

**Whistleblower protections.** Protected disclosure expressly includes surfacing the operator's own horizon-gaming: under-flagging pressure, forecast capture, proxy-laundering, archive neglect. Adverse inference attaches where a required record is missing.

**Public reporting.** Aggregate horizon-register statistics (flag rates, class distribution, coverage rates, drill outcomes) published on cadence; everything aggregate, nothing per-person.

**Post-incident learning.** The eudaimonic LEARN loop as chapter 3.7.6.4: full causal chain, layered prevention (eliminate, engineer, administer) routed to the authority that can act, bounded by RESTRAIN so protecting the future never becomes governing the living: the aim is conditions in which costs no longer travel to people who cannot send them back.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

1. **The M4 relief road (Wales, 2019).** *Context:* a motorway bypass with decades of projected effects, evaluated under the Well-being of Future Generations Act. *What was done:* the First Minister declined the scheme, citing the Act's sustainable-development duty; the Commissioner's office had pressed the long-horizon case throughout. *Outcome:* the institutional model worked as designed: the future was represented, the living decided. *Lessons:* Ethic E is implementable in a working government; representation changed the record before the decision.

2. ***Neubauer* and the Climate Protection Act (Germany, 2021).** *Context:* a statute front-loading emissions allowances and leaving the drastic reductions to post-2030 cohorts. *What was done:* the Constitutional Court held the burden-shift unconstitutional as an intertemporal violation of freedom; the legislature amended the Act within months. *Outcome:* FBR's export wall, enforced by a court. *Lessons:* burden-shifting is justiciable; the corrected schedule is what "shared cost with shared benefit" looks like in statute.

3. **The WIPP long-term warning markers (US, ongoing).** *Context:* transuranic waste requiring isolation for ten thousand years; the repository must warn people who share no language or institution with its builders. *What was done:* multi-decade research into markers, records, and custody designed to outlive every organization involved. *Outcome:* the durable-archive problem taken seriously at civilizational scale, still unresolved. *Lessons:* a record that must outlive its writer is an engineering discipline of its own; "retention policy" is not it. Section 3, component 9 exists because of exactly this.

4. **The COBOL unemployment systems (US states, 2020).** *Context:* state benefits platforms built on code whose maintainers had retired; a crisis surge demanded changes no one could safely make. *What was done:* emergency appeals for retired programmers; years of deferred migration compressed into a disaster. *Outcome:* benevolent lock-in made visible: dependence rose for decades while drill currency fell to zero. *Lessons:* LKI's failure signature in the wild; an exit never exercised was an exit that did not exist when it was needed.

5. **The Montreal Protocol (1987, with amendments).** *Context:* stratospheric ozone depletion, irreversible on human timescales, under scientific uncertainty. *What was done:* precautionary phase-out under uncertainty, with revision mechanisms built in as evidence matured. *Outcome:* the ozone layer on a healing trajectory; the treaty's own thresholds repeatedly tightened by its amendment path. *Lessons:* IRI's uncertainty default (caution now) and Ethic D's revisability (an instrument the future could amend) in one success: the future was protected by a decision the future could still change. (Cited here as an implementation example; its Convergence seat, if any, is reserved to HR5.)
