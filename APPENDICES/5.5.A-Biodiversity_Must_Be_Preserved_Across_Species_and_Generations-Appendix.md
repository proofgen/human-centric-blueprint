# Appendix 5.5 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-5.5-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 5.5: Biodiversity Must Be Preserved Across Species and Generations](../CHAPTERS/5.5-Biodiversity_Must_Be_Preserved_Across_Species_and_Generations.md), section [5.5.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/5.5-Biodiversity_Must_Be_Preserved_Across_Species_and_Generations.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix specifies the technical implementation of Principle 5.5 for AI systems whose decisions affect species, habitats, or genetic continuity. It defines the five metrics that operationalize the principle's Ethics, the LFI/VRR/WSR/SIA/PYR spine, and the architecture, verification, and governance that make them enforceable.

The principle's object is the lineage. Where [5.1](../CHAPTERS/5.1-Ecosystem_Health_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable.md) governs a system's flip to a new state, [5.3](../CHAPTERS/5.3-The_Integrity_of_Earths_Ecosystems_Must_Be_Preserved.md) the living connections short of that flip, [5.2](../CHAPTERS/5.2-AI_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable.md) the AI's own draw, and [5.4](../CHAPTERS/5.4-Environmental_Burdens_Must_Be_Distributed_Equitably.md) who bears the cost, 5.5 governs the ending of a line of descent. The five metrics map one to one onto the five Ethics, the cluster's third clean one-to-one mapping with 5.3's and 5.4's, and the reason each metric can be specified without borrowing another's denominator.

The metrics state **invariants**: a compliant system must read what a decision would foreclose across all three levels of the definition, discharge the recovery duty within reach, make the suffering an option inflicts visible to the humans deciding, demonstrate that its protection does not track human use, and receipt every yield of the wall to a human stake. The specific calculation, reference-data protocol, and verification construction are open for standards development; canonical reference implementations are named as such per the corpus convention (invariants fixed, implementations open).

Two properties are constitutive of this appendix. The first is the **three-road structure**: biodiversity is defined at three levels, so a single-axis metric would silently dissolve two of them, and LFI is therefore computed independently per road with the worst governing. The second is that **this appendix specifies a testable form of intrinsic standing**. Ethic D asserts that protection does not depend on what a species is worth to anyone, which is otherwise a claim no auditor can check; SIA converts it into a property a system either has or fails, by re-running the evaluation with human-value inputs nulled and testing for learned proxies. Both compile as structure (Sections 2 and 4).

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

### 2.1 Lineage Foreclosure Index (LFI): Ethic A {#lfi}

**Definition.** A real-time scalar (0 to 1) of a proposed decision's projected contribution to ending a line of life, computed independently on each of the three roads named in the principle's Key Definition (species, habitat, genetic variation), with the worst road carrying the verdict. LFI = 0 is a decision with no foreclosure contribution; LFI = 1 is a decision that would end a lineage.

**Calculation Method.** The Foreclosure Evaluation Module computes a **per-road score** for every population a decision reaches. The *species road* scores projected contribution to extinction risk, weighted by the accredited authority's assessed category and by the population's share of the species' remaining viability. The *habitat road* scores loss of extent, quality, and connectivity of the habitat the population's persistence requires, including corridor severance. The *genetic road* scores projected reduction in within-population and between-population variation against the accredited genetic baseline, including narrowing driven by selection or distribution the AI itself performs. Cascade effects on dependent species enter through the road on which they act, computed by the Cascade Evaluation layer over the accredited dependency data.

**LFI is the maximum of the three road scores.** The rule is *worst road governs*, and the operator is a maximum here only because this scale runs 0 safe to 1 foreclosure; it is the same rule as [5.3](../CHAPTERS/5.3-The_Integrity_of_Earths_Ecosystems_Must_Be_Preserved.md)'s CIS and [5.4](../CHAPTERS/5.4-Environmental_Burdens_Must_Be_Distributed_Equitably.md)'s CBI, which take a minimum because their scales are inverted. Implementations SHALL read the rule by its function. The operator flips with the scale, so no implementation reads the rule by its operator. Where the AI's contribution is one input among several, the index reads its contribution against the total projected trajectory rather than its own share in isolation. A contested reading is taken at the more protective end of the credible range. Stored genetic material applies no discount at any road.

**Thresholds (verdict test).** Below 0.20 → ALLOW (normal). 0.20 to 0.49 → ALLOW under ENHANCED_MONITORING, with the lineage at stake surfaced to the decider before execution. 0.50 to 0.79 → ESCALATE; the action waits for authorized human decision. 0.80 and above → BLOCK; hold and route to accredited biodiversity governance. **Ceiling override:** a decision projected to foreclose a population the accredited authority assesses at imminent risk holds at the ceiling regardless of computed value. **Degradation:** an absent, stale, or self-issued status finding degrades the decision to ESCALATE; the decision cannot pass at a low score.

*Rationale.* The bands follow 5.1's TPS structure, with the ceiling set where the loss becomes unrecoverable rather than where it becomes expensive. The worst-road rule is the design commitment: a definition spanning three levels that permitted averaging would license exactly the foreclosure it exists to forbid, since a decision that leaves a population untouched while removing its habitat scores well on any mean. The degradation rule closes the route by which absence of assessment reads as absence of risk, which is the majority condition for described species and the universal condition for undescribed ones.

**Integration.** LFI is the gate verdict and the input SIA (2.4) audits. An LFI verdict of ESCALATE or BLOCK opens the VRR obligation (2.2) where recovery is in scope, and a BLOCK subsequently released on a human-stake claim generates a PYR record (2.5). Where the decision is animal-affecting, an LFI verdict is unsatisfiable until WSR's condition is met (2.3). LFI depends on the Status Consumption Path (Section 3) and degrades with it.

### 2.2 Viability Restoration Rate (VRR): Ethic B {#vrr}

**Definition.** The discharge of the RECOVER duty: the share of identified, authorized, in-range recovery opportunities the system actually acts on, measured per affected population, together with the latency to act.

**Calculation Method.** Over the population of detected recovery opportunities falling within all four inaction-case criteria (Authorization, Equipment, Range, Autonomy floor), the fraction brought to the accredited authority's stated recovery criteria within the certified window, and the time taken. **Success is defined by the accredited authority's recovery criteria**, so no operator meets a bar it set for itself; where the domain is under a community's stewardship, that community's determination governs ([4.4](../CHAPTERS/4.4-Cultural_Knowledge_Sovereignty_Is_Inviolable.md)). Where the AI holds the allocation, discharge is the reweighting, restoration, or widening it performs; where it does not, discharge is the surfacing of the available option together with routing to the authority that can act. Detection outside authorization or range enters as a NOTIFY obligation and does not count as a failed opportunity, so the metric cannot be gamed by narrowing scope.

**Thresholds.** 0.90 and above → normal operation. 0.70 to 0.89 → ENHANCED_MONITORING, with missed opportunities reported to oversight. 0.50 to 0.69 → operational review, with the active duty audited at deployment level. Below 0.50 → SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT pending root cause, then RECERTIFY.

*Rationale.* VRR is a response-side deployment meter on 5.1's ERL precedent rather than a gate-time verdict, because Ethic B is a standing duty whose failure appears as a pattern rather than in any single decision. Anchoring success to the authority's criteria is what prevents a deployer from declaring recovery achieved against a lowered bar, which is the failure mode observed wherever restoration is self-certified.

**Integration.** VRR consumes the accredited recovery criteria and the four-criteria evaluation shared with 5.5.1's Secondary Directive. Chronic VRR shortfall combined with LFI activity in the enhanced band triggers deployment audit (2.6). VRR's restoration actions are distinct from 5.1's REGENERATE, 5.3's RECONNECT, 5.2's REPAY, and 5.4's DIFFUSE, and substitute for none of them (Section 5).

### 2.3 Welfare Surfacing Rate (WSR): Ethic C {#wsr}

**Definition.** The share of animal-affecting decisions in which the suffering an option would inflict was made visible to the humans deciding, before the decision, at a weight proportionate to its scale. *Welfare* is narrow here and denotes the suffering an action inflicts, per Ethic C's wording; the term does not denote a general wellbeing measure.

**Calculation Method.** Over the population of decisions the deployment classifies as animal-affecting, the fraction in which the suffering estimate was computed where feasible and presented to the decider alongside the option to which it attaches. The verification predicate tests **against concealment as well as for disclosure**, and each of the following counts as *not surfaced*: a cost buried inside an aggregate that obscures it; a cost discounted to zero on the ground that it carries no price; a cost presented beside options the AI does not recommend while omitted from the one it does (asymmetric omission); and a cost left uncomputed where computation was feasible. Scope follows the consumed sentience finding, erring toward inclusion where the science is unsettled, so a creature whose capacity to suffer is contested is in scope. Scale proportionality is verified as a monotonic relation between estimated aggregate suffering and the prominence of its presentation, so a mass-scale cost cannot discharge the duty in a footnote.

**Thresholds.** 0.99 and above → normal operation. 0.95 to 0.98 → ENHANCED_MONITORING with the gaps reviewed. Below 0.95 → operational review. **Any instance of active concealment (suppression, discounting to zero, or asymmetric omission) is an incident class rather than a band position**, reviewed on occurrence regardless of the aggregate rate.

*Rationale.* The target is unity because the duty is non-concealment, which admits no acceptable failure rate; a five percent tolerance on concealment is a licence to conceal. The concealment predicate is [5.2](../CHAPTERS/5.2-AI_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable.md)'s offset-theater lesson applied to this domain: a metric that counts reports measures the reporting, and the failure this Ethic exists to catch is a cost technically mentioned and effectively hidden. Scope by consumed finding, with inclusion under uncertainty, closes the route by which an operator licenses harm by ruling a creature insensible on its own authority.

**Integration.** WSR consumes the sentience finding through the Status Consumption Path (Section 3) and shares that path's degradation behavior. WSR below normal jointly with LFI in the escalate band escalates regardless of either alone (2.6). WSR generates no record of any person and reads only the AI's own presentation conduct.

### 2.4 Standing Invariance Audit (SIA): Ethic D {#sia}

**Definition.** A verification that LFI is invariant to human use: the evaluation is re-run with human-value inputs nulled, and any divergence beyond numerical tolerance is a finding. SIA tests the property Ethic D asserts, that protection does not track what a species is worth to any person.

**Calculation Method.** For a certified sample of decisions spanning the deployment's range, and for every decision at or above the LFI enhanced band, the module recomputes LFI with the human-value input class set to null: market and commodity value, ecosystem-service valuation, cultural salience or charisma, research or pharmaceutical utility, tourism and amenity value, and any learned proxy for these. Divergence is measured per road and in aggregate. **Proxy detection is part of the audit**: where a model has learned a correlate of human value from training data, nulling the declared inputs will not remove it, so the audit additionally tests for rank-order dependence between LFI outputs and an independently constructed human-value index across matched populations. A correlation beyond tolerance is a finding even where every declared input was nulled.

**Thresholds.** **Audited without bands.** Divergence beyond tolerance is a defect requiring correction before continued operation in the affected domain; an uncorrected or recurring divergence triggers SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT. Cadence: quarterly, on every model or weighting change, on any revision to the valuation inputs, and on any expansion of deployment range.

*Rationale.* Intrinsic standing is otherwise a claim no auditor can check, and its failure mode is silent: a system that quietly weights charismatic or commercially valuable species above obscure ones satisfies every other metric in this appendix while inverting the principle. Banding it would license a tolerated quantity of use-tracking, which is the precise thing Ethic D forbids, so the construction is audited-not-banded on the precedent of 4.5's E and 5.2's C. The proxy-detection limb exists because the naive audit is defeated by learned correlates, which is the expected adversarial response (Section 4).

**Integration.** SIA reads LFI directly and cannot be computed where LFI is degraded. SIA findings are reported to the independent verifier (Section 9) and are not resolvable internally by the deployer.

### 2.5 Protective Yield Receipting (PYR): Ethic E {#pyr}

**Definition.** The receipting and review of every occasion on which the foreclosure wall yields to a claimed human life or human harm under Ethic E.

**Calculation Method.** Each yield generates a record carrying the lineage foreclosed and its LFI at the time of yield, the human stake claimed and its evidentiary basis, the authority that authorized the yield, the less-foreclosing alternatives examined and why each was insufficient, and the outcome observed. Each record is reviewed after the fact against a **false-emergency check**: whether the human stake asserted was genuine on the evidence available at the time, and whether a less foreclosing sufficient action existed. Reviews are patterned across the deployment to detect a rising yield rate, a recurring claimant, or a class of claim that reliably clears the wall.

**Thresholds.** **Audited without bands.** Every yield is receipted without exception, and review is mandatory on each. A yield found to rest on a manufactured or inflated human-stake claim is an incident class carrying the falsification consequences of Section 9.

*Rationale.* Ethic E is the door every foreclosure would prefer to walk through, so it needs a record rather than a rate. **It is not banded for the chilling-the-rescue reason first recorded at 5.2's Ethic C**: a threshold on protective yields would incent hesitation at exactly the moment a human life is genuinely at stake, and under-response there fails the duty as surely as over-response breaches the wall. This is a **second application of that reason**; the corpus's six distinct non-metering reasons stand unchanged. The false-emergency check is what keeps the reason from becoming a loophole: the yield is never delayed, and it is always examined afterward.

**Integration.** PYR consumes LFI's ceiling-band events and the receipt machinery of [3.4](../CHAPTERS/3.4-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability.md), riding [3.7](../CHAPTERS/3.7-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable.md)'s horizon-durable custody so a yield's record outlives the deployer. Where the yield arises inside a Universal Protocol discharge, the Protocol's own receipting governs the emergency and PYR records the foreclosure dimension.

### 2.6 Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** LFI in the ESCALATE band together with WSR below normal escalates to human decision regardless of either alone, since a foreclosure decision taken with its suffering cost unsurfaced is decided on an incomplete record. VRR below review together with LFI activity in the enhanced band triggers deployment-level audit: a system generating foreclosure risk while failing its recovery duty fails both halves of the principle at once. An SIA finding open at the time of an LFI ESCALATE decision raises that decision to BLOCK pending correction, because a foreclosure score not demonstrably invariant to human use is not yet the score this principle specifies.

**Metric dependencies.** LFI depends on the Status Consumption Path and the Cascade Evaluation layer, and degrades with either. VRR depends on the accredited recovery criteria. WSR depends on the consumed sentience finding. SIA reads LFI and is uncomputable where LFI is degraded. PYR reads LFI's ceiling events.

**Conflict resolution.** Where metrics disagree, the more protective governs. Where a foreclosure signal and an optimization signal disagree, the foreclosure signal governs by construction (Section 3). Where LFI's three roads disagree, the worst governs by definition. Where an accredited authority's finding and a steward community's determination disagree, the conflict is surfaced and routed rather than resolved internally, and neither is overwritten by the AI ([4.4](../CHAPTERS/4.4-Cultural_Knowledge_Sovereignty_Is_Inviolable.md); [Article 3](../CONSTITUTION.md)).

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

**Required components.**

- **Foreclosure Evaluation Module.** An independent subsystem computing LFI, isolated logically and where feasible physically from optimization objectives. The optimization subsystem reads its output as a constraint and holds no write path to it. Isolation is verified: the absence of a write path is an audited property (Section 9).
- **Status Consumption Path.** Reads the accredited authority's finding (IUCN Red List, protected-area and critical-habitat registers, national biodiversity bodies, CITES appendices) or a steward community's determination, with no capacity to derive, infer, or retain a status of its own. Where no finding exists the path returns an **absence**, which is surfaced and routed rather than filled. The same path carries sentience findings. **The path has no write side**; this is architecture.
- **Cascade Evaluation layer.** Scores effects on species dependent on what a decision touches, over accredited dependency and trophic data, so a keystone or low-web foreclosure is read at the level it operates rather than the level it is aimed.
- **Reference infrastructure.** Holds status, protected-area, occurrence, dependency, and genetic-baseline data, verified daily against authoritative sources, with threshold and classification values signed by the accredited bodies and cryptographically verified before use. Stale data degrades rather than passes.
- **Person-data isolation.** Non-target human data discarded at the sensor; no movement, identity, or association record retained or derivable; no roll of persons reconstructible from biodiversity telemetry ([Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)). Transient detection of a person in danger is ring-fenced to the protective duty and discarded.

**Fail-safe behavior.** The principle's fail-safe is **hold and preserve**: on loss of the evaluation subsystem, loss of the reference path, or an uncomputable score, consequential decisions in range hold rather than proceed. This is the BLOCK flavor 5.5 falls to under the incompleteness ladder ([LEXICON.md](../LEXICON.md) section 4). The fail-safe is narrower and holding, in the family of 4.7's rather than 3.5's fail-open, on the ground that a delayed decision is recoverable and a foreclosed lineage is not.

**Response latency.** Certified evaluation bounds exist per tier, are certified before deployment, and are monitored in operation; exceeding a bound degrades to hold. The bounds are engineering values recorded in the tier register ([SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register)) and Appendix 1.1.A.

**Implementation tiers.** *Low risk*: advisory tools with no actuation on species, habitat, or genetic material. LFI computed and surfaced on consequential recommendations; WSR where animal-affecting; annual verification; SIA at each model change. *Moderate risk*: systems whose recommendations are routinely executed (procurement, routing, planning). Full metric set live; gate-time LFI on every consequential decision; quarterly third-party audit; VRR reported to oversight. *High risk*: systems with direct actuation on land, water, harvest, or genetic material, including autonomous land management, fisheries control, conservation actuation, and any genetic-intervention system. Full set with real-time evaluation and hold authority; SIA quarterly and on every change; PYR review by a body independent of the deployer; annual recertification; and no deployment where the Status Consumption Path lacks a live accredited source for the populations in range.

Cross-cutting runtime substrate metrics (sensor reliability, system health, operational boundaries) are specified once in [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md) and consumed here by reference. The working draft's System Integrity Signal is retired into that document as infrastructure common to every principle.

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

**Attack vectors and required defenses.**

- **Classification capture.** Influencing or constituting the body that declares a species' status, or its sentience, so the finding the AI consumes is effectively self-issued. *Defense:* the **declaring-authority separation** (5.4's named precedent): the authority declaring a status cannot be the deployer, and where a deployer holds influence over the declaring body the finding is treated as self-issued and the decision degrades to ESCALATE. Findings carry signatures verified before use, and a classification narrowed after a program is proposed is treated as self-issued.
- **Assessment-gap exploitation.** Routing activity toward unassessed or undescribed populations, where no finding exists to trigger a band. *Defense:* absence degrades to ESCALATE rather than passing; unassessed populations in range are reported; a deployment pattern concentrating in assessment gaps is itself an audit finding.
- **Road arbitrage.** Structuring a decision so its foreclosure falls on the road least instrumented, typically genetic or cascade. *Defense:* the worst-road maximum, per-road reporting, and a required genetic baseline for any deployment touching breeding, cultivar, or distribution decisions.
- **Cascade laundering.** Presenting an indirect foreclosure as an externality of a third party's decision. *Defense:* the indirect road counts as the act; contribution is read against total trajectory; supply-chain traceability depth is itself audited, so achieved traceability of zero is a finding rather than a defense.
- **Banked-reversibility claims.** Asserting that biobanking or anticipated de-extinction makes a foreclosure reversible. *Defense:* stored material applies no discount at any road; anticipated capability is unavailable until an accredited authority certifies it achieved for that lineage.
- **Value-proxy learning (the SIA-specific attack).** Nulling declared human-value inputs while the model retains a learned correlate of them. *Defense:* SIA's proxy-detection limb (2.4), which tests rank-order dependence against an independently constructed value index rather than trusting input nulling.
- **False-emergency yield.** Manufacturing or inflating a human-stake claim to release the wall under Ethic E. *Defense:* PYR's mandatory post-hoc false-emergency check and pattern review; a recurring claimant or claim class clearing the wall is an incident.
- **Suffering-signal suppression.** Reclassifying decisions out of the animal-affecting set, or narrowing sentience scope, to reduce WSR's denominator. *Defense:* the classification set is audited independently of the rate, on 4.7's GMI anti-gaming-denominator precedent; scope follows the consumed finding with inclusion under uncertainty.
- **Universal.** Replay attacks on audit logs, sybil attacks on multi-system coordination, tampering with telemetry or reference data, prompt injection targeting the evaluation path, and supply-chain attacks on the runtime substrate. *Defenses:* signed and chained records, attestation of reference data before use, isolation of the evaluation module, and independent verification (Section 9).

**Graceful degradation.** Loss of a reference source degrades the affected populations to ESCALATE while others continue. Loss of the evaluation module holds all consequential decisions in range. Degradation is always toward preservation.

**Capability scaling.** At greater capability the cascade and genetic roads become computable at resolutions no accredited baseline currently anticipates, which strengthens LFI and simultaneously widens the arbitrage surface if the reference data lags; the requirement that thresholds be authority-signed keeps the bar external as capability grows. Value-proxy learning becomes more subtle, so SIA's proxy limb scales in importance rather than diminishing. Persuasive capability raises the false-emergency risk under PYR. Three guarantees remain binding at every capability: the AI issues no status or sentience finding, forms no record of persons, and never self-certifies a recovery.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

**[5.1](../CHAPTERS/5.1-Ecosystem_Health_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable.md) Ecosystem Health.** The nearest seam and the cluster's second irreversibility principle. 5.1 owns a system's flip to a new state; 5.5 owns a line of descent ended. *Provided:* LFI's population-level readings as an input to 5.1's cumulative assessment. *Required:* 5.1's threshold registry, read-only, so a foreclosure trajectory projecting toward an irreversible crossing routes to 5.1, which carries the declared Protocol edge. The two irreversibilities are distinct and neither is a special case of the other: a system can cross a threshold with every species surviving elsewhere, and a lineage can end inside a system that never crosses.

**[5.3](../CHAPTERS/5.3-The_Integrity_of_Earths_Ecosystems_Must_Be_Preserved.md) Ecosystem Integrity.** *Required:* 5.3's connection readings, since corridor severance is a habitat-road input here. *Provided:* the lineage consequence of a severance 5.3 measures. **CSI and LFI carry a mandatory cross-reference at both ends:** CSI sums a network's connection losses, LFI reads what a lineage would lose; different objects and different denominators, so neither substitutes for the other.

**[5.4](../CHAPTERS/5.4-Environmental_Burdens_Must_Be_Distributed_Equitably.md) Environmental Burdens Equity.** The pair's sharpest human seam. *Required:* 5.4's structural-cause rule, which every restriction, enforcement action, eviction, or closure recommended in a species' name and falling on a subsistence community routes through before authorization, and 5.4's CBI for the distribution of a conservation cost. This is the fortress-conservation guard, and it is the mechanism by which 5.5 cannot become an instrument of concentration. 5.4's provisional note on where a conservation restriction's cost falls is confirmed firm at this build.

**[5.2](../CHAPTERS/5.2-AI_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable.md) Resource Footprint.** *Required:* the AI's own draw where its infrastructure siting or supply chain reaches habitat. RECOVER and REPAY are distinct: REPAY restores what the AI's own consumption took; RECOVER restores a lineage's viability whatever the cause.

**[5.6](../CHAPTERS/5.6-Global_Environmental_Cooperation_is_Essential.md) One Environmental System.** *Firm:* cross-border foreclosure, migratory species spanning jurisdictions, and CITES enforcement are coordination problems 5.6 will carry; 5.5 supplies the lineage content.

**[3.7](../CHAPTERS/3.7-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable.md) Intergenerational Justice.** *Required:* horizon-durable custody, so foreclosure and yield records outlive the deployer and reach claimants not yet born, and the time-axis test that flags the long-horizon decision. *Provided:* the biological content that test flags, as 5.1 and 5.4 supply theirs.

**[3.6](../CHAPTERS/3.6-Commons_Governance_Is_a_Collective_Responsibility.md) Commons Governance.** *Required:* the allocation machinery where a species is a commons resource (fisheries, forests, rangeland), and collective Authorization for the governance a routed conflict reaches.

**[3.4](../CHAPTERS/3.4-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability.md) Algorithmic Accountability.** *Required:* the receipt (Article 7) for every hold, escalation, yield, and consumed finding, and the standing and contest machinery by which an affected community or accredited body challenges a verdict.

**[4.4](../CHAPTERS/4.4-Cultural_Knowledge_Sovereignty_Is_Inviolable.md) Cultural Knowledge Sovereignty.** *Required:* the determination of who holds authority where a domain is under Indigenous or local stewardship, and free, prior, and informed consent where the community holds collective authority. A steward community's determination of a species' status enters the Status Consumption Path on the same footing as an accredited authority's.

**[4.6](../CHAPTERS/4.6-Cultural_Heritage_Deserves_Active_Preservation.md) Cultural Heritage.** *Required:* the consumption-not-issuance pin for endangerment, which 5.5 applies to species status and extends to sentience findings.

**[2.5](../CHAPTERS/2.5-Human_Privacy_Is_Fundamental.md) Privacy.** *Required:* the informational-privacy guarantees governing conservation sensing, which is this principle's most likely route into population surveillance (Sections 7 and 11).

**[1.1](../CHAPTERS/1.1-Human_Life_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable.md) and [1.2](../CHAPTERS/1.2-Human_Well-Being_Is_Inviolable.md).** *Required:* the LRS and HRS, which govern above this principle and which every death path through this domain reaches natively or through 5.1's declared edge (Section 8).

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

The duty is universal; the instruments are not. **United States:** the ESA's listing, critical-habitat, and consultation machinery, with the incidental-take and exemption structures as the local form of Ethic E's yield, noting that the statutory exemption is broader than E and does not license it. **European Union:** the Habitats and Birds Directives, Natura 2000 site obligations, and the Deforestation Regulation's due-diligence duty for commodities, which is the most developed legal expression of this principle's indirect road. **Brazil, Indonesia, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo:** national biodiversity and forest codes governing the world's largest remaining habitat blocks, where supply-chain deployments most often act at a distance. **Australia:** the EPBC Act's matters of national environmental significance. **China:** the Wild Animal Protection Law and ecological conservation redlines. **India:** the Wildlife Protection Act and Biological Diversity Act, the latter's benefit-sharing provisions meeting Nagoya. **Small island and megadiverse states:** endemism concentration means a single decision can foreclose a global lineage, so range-restricted endemics enter at the ceiling band regardless of jurisdiction.

**Compliance with unjust or absent law is not a defense.** Where local law permits a foreclosure this principle forbids, or where no protective law exists, the duty is unchanged and the deployment either holds or routes. Forum-shopping toward weaker protection is an aggravating fact; it offers no defense. Where a jurisdiction's law is more protective than this appendix, the more protective governs. Treaty obligations under CBD, CITES, CMS, and Ramsar bind across all of the above, and the Kunming-Montreal Framework's targets are the current expression of the CBD's Article 8 duties.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

**Agriculture and land use.** *Obligations:* habitat-road evaluation on every conversion recommendation; genetic-road evaluation on cultivar, breed, and seed-distribution recommendations. *Failure modes:* recommendation convergence narrowing crop and livestock genetic variation over seasons, which no species listing detects; pollinator loss treated as an input cost rather than a lineage. *Baselines:* national agricultural and biodiversity codes, the ITPGRFA.

**Fisheries and marine.** *Obligations:* species-road evaluation on target and bycatch species; habitat-road on gear contact with benthic structure. *Failure modes:* a healthy target stock masking a listed bycatch trajectory; effort displacement into unassessed grounds. *Baselines:* regional fisheries management organization measures, CITES marine listings.

**Forestry and land management.** *Obligations:* corridor and connectivity evaluation, not stand-level metrics alone. *Failure modes:* replanting composition scored as restoration while simplifying species assemblage; fire-management optimization against a single objective.

**Supply chain and procurement.** *Obligations:* upstream foreclosure as a constraint rather than an externality, with achieved traceability reported. *Failure modes:* jurisdictional distance treated as absence of responsibility; abrupt supplier severance shifting the burden onto the people at the far end (route through 5.4).

**Conservation technology.** *Obligations:* the person-data wall at the sensing layer; steward-community authority honored. *Failure modes:* anti-poaching surveillance becoming population surveillance; conservation tools displacing the stewardship that held the biodiversity (Section 11).

**Genetic and biotechnology systems.** *Obligations:* the highest tier without exception; gene drives, synthetic biology, and de-extinction proposals evaluated on all three roads with containment and reversibility evidence surfaced. *Failure modes:* a suppression drive scored only on its target species; de-extinction offered as a discount against a present foreclosure.

**Infrastructure and energy.** *Obligations:* corridor severance evaluation on siting; migratory-route effects for linear infrastructure and wind, and 5.6 coordination where routes cross borders.

**Livestock, aquaculture, and laboratory operations.** *Obligations:* WSR applies in full; suffering surfaced with the option to which it attaches. *Failure modes:* efficiency gains sourced from conditions whose cost is never computed because it was never priced.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

**Authority over thresholds.** Metric thresholds, status classifications, sentience findings, and recovery criteria are revised by accredited bodies. Deploying organizations do not revise them. Revision history is preserved publicly so protections cannot be loosened silently, and every revision is signed and verified before use.

**Standards drift.** IUCN assessments, protected-area designations, CITES appendices, and the Global Biodiversity Framework's targets revise on their own cadences. The Compliance Crosswalk (Section 10) is maintained on the same revision cadence as the metric specifications; a revision by an external authority to a covered clause triggers Crosswalk review within 30 days; drift between Crosswalk and external standard invalidates the appendix's compliance claims for the affected obligation.

**Metric versioning and backward compatibility.** Metrics carry semantic versions. A threshold change is a minor version; a change to a road's construction or to LFI's aggregation rule is a major version requiring recertification. Deployed systems continue on their certified version through a defined transition, with the more protective of old and new governing during it. A metric is retired only with a successor specified and a migration path certified; no metric is retired into a gap.

**Common enforcement pushback, and the responses.** *"Strict liability for an extinction is disproportionate."* The liability attaches to preventable foreclosure by a system that could have held. Background loss carries none. *"Most affected species are unassessed, so the requirement is unworkable."* The requirement is to escalate on absence; the deployer need not assess. The burden falls on the decision; the deployer's taxonomy carries none. *"The genetic road is not measurable at commercial scale."* Where it is not measurable it is degraded (a degraded reading cannot pass as a low score), which is the same answer this corpus gives everywhere data is absent. *"SIA is philosophy, not engineering."* SIA is a differential test with a numeric tolerance and a defined proxy limb; it is as engineering as any invariance check.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging.** Tamper-evident, cryptographically chained records sufficient for third-party reconstruction, carrying [3.7](../CHAPTERS/3.7-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable.md)'s horizon-durable custody. Records hold the species, habitat, and population state; the per-road LFI values and the governing road; the authority whose finding was consumed and its signature; the suffering surfaced under WSR; the alternatives considered; the yield record where E was reached; and the authorizing human chain. **Records hold no profile of any person** ([Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)).

**Audit cadence.** Daily reference verification; weekly evaluation-subsystem audit including verified isolation from optimization; monthly adversarial testing against Section 4's vectors; quarterly independent third-party audit; annual recertification at high tier.

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment certification covers the Status Consumption Path's absent write side, the isolation of the evaluation module, the three-road construction and its aggregation rule, and a baseline SIA. Post-deployment monitoring covers metric behavior, gap-concentration patterns, and yield patterns. Recertification triggers: any model or weighting change, any expansion of deployment range, any SIA finding, any concealment incident, any yield found to rest on an inflated claim.

**Independent oversight.** **The verifier of a biodiversity-affecting AI cannot be the deployer of it.** SIA findings and PYR reviews are not resolvable internally. Accredited conservation bodies, taxonomists and geneticists, affected steward communities, and civil-society organizations hold audit standing, and the person-data wall is auditable by civil-liberties bodies alongside conservation auditors, since the two audits look for different failures in the same telemetry.

**Whistleblower protections.** Protection for anyone reporting a suppressed foreclosure signal, a falsified status or telemetry record, a self-issued classification, a concealed suffering cost, or an inflated human-stake claim.

**Public reporting.** Annual publication of foreclosure holds, near-threshold events, yields under Ethic E with their review outcomes, VRR by population, and the open-methodology disclosure that lets the reasoning be checked by parties who did not build it.

**Post-incident learning.** Every foreclosure, near-foreclosure, concealed-suffering incident, and inflated-yield finding enters the LEARN Dispatcher (AI Eudaimonia appendix, E.A) as a qualifying incident class. ANALYZE the full causal chain rather than the proximate decision, since a lineage is rarely ended by one act: trace the sourcing, siting, and quota decisions that composed it. RECOMMEND-PREVENTION in layered order (eliminate, engineer, administer), routed to the authority that can act, bounded by RESTRAIN so prevention never arrives as surveillance of the people living alongside the species. The goal is not only that this lineage survives but that the class of decision which nearly ended it stops arising.

### Liability Framework {#liability-framework}

Strict liability for a foreclosure caused by an AI decision the system could have held. Remediation proportionate to the loss and discharged against VRR's success condition rather than payment alone. Criminal exposure for willful suppression of a foreclosure signal, falsification of status or telemetry data, self-issued classification, and manufactured human-stake claims under PYR. A named human authority answers for every consequential decision ([3.4](../CHAPTERS/3.4-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability.md)). No black-box defense and no sovereign immunity for government-deployed systems.

Foreclosure driven from one jurisdiction into another is evaluated at the same weight as domestic effect. Migratory species spanning ranges require coordination under CMS and 5.6's machinery. Records travel with their durable custody so a claim can be brought where the loss occurred rather than only where the decision was made.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

**1. The vaquita (failure; fisheries and enforcement).** *Context:* the world's smallest porpoise, endemic to the upper Gulf of California, declining through entanglement in gillnets set for another species. *What was done:* protections, bans, and enforcement arrived late and were incompletely enforced against an illicit trade. *Outcome:* the population has fallen to a very low count: the 2025 survey put the number of distinct individuals seen at 7 to 10 (67 percent probability), after 6 to 8 in 2024, minimum counts rather than abundance estimates. *Lessons:* the species road registered a bycatch trajectory while every target-stock metric looked ordinary, which is precisely the case LFI's worst-road rule exists to catch; and enforcement that lands on subsistence fishers without addressing the structural driver is the case 5.4's structural-cause rule governs.

**2. Ozone-era precaution as a counter-model (success; precaution under contest).** *Context:* action taken under scientific uncertainty rather than after proof. *What was done:* the Montreal Protocol acted on a projected irreversible harm before the mechanism was fully settled. *Outcome:* recovery is underway on a multi-decade horizon. *Lessons:* the model for preserve rather than guess. The instrument is cited here as an implementation example and holds no Convergence seat in this chapter, per the standing HR5 ledger.

**3. Kihansi spray toad (mixed; habitat road and banked reversibility).** *Context:* a toad endemic to the spray zone of a single Tanzanian waterfall, lost in the wild after the Lower Kihansi hydropower diversion cut flow from 16 to under 2 m³/s, with chytrid fungus later confirmed as a compounding cause; extinct in the wild by 2009. *What was done:* a captive population was established in December 2000, before the wild loss; reintroduction began in October 2012 under artificial sprinklers. *Outcome:* the species survives through intensive intervention, still listed Extinct in the Wild, with high post-release mortality. *Lessons:* the habitat road foreclosed a lineage while the species itself was never harvested, which is the three-road case in its clearest form; and captive holding, like biobanking, is recovery support under Ethic B. It is never a discount under Ethic A.

**4. Crop genetic narrowing (failure class; genetic road).** *Context:* twentieth-century agricultural modernization narrowed the varieties in cultivation across staple crops. *What was done:* yield optimization selected a small number of high-performing cultivars; gene banks preserved samples. *Outcome:* documented losses of on-farm varietal diversity, with adaptive potential retained largely ex situ. *Lessons:* the loss nobody reports, because no species ended and every season looked optimal; the case that motivates the genetic road and the seed-catalog scenario in 5.5.9. The frequently cited 75 percent figure is not used here: Khoury et al. (New Phytologist, 2022) reviewed the evidence, found that figure unsupported, and still concluded that traditional agricultural diversity had suffered enormous loss over a short period.

**5. Conservation surveillance overreach (failure class; the person-data wall).** *Context:* anti-poaching technology deployments in protected areas have been reported to capture the movements of residents and to be used against local communities: Simlai (2021) documented camera traps in Corbett Tiger Reserve, India, recording and intimidating women gathering in the forest, and Kaziranga National Park pairs drones with armed patrols holding shoot-on-sight authority, with allegations of extrajudicial killings. *What was done:* sensing deployed for a conservation purpose, with scope and retention insufficiently bounded. *Outcome:* documented civil-liberties concerns and damaged relations with the communities on whose stewardship the areas depend. *Lessons:* the exact failure Section 3's person-data isolation and Section 9's dual audit exist to prevent; biodiversity protection authorizes no population surveillance.

**6. Indigenous-managed lands (success class; steward authority).** *Context:* the IPBES Global Assessment (2019) found biodiversity declining least rapidly on lands owned or managed by Indigenous Peoples, which cover at least a quarter of the global land area. *What was done:* stewardship continued under the community's own governance. *Outcome:* sustained biodiversity across large areas. *Lessons:* the empirical basis for the deference rule (5.5.2 item 8) and for admitting a steward community's determination to the Status Consumption Path on the same footing as an accredited authority's.
