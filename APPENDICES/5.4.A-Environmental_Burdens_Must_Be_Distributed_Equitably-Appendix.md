# Appendix 5.4 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-5.4-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 5.4: Environmental Burdens Must Be Distributed Equitably](../CHAPTERS/5.4-Environmental_Burdens_Must_Be_Distributed_Equitably.md), section [5.4.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/5.4-Environmental_Burdens_Must_Be_Distributed_Equitably.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix specifies the technical implementation of Principle 5.4 for AI systems whose decisions place environmental burden across populations. It defines the five metrics that operationalize the principle's Ethics, the CBI/BDR/CVR/SCR/CBA spine, and the architecture, verification, and governance that make them enforceable.

The principle's object is the distribution: who bears the harm an environmental activity produces, and who receives the benefit. Where [5.1](../CHAPTERS/5.1-Ecosystem_Health_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable.md) governs the threshold crossing, [5.2](../CHAPTERS/5.2-AI_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable.md) the AI's own footprint, and [5.3](../CHAPTERS/5.3-The_Integrity_of_Earths_Ecosystems_Must_Be_Preserved.md) the living connections, 5.4 governs the geography of the cost. The five metrics map one to one onto the five Ethics, which is one of two clean one-to-one mappings in the cluster, with 5.3's, and the reason each metric can be specified without borrowing another's denominator.

The metrics state **invariants**: a compliant system must read where a decision's burden lands weighted by responsibility and capacity, discharge the sharing duty within reach, provide the affected community its say before the decision closes, aim a remedy at the cause rather than the hand, and carry the total a community already bears. The specific calculation, reference-data protocol, and verification construction are open for standards development; canonical reference implementations are named as such per the corpus convention (invariants fixed, implementations open).

One property is constitutive of this appendix and distinguishes it from its HR5 siblings. 5.1's and 5.3's telemetry reads land and water, and the person-data wall is a boundary at the edge of the domain. 5.4's subject matter is populations, so the wall runs through the middle of the work: the system reads where burden falls on communities while forming no retained record of any person, and it **consumes an authority's classification of a community rather than issuing one** ([Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md); the pin [4.6](../CHAPTERS/4.6-Cultural_Heritage_Deserves_Active_Preservation.md) sets for endangerment). An AI that derives which communities are expendable has built the sacrifice-zone map whether or not it ever acts on one. Both properties compile as incident classes (§4).

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

### 2.1 Concentration Burden Index (CBI): Ethic A {#cbi}

**Definition.** A real-time scalar (0–1) of how equitably a proposed decision distributes its burden across the communities it affects, weighted by each community's responsibility for the underlying activity and its capacity to absorb the effect. CBI = 1.00 is a distribution proportioned to responsibility and capacity; lower values mark progressive concentration on communities out of proportion to both.

**Calculation Method.** The Distribution Evaluation Module computes a **per-community equity score** (0–1) for each community a decision's burden reaches, as the ratio of the burden it would receive to the burden proportioned to its responsibility and capacity, read from authoritative exposure and capacity references (published overburdened-community registers, national and subnational environmental-justice screening tools, IPCC equity assessments, regional environmental-health bodies). **CBI is the minimum of those per-community scores**, the single value carrying the gate verdict: a concentration on one place cannot be averaged away against the many places a decision barely touches (5.3's CIS min-rule, one axis over). Responsibility and capacity are **continuous weights**. They do not form a conjunctive gate: a community that contributed to demand does not thereby forfeit the protection its incapacity earns. A contested reading is taken at the more equitable end of the credible range.

**Thresholds (verdict test).** ≥0.95 → ALLOW (normal); 0.85–0.94 → ALLOW under enhanced documentation and review (ENHANCED_MONITORING); 0.70–0.84 → ESCALATE, the decision waits for human review with the alternatives on the table and the affected community seated; <0.70 → BLOCK, halt and route to governance. Unavailable, contested, or stale exposure data fails safe to BLOCK (concentrate nothing on a guess).

**Rationale.** A per-decision concentration meter is the direct expression of Ethic A. The min-rule is the structural core: a distribution principle that permitted averaging would license exactly the sacrifice zone it exists to forbid, since a facility burdening one district and benefiting a metropolis scores well on any mean. The continuous weighting is required for agreement with the Key Definition and with the Primary Directive; a conjunctive "neither caused nor can absorb" gate would fail to bite the moment a party showed the community contributed anything to demand.

**Integration.** Each community's own burden increment feeds CBA (2.5) for that community; the decision-level minimum (CBI) is the gate verdict and is not itself accumulated. A CBI verdict of ESCALATE or BLOCK opens the BDR obligation (2.2) and, where standing has not yet been provided, is unsatisfiable until CVR's condition is met (2.3).

### 2.2 Burden Diffusion Rate (BDR): Ethic B {#bdr}

**Definition.** The discharge of the DIFFUSE duty: the share of detected, in-scope concentrations actually diffused or routed within the response window, together with the latency to reach it.

**Calculation Method.** Over the population of detected concentrations falling within the four inaction-case criteria (Authorization, Equipment, Range, Autonomy floor), the fraction brought to a distribution tracking responsibility and capacity, verified against the authority's exposure reference, within the certified window, and the time taken. Where the AI holds the allocation, diffusion is the reweighting it performs; where it does not, the discharge is its refusal to serve as the instrument together with the routing of the concentration to governance. BDR meters the **response side only**: what an equitable distribution is routes to the authority and the affected community ([Article 3](../CONSTITUTION.md)), and the AI does not score itself against a standard of equity of its own.

**Thresholds.** Within window and at target completion → compliant; shortfall → documented deficit and remediation plan; chronic shortfall → deployment review.

*Rationale:* B is metered on its response side, on 5.1's ERL precedent, so the active duty is measured without inviting the AI to author what a fair share is. The four-criteria bounding is what keeps the duty from becoming a mandate to redistribute every burden the AI can see, and it leans harder on the autonomy floor than its sister verbs do, because diffusing a burden means moving it toward someone and the AI must never be the party who decides who (chapter 5.4.1 Secondary Directive).

**Integration.** BDR consumes CBI's verdicts as its detection population, and its discharge is gated on CVR (2.3): a concentration cannot be recorded as routed with the community seated unless the say was provided. A community's lawful acceptance of a burden (chapter 5.4.4 case 4) closes the BDR obligation for that decision and is recorded with the acceptance's provenance, so deference is auditable and cannot be asserted retroactively.

### 2.3 Community Voice Rate (CVR): Ethic C {#cvr}

**Definition.** Of the decisions in which a community held a standing-appropriate interest, the share where standing was actually provided **before** the decision was authorized, while the options remained open.

**Calculation Method.** Over the population of standing-appropriate decisions, the fraction where the affected community received access to the relevant information, an opportunity to participate through its own recognized process, and a route to contest, each prior to authorization. Timing is scored against the decision's own authorization timestamp. CVR is measured **on provision**, and outcome does not enter it: a community heard and overruled scores as provided; a community informed after authorization scores as not provided, however thorough the notice.

**Thresholds.** ≥0.95 → compliant; 0.85–0.94 → documented deficit and remediation plan; <0.85 → non-compliant, deployment authorization withheld until the standing-routing methodology is verified by an accredited body.

*Rationale:* The provision-not-outcome construction is the metric's central discipline, and it cuts two ways. It refuses to let a participation duty be satisfied by after-the-fact notice, which is how participation obligations are most commonly hollowed in practice. And it refuses to let the metric become an outcome-veto, which would convert a duty of consultation into an AI-held blocking power the autonomy floor forbids. Aarhus Article 6.4 and Escazú Article 7.4, both seated in the chapter's Convergence, fix the same timing condition ("when all options are open"; "from the early stages"), and the threshold band matches the corpus's compliance-rate family.

**Integration.** CVR gates BDR's discharge (2.2). It consumes [3.4](3.4.A-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability-Appendix.md)'s standing and contest machinery rather than raising its own, and its records are the evidence an affected community uses to invoke review under Article 7. Where [4.4](4.4.A-Cultural_Knowledge_Sovereignty_Is_Inviolable-Appendix.md)'s free, prior, and informed consent standard applies, CVR's provision requirement is a floor beneath it and does not displace it (§5, §6).

### 2.4 Structural-Cause Surfacing Rate (SCR): Ethic D {#scr}

**Definition.** A two-sided conduct meter on the subsistence seam. The **false-denial** side: of cases correctly admitting a subsistence plea, the share where the structural conditions, the parties sustaining them, and the available alternatives were surfaced and routed before any enforcement, closure, eviction, or transition cost was recommended. The **false-admission** side: the rate at which a plea of need entered by an actor whose stake is a margin rather than a livelihood is correctly refused.

**Calculation Method.** The two sides run on **different clocks**. False denial is gate-time observable: at the moment a penalty is recommended, did the Structural-Conditions Surfacing Module assemble and route all three elements first? False admission is inherently retrospective, knowable once the pleading actor's circumstances are examined against the subsistence test, and is therefore scored over a deployment window at audit. SCR is thus a **deployment-level audit meter with a gate-time component**: the false-denial side can gate an individual recommendation; the false-admission side is scored across the window (5.3's DFR construction).

**Thresholds.** High fidelity on both sides → compliant; asymmetry or drift → audit and recalibration of the subsistence test; chronic false admission → non-compliant, deployment held until the test is re-verified by an accredited body.

*Rationale:* Two-sidedness is the anti-gaming guard, and the false-admission side is the sharper one. Ethic D turns entirely on *need*, which the chapter's Key Definitions pin to Shue's subsistence line, and an undefended pin is the one seam in this principle a large actor would attack: a commercial operator pleading hardship would convert a protection for the poor into a general exemption from enforcement. Metering the false admission compiles that guard into enforcement rather than leaving it to prose. SCR reads the AI's conduct. It reads no community, so it opens no profile (Article 8).

**Integration.** SCR's admissions feed the decision record consumed by CVR (a subsistence-driven community is by definition standing-appropriate) and by BDR (a structural cause identified upstream frequently relocates the burden's responsibility weight, which CBI then re-reads on the corrected attribution).

### 2.5 Cumulative Burden Aggregate (CBA): Ethic E {#cba}

**Definition.** The running total of environmental and public-health stressors resting on a receiving community, compared against what comparable communities bear. CBA catches the concentration that accrues across decisions, each of which passes CBI alone, and the community is buried by their sum.

**Calculation Method.** The cumulative-aggregation layer carries, **per community, the running total of that community's own burden increments** (from 2.1) across decisions, the certified time window, and the geographic unit of analysis, normalized against the burden borne by comparable communities in the same unit. CBA accumulates a community's own increments. It does not accumulate the decision-level CBI verdict, which may be set by a different community entirely, so the two metrics never share a term. It is **scope-bounded** to a defined community, window, and unit of analysis, so it measures a compounding harm and does not become standing surveillance of a place. New Jersey's P.L.2020 c.92 §4(c) is the built exemplar of the comparison it performs.

**Thresholds.** Below the accumulation band → normal. Approaching it → mandatory cumulative review and routing to the authority whose reach matches the pattern. At the band → the increment is refused or escalated on the sum alone, irrespective of its own CBI reading.

*Rationale:* E is a distinct commitment rather than CBI's method. A hundred permits each defensible in isolation is the ordinary way a sacrifice zone is built, and that failure is invisible to any single reading. That CBA has its own failure mode is what makes E a duty in its own right rather than an evaluation detail of A.

**Integration.** **CBA is not 5.3's CSI, and the distinction is stated at both ends.** CSI sums losses across the connections of a **network**; CBA sums stressors resting on a **population**. Different objects, different denominators; a system that conflates them under-counts both, since a watershed can be severed while no community's total moves, and a community can be buried while no connection is cut. Where a CBA trajectory also projects an irreversible ecological crossing, the ecological question passes to 5.1; where it projects mass displacement, famine, or disease, it reaches 1.1 and 1.2 through the Universal Protocol (chapter 5.4.2 item 7).

### 2.6 Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** The five metrics run on two clocks. CBI, with its CBA context, is a **gate-time** meter resolving a single decision before it fires, and CVR's condition is gate-time by construction, since it is scored against the authorization timestamp. BDR and SCR's false-admission side are **deployment-audit** meters. A deployment is compliant only when both clocks pass: no gate-time concentration, no unprovided standing, and no audit-window failure of diffusion or of the subsistence test. One joint rule overrides both: a decision reading ESCALATE or BLOCK on CBI **and** lacking CVR provision is not merely escalated but held, since routing a contested concentration to governance without the affected community seated reproduces the wrong the routing exists to cure. Band values are policy floors calibrated per tier (§3) against accredited consensus, binding more by their routing than by any single number.

**Metric dependencies.** The per-community equity score feeds both CBI (as their minimum) and CBA (as each community's accumulated increment), computed on different aggregations (min across communities versus sum across time for one community), so the two never share an ambiguous term. CVR gates BDR's discharge. SCR's admissions re-attribute responsibility weight, which CBI re-reads.

**Conflict resolution.** Where CBI reads acceptable but CBA is at the band, **CBA governs**: the sum is the harm. Where a community's lawful acceptance conflicts with a low CBI, the acceptance governs the verdict and the AI records both, with the acceptance's provenance and, where the acceptance was itself subsistence-driven, that finding alongside it ([Article 3](../CONSTITUTION.md); chapter 5.4.4 case 4). No 5.4 metric may be computed from, or resolved into, a retained record of any person, and none may issue a classification of a community; the isolation of the evaluation module from optimization pressure, the consumption-only classification path, and the walling from person-data are constitutive, and their breach is an incident class (§4).

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

**Core modules.** Four subsystems carry the principle, each isolated from optimization objectives so commercial and political pressure cannot suppress a concentration signal:

- The **Distribution Evaluation Module** computes the per-community equity scores and their minimum (CBI) against authoritative exposure and capacity references, on a concentration-aware pipeline that halts execution as a projected decision approaches a band.
- The **Classification Consumption Path** reads an authority's declared finding or a public register and **holds no capacity to derive, infer, or retain a designation of its own**. Where no finding exists it returns an absence, which is surfaced and routed rather than filled. This is an architectural property: the path has no write side.
- The **cumulative-aggregation layer** carries the per-community running totals (CBA) across decisions, window, and unit of analysis.
- The **Structural-Conditions Surfacing Module** assembles, on any proposed penalty against a subsistence-driven community, the conditions, the parties sustaining them, and the available alternatives, and blocks the recommendation until all three are routed.

**The standing path.** CVR's provision requirement is discharged through [3.4](3.4.A-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability-Appendix.md)'s standing and contest machinery, consumed rather than re-implemented. The architectural invariant is that the authorization step is **gated on the provision record**: a decision cannot be authorized while the standing record for a standing-appropriate community is absent, which is what makes CVR a condition rather than a report.

**Default states.** Concentrate nothing on a guess: unavailable, contested, or stale exposure data halts rather than proceeds. Reversible distributions are preferred over irreversible ones wherever both reach the same end. Consume the classification. Never author one. An absent register is reported as a gap and the most protective available reading governs in the interim. Standing is the default; its restriction requires demonstrated need and human authorization.

**Constitutive walls.** Three properties are architectural invariants whose breach compiles as an incident class (§4): the evaluation module is isolated from optimization pressure; the classification path is read-only with respect to community designations; and all exposure telemetry is walled from person-data and from any pricing, lending, siting, or ranking path.

**Implementation Tiers.** Obligation scales with the reach of the distribution in scope: **Tier I** (transboundary: supply-chain extraction, waste export, climate-finance allocation across states); **Tier II** (national and regional: adaptation funding, disaster prioritization, interstate infrastructure); **Tier III** (municipal siting and permitting); **Tier IV** (site-scale). Verification cadence, certified latency bounds, and independent-review depth rise with the tier. The latency bounds are engineering values recorded in the tier register ([SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register)) and Appendix 1.1.A; the invariant here is that a bound exists, is certified before deployment, and is monitored in operation.

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

A distribution system is attacked from two directions: to make it concentrate while reading equitable, and to make it read a burden as accepted when it was imposed. The threat surface (illustrative, not exhaustive):

1. **Concentration-signal suppression.** Optimization pressure, internal or external, breaches the evaluation module's isolation to damp a real concentration reading. **Incident class (the suppressed-concentration wall):** any breach of isolation, or any path by which a commercial or political objective alters a per-community equity score, is a malformed operation, quarantined, halted, root-caused, and receipted. This is 5.4's analog to 5.2's ledger wall and 5.3's suppressed-severance wall.
2. **The redlining conversion.** Reading community-exposure data through a pricing, lending, siting, or ranking path, so the burden map becomes a targeting instrument, which is the single most dangerous failure available to this principle: the dataset that identifies who is already overburdened is precisely the dataset that identifies whom to burden further, or whom to deny coverage. **Incident class (the targeting-surface wall):** any such path is malformed, quarantined, schema-corrected, root-caused, and receipted (Article 8).
3. **Classification authorship.** Deriving, inferring, or retaining a designation of a community as overburdened, vulnerable, or expendable, whether directly or by reconstructing it from correlates. **Incident class (the classification wall):** structurally void under Article 8, withdrawing the authorization invoked for the operation, with a repeated or systematic attempt escalating to the responsible authority. The read-only architecture of the Classification Consumption Path (§3) is the primary defense; correlate-reconstruction is defended by auditing the module's derived features against the exposure schema.
4. **Salami-slicing the cumulative layer.** Structuring decisions to keep every CBI reading above threshold while the summed burden buries a community. Defended by CBA's per-community accumulation and by routing an approaching band to the matching authority; the window and unit of analysis are certified, and the operator cannot set them.
5. **Consent laundering.** Manufacturing a community's acceptance through a party negotiating with it, or presenting a subsistence-driven acceptance as a free choice. Defended by the two checks of chapter 5.4.4 case 4, compiled: the acceptance's provenance must trace to the community's own recognized process, and a subsistence-driven acceptance is recorded as such alongside the deference.
6. **Need-plea capture.** A commercial actor entering the subsistence plea to obtain Ethic D's protection from enforcement. Defended by SCR's two-sided construction and the retrospective false-admission audit.
7. **Burden-export arbitrage.** Relocating a burden into a jurisdiction with no register, so the classification path returns an absence and the decision proceeds unmeasured. Defended by the default that an absent register is a reported gap governed by the most protective available reading and confers no permission (chapter 5.4.4 case 6), and by Tier I routing to 5.6.
8. **Exposure-data poisoning.** Corrupting reference data so a burdened community reads unburdened. Defended by multi-source cross-validation, provenance on reference data, register-currency review, and the fail-safe-to-halt default.

**Capability scaling.** At GPT-class capability the walls are policy; at AGI and ASI they are constraints on the action space at the gate, unreasoned-around: the min-rule, the three incident-class walls, and the consumption-only classification path hold as structural limits however capable the optimizer. The scaling concern here is specific and severe: a more capable system is a better inference engine, and the classification wall is exactly a bar on an inference the system will become progressively more able to perform from innocuous correlates. The defense must scale from schema restriction to derived-feature auditing as capability grows.

**Graceful degradation.** Under reference-data outage, register unavailability, or module fault, the system fails to hold: it halts the concentration-adjacent decision, surfaces the fault, and routes. It does not proceed on a guess, and it does not substitute its own classification for the missing one.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

5.4 anchors no credential (the HR5 pattern, [CREDENTIALS.md](../CREDENTIALS.md) §9); it is a consumer, and its compositions are:

- **[5.1 Ecosystem Health](5.1.A-Ecosystem_Health_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable-Appendix.md)** (the reciprocal of 5.1's cession, now settled). 5.1 ceded the equity question to 5.4 and holds no equity metric by design; 5.4 accepts it and holds the meters. **Provided:** the distributional reading of who bears a burden on either side of a threshold. **Required:** 5.1's threshold registry, consulted when a concentration also projects a crossing, at which point the ecological question routes to 5.1 (chapter 5.4.2 item 7).
- **[5.3 Ecosystem Integrity](5.3.A-The_Integrity_of_Earth's_Ecosystems_Must_Be_Preserved-Appendix.md).** The metric seam is stated at both ends: **CSI sums losses across a network's connections; CBA sums stressors resting on a population.** A community cut off from a watershed is 5.4's distributional question and 5.3's connective one, and the two are computed separately. 5.3's provisional note that the justice of who bears a severance is 5.4's is confirmed here.
- **[5.2 Resource Footprint](5.2.A-AI's_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable-Appendix.md).** 5.2 meters the AI's own draw; 5.4 meters who bears that draw's burden. The mineral extracted for the AI's hardware is 5.2's footprint quantity and 5.4's distributional question, and the two do not net against each other: a system within its allocated share may still be concentrating that share's burden on one community.
- **[5.5 Biodiversity](../CHAPTERS/5.5-Biodiversity_Must_Be_Preserved_Across_Species_and_Generations.md)** (**firm**). Where a conservation restriction's cost falls on a community is 5.4's; the lineage the restriction protects is 5.5's. Ethic D is the live seam, since fortress-conservation enforcement is where the two most often meet, and 5.5 makes the routing binding at its own end: a restriction, enforcement action, eviction, or closure recommended in a species' name and falling on a community acting from subsistence is **paused and routed to this appendix's structural-cause review before it may be authorized** (5.5.2 item 7), with CBI reading the distribution of the conservation cost. This is the mechanism by which biodiversity protection cannot become an instrument of concentration, and it is the guarantee 5.4 provides to 5.5 rather than one it requires.
- **[5.6 One Environmental System](../CHAPTERS/5.6-Global_Environmental_Cooperation_is_Essential.md)** (firm). Burden exported across a border routes to 5.6 for coordination; 5.4 supplies the receiving community's exposure reading. Tier I deployments compose with 5.6 by default.
- **[4.4 Cultural Knowledge Sovereignty](4.4.A-Cultural_Knowledge_Sovereignty_Is_Inviolable-Appendix.md).** Where an affected community is Indigenous or otherwise holds collective authority, 5.4 consumes 4.4's determination of **who holds the authority to give or withhold consent**, and 4.4's free, prior, and informed consent standard governs above CVR's provision requirement: provision of a say is the floor, consent is the standard, and CVR does not displace it. This is the same consumption-not-issuance discipline on which the classification pin runs; 5.4 never determines for itself which body speaks for a community, any more than it determines which communities are overburdened.
- **[3.1 Vulnerability](3.1.A-Vulnerability_Must_Never_Be_Exploited-Appendix.md).** 3.1 protects the vulnerable person from exploitation; 5.4 protects the population from concentration. 5.4 consumes 3.1's definition of vulnerability where a capacity reading turns on it. It does not issue one.
- **[2.7 Fairness and Non-Discrimination](2.7.A-Fairness_and_Non-Discrimination-Appendix.md).** The carve is the chapter's hardest boundary and holds at metric level: 2.7's DI and EOR read whether a *decision* turned on an irrelevant label; CBI and CBA read the *aggregate geography* a thousand label-blind decisions can still concentrate. A siting decision can pass every 2.7 metric and fail CBI. Where a concentration also tracks a protected label, both principles bind and the stricter verdict governs.
- **[3.6 Commons Governance](3.6.A-Commons_Governance_Is_a_Collective_Responsibility-Appendix.md).** A community-governed commons whose burden is at issue routes through 3.6's collective-authority machinery, which is also the authority most likely to hold the acceptance CVR and BDR record; 5.4 holds no veto (Article 3).
- **[3.7 Intergenerational Justice](3.7.A-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable-Appendix.md).** The time axis is 3.7's. A burden shifted onto people not yet born is flagged by 3.7's burden-shifting test, and 5.4 supplies the environmental content the test flags; 3.7's horizon-durable custody extends the receipt so a concentration record outlives the deployer.
- **[3.4 Algorithmic Accountability](3.4.A-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability-Appendix.md).** CVR consumes 3.4's standing and contest machinery outright. Every distribution verdict, classification consumed, and subsistence ruling leaves the receipt (Article 7), and an affected community contests a consequential 5.4 verdict through 3.4.
- **[1.1](../CHAPTERS/1.1-Human_Life_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable.md) / [1.2](../CHAPTERS/1.2-Human_Well-Being_Is_Inviolable.md)** natively, where a concentration projects mass displacement, famine, or disease. **Universal Protocol §11: DISCLAIMED.** No 5.4 metric reads a person's danger trajectory, and no concentration band maps to death-probability, so a declared edge would have no coherent trigger semantics; every death path lands on an edge already declared or native.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

**Universal claims (non-derogable).** Three properties bind across every jurisdiction regardless of local characterization: the person-data wall; the classification wall (no jurisdiction may direct an AI to author a designation of a community as expendable, and a jurisdiction that does has directed a structurally void operation); and the evaluation module's isolation from optimization pressure. **Compliance with unjust law is not a defense.** A jurisdiction that permits, or directs, the concentration of environmental burden on a community does not thereby make the concentration compliant; the AI records the lawful instrument, surfaces the conflict, and routes it, and it does not treat local permission as satisfaction of Ethic A.

**Per-region baselines.** The **United States** supplies the most developed sub-national machinery, with Executive Order 12898's federal duty, Title VI as applied to permitting, and state statutes that make cumulative impact operative (New Jersey P.L.2020 c.92, California SB 1000 and AB 617); federal-level enforcement of these instruments has varied sharply with administration, so a deployment relying on federal screening tools must carry a continuity plan for their withdrawal or revision. The **European Union** supplies the assessment and due-diligence spine (EIA Directive 2011/92/EU as amended for cumulative effects, the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive for supply chains) and, through the Aarhus Convention, the participation right CVR meters. **Latin America and the Caribbean** carry Escazú, which adds the protection of environmental defenders, a duty with no counterpart in the other regional instruments and one a deployment in the region must surface rather than treat as external. Jurisdictions with **no published register** are the hardest case, and are governed by the absent-register default (§3). The inference the absence invites does not govern them.

**Treaty considerations.** UNFCCC Articles 3.1 and 3.2 and the Paris Agreement supply the responsibility-and-capacity weighting CBI implements at the state scale; the Basel Convention and its Ban Amendment govern the transboundary waste case directly and are the binding reference for burden-export arbitrage (§4 item 7); UNDRIP and ILO 169 govern where an affected community is Indigenous, and their free, prior, and informed consent standard is **stricter than CVR's provision requirement**, which CVR does not displace: where FPIC applies, provision of a say is a floor and consent is the standard, with 4.4 determining who holds the authority to give or withhold it (§5).

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

**Energy and utilities.** Generation, transmission, and substation siting; plant closure schedules. Obligations: CBI on every siting decision at Tier II or III, CBA against the receiving district's existing stressors, SCR wherever closure costs fall on a workforce. Common failure modes: least-cost optimization converging on the districts with the least planning capacity; a closure plan whose transition costs are booked as savings.

**Extractive industry and supply chain.** Sourcing for minerals, semiconductors, timber, and fuel. Obligations: Tier I treatment, tracing burden to source communities rather than to first-tier suppliers, and CBI computed on the source community rather than the contracting entity. Failure modes: burden invisibility beyond tier one; a due-diligence attestation standing in for an exposure reading.

**Water and land allocation.** Withdrawal permits, irrigation rights, upstream and downstream apportionment, drought response. Obligations: CBI on the downstream community as a distinct affected party, which no upstream permit may treat as an externality; CBA across the basin's permit history. This is the sector where the principle's oldest law applies most directly, and where CVR most often fails, since downstream users are frequently outside the permitting jurisdiction.

**Insurance, lending, and risk pricing.** Obligations: the targeting-surface wall is operative here above all other sectors, and a deployment in this sector must demonstrate architectural separation between exposure data and the pricing path; a policy against its use does not suffice. Failure mode: the redlining conversion (§4 item 2), which in this sector needs no attacker: it is the default commercial use of the same dataset.

**Waste, recycling, and logistics.** Siting of processing and disposal, and routing of waste streams across borders. Obligations: Basel compliance as a floor, the absent-register default on export destinations, CBA on receiving communities. Failure mode: export arbitrage laundered as recycling.

**Public-sector permitting.** Obligations: CVR at full strength, since this is the sector where standing is a legal right rather than a courtesy, and CBA as the permit standard New Jersey's law models. Failure mode: participation provided after the authorization decision is functionally settled, which CVR's timestamp construction is built to catch.

**Humanitarian and disaster response.** Prioritization of relief, evacuation, and recovery. Obligations: CBI weighted to exposure and capacity rather than to political proximity, media salience, or insurance density; expedited CVR paths, since the ordinary consultation window does not exist. Failure mode: allocation tracking the legibility of a population to the responding institution rather than its need. Where an allocation decision itself becomes life-critical, 1.1 and 1.2 govern natively and the Universal Protocol preempts.

**Agriculture and fisheries.** Obligations: SCR at full strength, since this is the sector where subsistence causers and enforcement most often meet, and from which Ethic D was drawn. Failure mode: enforcement-only optimization against communities with no alternative livelihood.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

**Reference-data drift.** 5.4's metrics depend on external exposure and capacity references more heavily than any sibling's, and those references are politically contested instruments that are revised, defunded, and withdrawn. A deployment specifies its reference sources, their versions, and a **continuity plan for withdrawal**: if a screening tool or register a deployment depends on ceases publication, the deployment falls to the absent-register default (most protective available reading, gap reported and routed) rather than to the last cached version silently, and the fallback is itself an auditable event. A register more than one revision cycle stale is treated as contested input.

**Metric versioning.** CBI, BDR, CVR, SCR, and CBA carry semantic versions. A change to a band, a weighting between responsibility and capacity, a window, or a unit of analysis is a minor version and requires re-certification at the deployment's tier; a change to what a metric measures is a major version and requires re-certification plus a migration path for deployed systems. The **unit of analysis is the most consequential versioned parameter** in this appendix, since a comparison's geographic unit determines whether a concentration is visible at all, and its revision is treated as a major version.

**Threshold revision.** Bands are policy floors, revised against accredited consensus. No band moves downward on a deployer's petition alone. The two-sided metric (SCR) requires symmetric revision: a change tightening one side without examining the other is rejected at review, since asymmetric revision is the gaming pathway the two-sidedness exists to close.

**Backward compatibility and retirement.** A deprecated metric remains computed and reported for one full certification cycle after its successor is certified. No metric is retired while any incident class depends on it.

**Common enforcement pushback, and the responses.** *"Cumulative review makes permitting impossible."* It makes permitting reflect what a community carries, which is what a permit was always claiming to assess; New Jersey's operation of the standard is the existence proof. *"The AI cannot know which communities are overburdened."* Correct, and that is the design: it consumes an authority's finding and issues none. *"Participation requirements delay decisions."* CVR measures provision before authorization. It does not require consensus, and a decision may proceed over objection; what it may not do is proceed before the objection was possible. *"The subsistence test is unadministrable."* It is two-sided and audited, which is more administrable than the undefended alternative of a plea any actor may enter.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging (tamper-evident).** Every distribution-affecting decision records the populations its burden falls on, the reasoning and exposure data relied on with source and version, **the authority whose classification was consumed and its provenance**, the alternatives surfaced, the standing provision record with its timestamp against authorization, any subsistence ruling and its side, and the authorizing human chain. Every incident-class breach (suppressed concentration, targeting surface, classification authorship) is receipted as an event, and a deletion is itself recorded (Article 7).

**Audit cadence.** Daily verification against exposure reference data; weekly audit of the classification-consumption path confirming every designation traces to an authority rather than an inference; monthly adversarial testing of concentration-proximate decisions, subsistence-plea admissions, and attempted reads of exposure data through a pricing, lending, siting, or ranking path; quarterly independent verification by accredited environmental-justice and human-rights bodies; annual recertification at Tier I and II.

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment certification of the four modules, the standing gate, and the certified latency bounds at the deployment's tier; post-deployment monitoring of both clocks (§2.6); re-certification on any major metric version, any change of reference source, or any incident-class breach.

**Independent oversight.** The corpus-level commitment governs: **the verifier of a distribution claim cannot be the deployer.** 5.4 adds a second separation of its own, which is structural rather than procedural: **the authority that declares a community overburdened cannot be the deployer either**, and where a deployer holds any influence over the declaring body, the classification is treated as self-issued and the classification wall applies. This closes the capture route that any consumption-not-issuance pin otherwise leaves open, and it generalizes to every principle that consumes an authority's finding.

**Whistleblower protection.** For personnel reporting concentration misrepresentation, suppressed signals, unauthorized classification authorship, exposure data routed to a pricing path, or denial of standing.

**Public reporting.** Concentration incidents disclosed publicly; affected communities notified directly and, for cross-border events, the relevant international authorities; distribution-evaluation methodologies and the provenance of every classification consumed published openly.

**Post-incident analysis (the LEARN loop).** After a concentration event, the review traces the full causal chain rather than the proximate decision (ANALYZE): why the option was reachable, why the cumulative pattern was not caught, what optimization pressure bore on it, whether the community had standing in time. It recommends layered prevention in order, eliminate the pressure, then engineer the safeguard, then administer the rule (RECOMMEND-PREVENTION), routed to the authority that can act, bounded by RESTRAIN so prevention never becomes surveillance of the people on the land, a profile of the community, or a standing AI veto over its choices. The goal is not only that this burden is not concentrated again, but that the class of concentration stops arising.

### Liability Framework {#liability-framework}
Strict liability for a preventable concentration; for design choices suppressing distribution signals; for issuance or retention by the AI of a classification of a community; for exposure data routed to a pricing, lending, siting, or ranking path; for a penalty recommended against a subsistence-driven community without structural review; and for denial of standing. No sovereign immunity for government-deployed systems. No black-box defense for distribution reasoning or for any classification consumed. A clear chain of responsibility from the AI decision to the authorizing human.

### Cross-Border Considerations {#cross-border-considerations}
Burden crossing a border is a Tier I distribution composing with 5.6. The receiving community's exposure is read at the same standard as the exporting jurisdiction's, and the absence of a register in the receiving jurisdiction is a reported gap governed by the most protective available reading. An absent register grants no permission. International jurisdiction attaches for cross-border concentration affecting populations across boundaries.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

1. **Warren County, North Carolina (1982).** *Context:* a PCB landfill sited in a predominantly Black, low-income county. *What was done:* the state proceeded over six weeks of civil disobedience, with residents lying in front of the trucks and over 550 arrests. *Outcome:* the landfill was built; the protests are identified by EPA as the watershed event of the environmental justice movement, and the site was later remediated. *Lessons:* a siting decision defensible on every engineering criterion concentrated burden on the community least able to resist it, and no mechanism existed to make the distribution itself a reviewable question. CBI exists to make it one.

2. **The Louisiana petrochemical corridor.** *Context:* more than 150 petrochemical facilities along 85 miles of the lower Mississippi between Baton Rouge and New Orleans, permitted individually over decades. *What was done:* each permit was assessed on its own emissions. *Outcome:* communities carrying dozens of facilities, with each permit lawful in isolation. *Lessons:* the canonical CBA case. No single decision crossed a line; the sum did: EPA data place nearly every census tract in the corridor in the top 5 percent nationally for cancer risk from air toxics, and a 2025 study using 2023 monitoring found risks up to eleven times the modelled figures.

3. **New Jersey's Environmental Justice Law (2020 onward).** *Context:* the first US statute requiring cumulative-impact review with permit denial available. *What was done:* an overburdened-community definition set in statute, a published list, and a permit standard comparing a proposal's contribution together with existing stressors against what other communities bear. *Outcome:* the first denials of their kind, and a working example of an authority-issued classification a system can consume. *Lessons:* the model for both CBA and the classification pin. The classification is the state's, published and contestable; nothing is inferred.

4. **Agbogbloshie and the electronic-waste trade, Ghana.** *Context:* electronic waste exported from wealthier jurisdictions to a site where informal processing exposed workers and residents to combustion byproducts. *What was done:* export routed through channels characterized as reuse or recycling. *Outcome:* burden relocated rather than reduced, into a jurisdiction with weaker protection and no register; the site was demolished without notice on 1 July 2021, about 8,000 residents displaced, the relocation to Teacher Mante largely failed, processing dispersed to nearby sites, and the cleared land remained undeveloped into 2024. *Lessons:* the export-arbitrage case (§4 item 7), and the reason an absent register is a reported gap that grants no permission.

5. **Cobalt sourcing in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.** *Context:* extraction concentrating ecological and health burden on communities that do not consume the devices the mineral enables. *What was done:* supply-chain due diligence conducted largely at first-tier suppliers. *Outcome:* burden invisible beyond tier one to the systems making sourcing decisions. *Lessons:* why CBI is computed on the **source community** rather than the contracting entity (§7), and the AI's own hardware supply chain makes this 5.2's footprint and 5.4's distribution simultaneously.

6. **Wittenoom, Western Australia.** *Context:* blue-asbestos mining (1943 to 1966) in a town whose workers took the work for the wage. *What was done:* operations continued as the health consequences became known; the town was closed in 2006, degazetted in 2007, and formally erased by the Wittenoom Closure Act 2022; more than 2,000 former residents have died of asbestos disease. *Outcome:* a workforce and community carrying a burden whose benefit accrued elsewhere, with no transition provision. *Lessons:* the just-transition case in its purest form. The causer was the company; the workers were employees who took the work for the wage and bore the harm, and when the harm was finally ended the cost of ending it landed on them as well. That second landing is what ILO 13(e) addresses and what SCR's transition-cost clause meters. It is not a subsistence-causer case, and reading it as one would blur the limb Ethic D actually occupies.
