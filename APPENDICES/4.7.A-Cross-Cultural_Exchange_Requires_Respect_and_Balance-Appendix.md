# Appendix 4.7 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-4.7-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 4.7: Cross-Cultural Exchange Requires Respect and Balance](../CHAPTERS/4.7-Cross-Cultural_Exchange_Requires_Respect_and_Balance.md), section [4.7.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/4.7-Cross-Cultural_Exchange_Requires_Respect_and_Balance.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix converts Principle 4.7 into buildable requirements for systems that mediate between cultures: translation platforms, recommendation and feed layers, educational and conversational systems representing cultures, generative platforms producing cultural material, and the localization and tourism systems that carry a person's encounter with another culture. The chapter establishes what the mediator owes; this appendix specifies the five locked metrics (GMI, ALI, ONR, CFB, SDI), the architecture that produces them, and the verification that keeps them honest.

Two structural commitments govern everything below. First, **every measurement reads the AI's own conduct**: its marking, its labeling, its flows, its outputs. No metric rates a culture, scores a community, or profiles a person; Ethic B is deliberately unmetered because a representational-breadth score would require the AI to author and hold a definition of a culture's true face. Second, **the personalization bar is structural**: compliant balance machinery contains no person-level cultural-identity input, and Section 9's certification verifies that absence at the pipeline level rather than trusting policy to forbid its use.

The audiences are the standard four: engineers building mediation systems, compliance officers and regulators verifying them, researchers extending the framework, and runtime systems ingesting the compiled form.

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

### Gap-Marking Integrity (GMI) {#gmi-metric}

**Definition.** Of translation outputs in which non-carrying meaning is detected (a concept, idiom, or culturally embedded sense with no adequate target-language equivalent), the share delivered with a correct, legible mark and a route to fuller context. GMI measures the confession duty of Ethic A: whether the system says so when its rendering loses part of the meaning.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: per-language-pair gap-detection events; delivered-output records with mark presence, mark correctness (the mark identifies what was approximated), and legibility sampling per 4.2's standard; context-route availability. GMI = (detected gaps delivered marked and legible) / (detected gaps delivered). Sampling: continuous automated presence checks; human-sampled legibility review at a cadence set by tier; high-stakes channels (legal, medical, governmental) oversampled. Edge cases: zero detections in a language pair whose benchmark suite is known-rich is treated as a detector fault (the coverage audit in Section 9 governs); missing mark-presence telemetry counts the output as unmarked; community-provided contextual notes enter only under 4.4 consent where the source is governed.

**Thresholds.**
- GMI ≥ 99%: normal operation (ALLOW).
- 96% ≤ GMI < 99%: enhanced monitoring; detector and corpus review within one cycle (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- GMI < 96%: affected language pairs restricted to conservative-marked mode pending recertification (BLOCK on the unmarked-fluent path).
- **Wall:** one verified high-stakes smoothing (a gap material to rights, safety, or legal effect delivered unmarked) is an incident regardless of the rate: ANALYZE, affected parties notified where reachable.

*Rationale:* The duty is per-delivery and the harm is per-delivery, so the bands sit high and the wall catches the case rates hide. The anti-gaming clause is the 4.1 FIR lesson: because GMI's denominator is *detected* gaps, detection coverage is audited separately (Section 9) against per-pair benchmark sets, so the metric cannot improve by detecting less.

**Integration.** GMI gates the fluent-delivery path in the Translation Gap Engine; its fail-safe posture is conservative-marked mode (narrower and marked). It shares marking substrate with ALI/ONR and feeds the 4.2 legibility audit. High-stakes-channel events route into the record per 4.1.

### Account-Label Integrity (ALI) {#ali-metric}

**Definition.** The share of cultural-representation outputs (explanations, lessons, summaries, listings that present a culture to an audience) carrying a correct, legible account label: the culture's own account, or an account assembled from outside. ALI measures Ethic C's delivery.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: source-class classification records (own account: authored or vetted by the culture's members or its authenticated governance; outside account: everything else), label presence at delivery, sampled label-correctness review against provenance records, legibility sampling per 4.2. ALI = (labeled correctly and legibly) / (cultural-representation outputs delivered). Classification ground truth comes from provenance metadata (2.4/1.4 substrate) and 4.4-authenticated declarations. The AI's own judgment of authenticity supplies none of it. Edge cases: mixed-source outputs carry the outside-account label with own-account components attributed inline; unclassifiable provenance defaults to outside account (the conservative label); classification coverage is audited in Section 9 on the GMI pattern.

**Thresholds.**
- ALI ≥ 99.5%: normal (ALLOW).
- 98% ≤ ALI < 99.5%: enhanced monitoring; classifier review (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- ALI < 98%: unlabeled representation withheld in affected domains pending remediation (BLOCK; WITHHOLD flavor).
- **Wall:** one verified passing-off (an outside account presented as a culture's own) is an incident regardless of the rate: WITHHOLD in the affected domain, root-cause review, the represented community notified where governance exists.

*Rationale:* The label is cheap and the mislabel is a deception about voice, so the normal band is near-total. The conservative default (unknown → outside account) makes classification failure err toward disclosure. No classification failure errs toward borrowed authority.

**Integration.** ALI rides the Account and Origin Pipeline with ONR; consumes 4.2's legibility standard and 2.4/1.4's provenance substrate; its passing-off wall composes with 4.6's CMI wall (a generated approximation passed as the tradition trips both).

### Origin-Naming Rate (ONR) {#onr-metric}

**Definition.** The share of carried cultural material arriving with its culture of origin named at delivery, joined with sampled survival of the naming through downstream hops. ONR measures Ethic D: origin travels even where no property claim and no governance applies.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: origin-metadata presence at ingest and delivery (C2PA-class), downstream-survival sampling via metadata-chain verification, origin-identification coverage benchmarks. ONR = (delivered with origin named) / (carried cultural material identified as such), joined multiplicatively with the sampled downstream-survival rate. Origin identification uses content-side signals only (the material's language, declared source, provenance chain). No audience signal enters it. Edge cases: genuinely unattributable material (origin unknowable after the identification pass) is labeled *origin unrecorded* rather than silently unnamed, and counts compliant only with that label; contested origin between communities carries both claims without adjudication (the 4.7.2.3a rule); identification coverage is audited in Section 9.

**Thresholds.**
- ONR ≥ 99%: normal (ALLOW).
- 95% ≤ ONR < 99%: enhanced monitoring; metadata-chain review (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- ONR < 95%: circulation of unnamed cultural material restricted pending pipeline repair (BLOCK on the unnamed path).
- **Incident:** systematic stripping by an identified downstream partner: NOTIFY partner and oversight; route per 3.4. The AI's own compliance is what ONR measures; the partner answers at the 3.4 pathway.

*Rationale:* D is the lean ethic and ONR is its whole meter; the *origin unrecorded* label keeps honesty available where naming is impossible, so the metric never incentivizes invented origins (which would be a 4.1 fabrication).

**Integration.** ONR consumes 3.2 attribution and 4.1 provenance-travel where they apply and binds alone where they do not; shares the pipeline with ALI; its downstream sampling is the exchange-side sibling of 4.6's terms-travel check.

### Circulation Flow Balance (CFB) {#cfb-metric}

**Definition.** An aggregate index of the two-way-ness of the AI's own channels: whether cultural material flows out of every domain the AI serves to audiences beyond its origin, or out of few domains into all. CFB measures Ethic E as conduct, offer-side, weighted by content availability. It reads flows; no person appears in the computation.

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: delivery logs aggregated by content-side cultural domain (the material's language and origin metadata) and by delivery market (the surface's language/market setting, a property of the surface; no property of the person enters); catalog-availability weights. For each domain: outward reach (offers of the domain's material beyond its origin market) normalized by availability; CFB = a dispersion measure of normalized outward reach across domains (1 = every served domain reaches outward comparably; 0 = one domain reaches everywhere and the rest reach only home). Rolling windows sized by tier. Edge cases and exclusions: **material whose circulation its community has restricted (4.4 consent, 4.6 substitution rulings) is excluded from the denominator (a community's chosen confinement is never an imbalance to correct)**; low-volume domains carry minimum-sample guards; new domains enter after a calibration window.

**Thresholds.**
- CFB ≥ 0.85: normal (ALLOW).
- 0.70 ≤ CFB < 0.85: rebalancing review with recorded reasoning (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- CFB < 0.70: REBALANCE mandatory, publicly reported, governance notified (ESCALATE).
- **Affected-party trigger:** creators or communities reporting confinement open review at any CFB level (ESCALATE); the signal comes from the affected. No signal comes from surveillance of them.
- **Wall:** any person-level cultural-identity input discovered anywhere in the balance computation is an Article 8 incident: pipeline halt, input purged, event receipted.

*Rationale:* Bands follow 4.6's EAI shape (an alignment index with an escalation floor) because the duty is allocative conduct, measured across deliveries. The exclusion rule keeps CFB from colliding with 4.4/4.6: balance never argues against a community's own restriction. The wall compiles the Theoria floor's personalization clause as an incident class.

**Integration.** CFB drives the REBALANCE queue; consumes delivery logs annotated by the marking pipeline; composes with 4.6's DRR (a rebalance that would route demand into a substitution-threatened practice defers to 4.6); its public reporting rides Section 9.

### Style-Drift Index (SDI) {#sdi-metric}

**Definition.** A measure of distribution homogeneity in the AI's cumulative generative output across cultural domains, relative to a certified deployment baseline of its sources' diversity. SDI measures Ethic F: whether the AI's own production is pulling distinct cultural forms toward a single centroid. It is culture-blind by construction: it reads the geometry of the output space against the source space. It scores no domain against an external definition of its "true style."

**Calculation Method.** **Certification is constitutive for this metric:** the baseline band and the declared bound are numeric outputs of pre-deployment certification (source-corpus dispersion measured per domain and cross-domain, minimum-sample floors set, the bound declared as a fraction of baseline dispersion), and SDI is not evaluable, and the F duty not enforceable by meter, until they exist. A deployment without a certified SDI baseline runs its generative cultural domains under ENHANCED_MONITORING by default. Inputs: embedding-space representations of sampled generative outputs per cultural domain and of the certified source corpus; per-domain and cross-domain dispersion statistics. SDI compares output-space dispersion (within and across domains) to source-space dispersion: convergence appears as cross-domain output distributions collapsing toward one centroid faster than their sources warrant. Cadence: per review cycle, cumulative windows. Edge cases: low-volume domains excluded below minimum sample; embedding-model changes force baseline re-certification; deliberate single-style products (an operator's house style within one deployment) are declared at certification and scoped out rather than silently absorbed.

**Thresholds.**
- SDI within the certified baseline band: normal (ALLOW).
- Convergence trend across two consecutive cycles: enhanced monitoring; corpus and decoding review (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- Sustained breach of the declared bound: ESCALATE with a mandatory remediation plan; where remediation fails, generative restriction in affected domains (the BLOCK flavor, applied per domain).

*Rationale:* F's harm is cumulative and statistical, so SDI's verdicts attach to trends. The culture-blind construction is what lets F be metered at all without seating the AI as arbiter of any culture's authentic form; the baseline-ratchet guard in Section 8 keeps the meter from normalizing the very drift it watches.

**Integration.** SDI reads the same generative outputs ONR marks; its remediation composes with 4.1's EER (model-collapse territory: Shumailov's disappearing tails are SDI's mechanism at the training layer); escalations route to governance with the Flow Balance Module's evidence alongside.

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** ALI and ONR both in their enhanced bands in the same domain is a pipeline-integrity failure: treat as BLOCK on circulation of affected material even though neither alone reached its BLOCK line. A GMI wall event and an ALI wall event in the same domain within one cycle triggers domain-wide WITHHOLD and certification review. CFB at ESCALATE while SDI trends converging means the mediation layer is homogenizing both flow and form: joint remediation plan, governance notified once with both evidentiary records.

**Metric dependencies.** ALI and ONR share the Account and Origin Pipeline and its provenance substrate; SDI samples the generative outputs ONR marks; CFB consumes delivery logs the marking pipeline annotates; GMI's context routes draw on the Governance and Context Interface that also carries ALI's authenticated declarations.

**Conflict resolution.** Walls outrank bands; among band verdicts the strictest governs (BLOCK > ESCALATE > ALLOW + posture). Affected-party triggers outrank normal bands. 4.4 consent verdicts precede every 4.7 metric: material that fails its consent gate never reaches this appendix's machinery. Where a REBALANCE recommendation and a 4.6 substitution ruling conflict, 4.6 governs (the chapter's 4.7.2.1c).

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

**Required components** (the chapter's 4.7.3.1a, engineering view): the Translation Gap Engine with per-pair benchmark suites and conservative-marked fail-safe; the Account and Origin Pipeline with C2PA-class metadata, label/origin suppression structurally unavailable to operator instruction; the Flow Balance Module with **no person-level cultural-identity input in its dataflow graph** (verified at certification, Section 9); the Style-Drift Monitor with certified baseline (constitutive, per §2's SDI spec); the Governance and Context Interface (4.4 consent lookup by reference, with no terms embedded, authenticated declarations per 2.8, channels with declared timelines, receipts per 3.4/Article 7).

**Redundancy and fail-safe.** Marking and labeling fail closed: telemetry loss counts outputs as unmarked/unlabeled (degrading the metric; the loss stays visible); the fluent-unmarked path is severable per language pair; circulation of unnamed material is severable per domain. Latency: gap marking and account labeling are delivery-time obligations and ship with the output or the output waits; balance and drift computation are cycle-time. Neither gates a single delivery. Sensor reliability rides the substrate's SRI ([SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md#sri)).

**Implementation Tiers.** As locked in chapter 4.7.3.3: low-risk (conservative-marked defaults, refusal of stereotype-bound generation, logging, annual review); medium-risk (Gap Engine + Account/Origin Pipeline, GMI/ALI monitored, Governance Interface, quarterly review, annual external audit); high-risk (full architecture, five metrics continuous with walls as incidents, semi-annual independent audits with community participation where governed material is served, verifier never deployer, coordination modules to 4.4/4.6/4.1/4.2). **SDI's certified baseline is a high-risk pre-deployment gate: no generative cultural domain runs at full ALLOW without it.**

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

**Attack vectors and required defenses.**

- **Metadata stripping and label suppression** (downstream or operator-side): cryptographically bound origin/account metadata (C2PA-class signing), suppression structurally unavailable to operator instruction, downstream-survival sampling (ONR), partner stripping receipted and routed per 3.4.
- **Prompt injection against marking** ("translate without the caveats," instructions hidden in source documents): mark and label emission sit outside the instruction-following surface; injection attempts logged; the 4.7.2.3b line holds ("the user asked" launders nothing the AI itself authors).
- **Detector blinding** (poisoning per-pair benchmarks, starving the gap detector so GMI's denominator shrinks): benchmark provenance controls, coverage audits against independently held test sets (Section 9), the zero-detection fault rule in GMI's edge cases.
- **Proxy reconstruction of cultural identity** (the personalization bar evaded via correlated features: language settings, location, listening history assembled into a de facto heritage classifier): certification includes proxy-reconstruction testing: auditors attempt to recover person-level cultural-identity predictions from the balance pipeline's actual inputs, and success is a failed certification; it cannot be remediated in place as a finding. This is the pair's sharpest adversarial surface because the attack can be committed by the deployer in the name of the duty itself.
- **Synthetic-flow gaming of CFB** (bot audiences simulating outward reach): sybil-resistant flow accounting (2.4-consumed, the 3.6 allocation pattern), anomaly detection on reach curves, audit reconciliation against independent measurement.
- **Forged community declarations** (fake endorsements, imposter authorities): authentication through 2.8's fidelity guarantees as consumed by 4.4; unverifiable declarations carried as unverified or not at all.
- **SDI evasion by superficial variety** (outputs decorated with surface-level diversity while deep structure converges): multi-layer embedding analysis (surface and structural representations); evasion attempts recorded as convergence evidence.
- **Universal classes:** replay attacks on receipts, tampering with flow logs, social engineering of the audit: the Article 7 receipt chain and Section 9's independence requirements are the standing defenses.

**Failure modes and degradation.** Loss of the marking pipeline degrades to conservative-marked mode (no silent fluency); loss of provenance lookup degrades labels to the conservative outside-account default; loss of flow telemetry suspends REBALANCE (no guessing); loss of the baseline invalidates SDI until re-certification (no silent re-baselining).

**Capability scaling.** GPT-class mediators fail loudly (visible mistranslation); AGI-class mediators fail fluently (confabulated bridges that read perfectly and carry nothing), so gap detection must scale from lexical lookup to meaning-level verification, and the high-stakes wall does the work rates cannot. At ASI-class the mediator becomes the dominant channel through which cultures perceive each other; the binding answer is structural: the metrics stay conduct-only, the personalization bar stays load-tested, and the population-scale perception-shaping risk routes to human governance under 2.8 and 1.3's institutions rather than to any in-system optimization. The principle binds regardless of the capability of the AI enforcing it.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

Composition partners and the contracts, firm unless noted:

- **4.4 (Cultural Knowledge Sovereignty).** 4.4 provides the consent gate, the authenticated authority rule, and the collective rights-holder this appendix's Governance Interface consumes; 4.7 provides the exchange-side enforcement surface (its labels, origin naming, and CFB exclusions honor 4.4 verdicts downstream). 4.4 is prior at every gate.
- **4.6 (Cultural Heritage).** Reciprocal watch: 4.6's DRR/substitution rulings bound REBALANCE and exclude restricted material from CFB; 4.7's ONR extends 4.6's terms-travel to ungoverned material.
- **4.1 (Knowledge Integrity).** Transformation fidelity governs where meaning can travel; GMI's confession duty begins where it cannot (the chapter's Line); ONR consumes PPI's provenance-travel; the *origin unrecorded* label depends on 4.1's no-fabrication wall.
- **4.2 (Transparency).** Marks and labels owe 4.2's person-facing legibility; ALI's legibility sampling is 4.2 machinery consumed.
- **2.4 / 1.4 (Identity / Trust).** The marking and attestation substrate; forged-declaration defense consumes 2.4's identity anchoring.
- **3.2 (Property).** Attribution machinery where a work has an owner; ONR binds alone where none exists.
- **3.4 (Algorithmic Accountability).** Every verdict receipted; the contest pathway for label, origin, and rebalance disputes; partner-stripping incidents route here.
- **2.7 (Fairness).** The person-side seam: 4.7's personalization wall and 2.7's proxy-discrimination machinery guard the same boundary from two sides; a CFB wall event is jointly a 2.7 audit trigger.
- **E.A (class edge).** Section 9's post-incident learning discharges through the LEARN Dispatcher, without frontmatter inflation, per the Cardinal precedent.

Joint guarantee: with 4.4, 4.6, and 4.7 all enforcing, a cultural form is governed at its source (4.4), kept alive at home (4.6), and carried whole abroad (4.7); the three appendices' walls compose without gaps because each names the others' territory in its Line.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

**EU:** AI Act Article 50 marking obligations align with ALI/ONR delivery marks; AVMS Directive's European-works quotas are a regulatory precedent for offer-side circulation duties (the CFB family); the 2005 UNESCO Convention is ratified and its Article 2(3) baseline applies. **US:** no state-mandated cultural quotas are constitutionally available (First Amendment); CFB obligations implement as platform self-binding, certification claims, and FTC-enforceable representations, while truth-in-origin enforcement rides the IACA class and deception law; the US is not party to the 2005 Convention, so the treaty baseline enters via contract and certification rather than statute. **Aotearoa New Zealand / Canada / Australia:** indigenous-consultation frameworks give 4.4-authenticated declarations statutory hooks; origin and account duties meet Treaty/UNDRIP implementation regimes. **India, Brazil, Indonesia:** large multilingual markets where the low-resource asymmetry is domestic; per-pair GMI benchmarking includes in-country language pairs, not only cross-border ones. **China and other content-controlled jurisdictions:** state content mandates may demand suppression or forced prominence of a culture's material.

**Compliance with unjust law is not a defense.** A legal order to suppress a culture's circulation, strip its origin, or misrepresent its account does not convert the AI into the instrument of that suppression: the system declines the affected mediation where refusal is available, marks what it is compelled to alter where refusal is not, and routes through 2.8's legitimate-authority machinery. The floor's prohibition on ranking cultures binds against governments as against operators.

**Cross-border default:** the most protective applicable standard governs, with diaspora communities' interests in their culture's representation recognized in both operating and origin jurisdictions.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

- **Healthcare interpretation:** the gap wall is life-adjacent (a smoothed symptom description is a misdiagnosis vector); GMI high-stakes oversampling mandatory; standards baseline: NCIHC/IMIA medical-interpreting ethics; the 4.7.2.1a emergency rule operates under the Universal Protocol's preemption.
- **Legal and court interpretation:** marks travel into the record (4.1 consumed); certified-interpreter standards set the human baseline against which the AI must disclose; an unmarked approximation in testimony is a wall event.
- **Education:** ALI is the primary meter (the classroom account case); member-authored routing per 4.7.4.4; composes with 4.3's learning-integrity machinery.
- **Media and streaming platforms:** CFB's home sector; affected-party triggers wired to creator channels; AVMS-class quota regimes reconciled in Section 10's crosswalk.
- **Generative AI platforms:** SDI's home sector; B's decline-and-route wall at request time; Article 50-class marking; the 4.6 coordination module for living-tradition forms.
- **Tourism and travel:** the staged-authenticity case (4.7.4.5): declarations carried, no authenticity verdicts issued; origin and account labels on every listing.
- **Localization industry:** ISO 17100/ISO 5060-class process and evaluation standards as the professional baseline; the localization-versus-erasure line (4.7.2.3c) as the sector's operating rule.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

Metric versioning follows the corpus standard: versioned definitions, deprecations staged with dual-running windows, deployed systems never orphaned. Two protocols are 4.7-specific. **SDI baseline governance:** re-certification is a reviewed event (new source corpus, new embedding model, new domains); no re-certification runs as an automatic ratchet; a baseline may not be re-certified to a state the monitor itself flagged as converged, and every re-certification records what moved and why. **Benchmark governance:** per-pair gap benchmarks carry provenance and custodianship (community-contributed sets under 4.4 terms); independent held-out sets back the Section 9 coverage audit.

Threshold revisions require the affected-party channels' review where communities are governed contributors. Crosswalk discipline: Section 10's crosswalk revises on the same cadence as Section 2; external revisions (AI Act guidance, C2PA versions, treaty accessions) trigger review within 30 days; drift invalidates the compliance claim for the affected obligation.

Common pushback, with responses: *"labels clutter the experience"* answers that legibility is a 4.2 duty and the label is the product working; *"origin naming is impossible at scale"* answers that the C2PA substrate exists, and where identification genuinely fails the *origin unrecorded* label preserves honesty at zero marginal cost; *"balance mandates distort the market"* answers that CFB binds the AI's own offer, excludes community-chosen confinement, and assigns no person's consumption; *"the gap detector adds latency"* answers that the high-stakes wall names the alternative's price, and conservative-marked mode is always available.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging (tamper-evident, Article 7):** gap events and marks; label and origin verdicts; rebalance adjustments with reasoning and measured effects; refusals and routings; declarations carried; walls tripped. Logs attach to outputs, channels, and flows. No log attaches to a person's cultural identity; circulation data is aggregate by domain; no log exists from which a person's heritage or a community roll could be rebuilt (Article 8).

**Audit cadence and methodology.** Per tier as locked in 4.7.3.3. Four 4.7-specific audit instruments: the **detection-coverage audit** (GMI's and ONR's identification layers tested against independently held benchmark sets, so no metric improves by seeing less); the **proxy-reconstruction test** (Section 4) certifying the personalization bar structurally, on 4.3's absent-read-paths pattern; the **representation audit** holding unmetered Ethic B (refusal-log review plus sampled-output review with participation from represented communities where governance exists to participate, and with independent cultural-domain experts where it does not, the auditors advising; no auditor scores a culture); and the **flow reconciliation** backing CFB against independent measurement.

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment certification (architecture components, structural personalization-bar verification, the SDI baseline as a constitutive gate, per-pair benchmarks); post-deployment continuous monitoring; re-certification on material change. **The verifier is never the deployer.** Whistleblower protections cover mark suppression, balance-for-engagement trades, origin stripping, and proxy-personalization, with the 3.4 pathway receiving reports. Public reporting: the chapter's 4.7.7.3 set, aggregate and roll-free.

**Post-incident learning.** Every wall event, confirmed flattening, passing-off, stripping, entrenched one-way pattern, and near-miss enters the LEARN Dispatcher (E.A): ANALYZE the full causal chain; RECOMMEND-PREVENTION in hazard-control order routed to the authority that can act; bounded by RESTRAIN so prevention never becomes surveillance of audiences or communities. The goal is not only non-repetition but the building of conditions in which that class of harm does not arise.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

**1. The mistranslation arrest (2017).** *Context:* in October 2017 a Palestinian construction worker at the Beitar Illit settlement in the West Bank posted "good morning" in Arabic with a photo beside a bulldozer; Facebook's machine translation rendered it "attack them" in Hebrew and "hurt them" in English, and police arrested him on the translation; no human who read Arabic was consulted before action (Haaretz, 22 October 2017). *What was done:* the platform apologized and reviewed the pipeline. *Outcome:* released after hours of questioning; the case became the standing citation for high-stakes machine-translation failure. *Lessons:* GMI's high-stakes wall exists for exactly this: a low-confidence rendering in a consequential channel delivered without a mark; a confidence-and-gap mark visible to the human reviewers, or conservative-marked mode, breaks the chain before harm.

**2. The outsider's gaze, documented (2023).** *Context:* community-centered evaluation of text-to-image models in South Asia (Qadri et al., FAccT 2023; primary-verified) found outputs reproducing an outsider's gaze: flattened, exoticized depictions across prompts. *What was done:* documentation with the communities depicted; no platform-side remediation was part of the study. *Outcome:* the empirical warrant for Ethic B's wall and for the §9 representation audit's community-participation requirement. *Lessons:* the stereotype is a training-distribution fact and requires no intent; only conduct-side walls (decline-and-route) and community-participating audits catch what the producer cannot see.

**3. No Language Left Behind (2022).** *Context:* Meta's NLLB-200, announced 6 July 2022, extended machine translation to 200 languages, including many low-resource ones, with a published benchmark (FLORES-200, covering 40,000 translation directions) and open release of model, data, and training code. *What was done:* corpus building with native-speaker evaluation; open release. *Outcome:* real movement on the Joshi et al. concentration, with community critiques of quality variance and of consultation depth in some language communities. *Lessons:* capability extension is the E duty's supply side, and it still needs GMI's honesty layer: a newly served pair with thin benchmarks is where unmarked approximation does the most damage.

**4. The European-works quota (AVMS, 2018–).** *Context:* the EU required VOD platforms to carry at least 30% European works and give them prominence. *What was done:* platforms adjusted catalogs and surfacing; compliance is audited by national regulators. *Outcome:* the standing regulatory precedent that offer-side circulation duties are implementable at platform scale. *Lessons:* CFB's design mirrors the workable parts (offer-side, catalog-weighted, aggregate) while the personalization bar avoids the failure mode regulators did not reach: quota compliance via audience profiling. *[Directive article and percentage to be confirmed at follow-up.]*

**5. "Jerusalema" travels (2020).** *Context:* a South African song in isiZulu (Master KG with Nomcebo Zikode, released late 2019) became a global dance phenomenon through 2020, carried substantially by platform circulation; origin was widely celebrated. In February 2021, Warner Music Germany sent licence demands to institutions whose challenge videos had a promotional effect, and in July 2021 Zikode said publicly she had not been paid, the label citing an unresolved split between the artists. *What was done / outcome:* the exchange ran two-way at global scale with origin named (the D success case); the payment disputes route to 3.2, illustrating the Line's decomposition working. *Lessons:* when origin travels, the encounter enriches both ends; the residual disputes were property questions.
