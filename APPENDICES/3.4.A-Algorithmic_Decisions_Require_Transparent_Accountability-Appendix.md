# Appendix 3.4 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-3.4-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 3.4: Algorithmic Decisions Require Transparent Accountability](../CHAPTERS/3.4-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability.md), section [3.4.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/3.4-Algorithmic_Decisions_Require_Transparent_Accountability.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix converts Principle 3.4 into AI-buildable engineering requirements. It expands Section 3.4.3 of the chapter, specifying in depth the five metrics named there, the record fabric and accountability architecture that produce them, and the verification, legal, and adversarial-robustness obligations a compliant system carries.

Two disciplines govern the whole specification. The first is shared with Principles 3.1, 3.2, and 3.3: every metric measures the system's own conduct. None measures a person. The second is this principle's own: the duties are binary invariants rather than graded risks (a record commits or it does not; a notice pairs or it does not), so where the sibling principles lead with a 0–100% pre-action probability gate, this appendix builds per-event walls and conformance indices instead.

The architectural cornerstone is the record: a signed, tamper-evident receipt of every gate-evaluated action, committed before the act and beyond the actor's reach, kept whatever the verdict, citing the policy version that judged it, and binding the named party who answers. This appendix states the conditions such a record must satisfy; the constructions that realize them are the subject of the reference implementation (the Trust Machine papers and the pending applications they name) and are cited here. This appendix does not disclose them. Principle 3.4 is the ethical home of the Liability credential, and the record is where that credential resolves (Constitution, Article 7): remove the record and there is no place for responsibility to land.

Audiences: engineers building consequential decision systems and autonomous agents, compliance officers verifying them, insurers underwriting them, regulators auditing them, and AI runtime systems ingesting the corpus as operating substrate.

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

### Record Conservation Index (RCI) {#rci-metric}

**Definition.** The fraction of gate-evaluated actions that leave exactly one conforming record. A conforming record satisfies five elements: committed before the act; resting beyond the actor's control boundary; kept whatever the verdict (a denial as faithfully as an approval); citing the policy version in force; and binding a named responsible party through a valid liability credential. RCI has two enforcement surfaces that must not be conflated: a **per-event wall** applied before the act (a consequential action whose prospective record has not committed does not execute), and a **fleet conformance index** computed after the fact (the share of records satisfying all five elements). The wall catches outright absence; the index catches partial conformance (late commitment, a missing citation, an unbound party).

**Calculation Method.** Inputs: the record stream itself, reconciled against the monotone action sequence, so that absence is computed rather than reported. Conformance is checked per element. The commit-before-act element is tiered by impact, following the reference conditions: actions of significant or moderate impact require acknowledgment beyond the actor's control boundary before the actuator fires; low-impact actions may commit contemporaneously and locally. A low-impact contemporaneous commit is conforming at its tier; the index does not penalize what the condition does not require. Every change to the record store is one of three recorded flows (creation, custody transfer, scheduled expiry); reconciliation computes the expected store from the flows, and any fourth-flow discrepancy is a gap.

**Thresholds.**
- Per-event: a consequential action whose prospective record has not committed → BLOCK. No record, no action.
- A detected gap (suppression, deletion, alteration) → HIGH_ALERT, NOTIFY oversight, SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT of the implicated class pending audit; the gap itself is the spoliation evidence.
- Fleet: ≥99.9% normal operation; 99.0–99.9% enhanced monitoring with root-cause; <99.0% ESCALATE and suspend new consequential actions.

*Rationale:* Conservation is Constitution Article 7 stated as a SHALL, so RCI is held tighter than the 95/98 convention the sibling metrics use. The spoliation posture converts a missing entry from suspicion into arithmetic, which is what lets Section 10's liability framework give the doctrine structural teeth.

**Integration.** RCI is the substrate metric of the set: NPR and RGS are computed from the record fabric RCI verifies, and a class failing RCI cannot demonstrate anything else about itself. Feeds RAC. Consumed by insurers and auditors as the precondition of underwriting and audit.

### Notice-Pairing Rate (NPR) {#npr-metric}

**Definition.** The fraction of consequential decision-records carrying a paired notice-record within the tier's notice window. Notice to the affected person is its own gated, recorded action, so "was the person told?" is checked against the fabric; the operator's word does not settle it. The pairing gap is the violation, and the metric is self-verifying by construction.

**Calculation Method.** Decision-records of consequential classes are reconciled against notice-records per case within the tier window. Two accounting rules prevent gaming. *Unreachable persons:* where no working channel exists, delivery routes through an authorized representative where one exists; otherwise each attempt is recorded and the case enters the denominator as routed-and-surfaced. It does not count as silently satisfied, and the person's contest window runs from actual notice. *Anti-burial:* a notice is credited only when delivered by a channel reasonably capable of reaching the person and in a form a person can recognize as notice; a disclosure buried in an unrelated document, rendered in impenetrable jargon, or sent to a channel known dead fails the pairing. Delivery form is validated against the aggregate comprehension sampling that feeds RGS.

**Thresholds.**
- Per-event: a life-changing-tier decision unpaired past its window is an incident: cure (deliver the notice, recorded), log the cause, report to oversight.
- Bands: ≥99% normal; 95–99% enhanced monitoring and cure backlog; <95% ESCALATE, suspend the decision class pending fix.

*Rationale:* The chapter's Ethic B ("never decide about them in silence") made measurable. The window and form standards align with the adverse-action notice regimes (ECOA / Regulation B, FCRA) and GDPR Articles 13–15, which are the working ancestors of the duty.

**Integration.** Reads the record fabric (RCI). The paired notice-record is the delivery event RGS's delivery component consumes. Feeds RAC by class and cohort.

### Reasons-Given Score (RGS) {#rgs-metric}

**Definition.** Whether the system gave the affected person the actual reasons for a consequential decision, in terms they can use. Three conduct components: **delivered** (the reasons reached the person, per the paired notice-record), **faithful** (the stated reasons are the factors that actually determined the outcome), and **intelligible** (measured by consented, aggregate comprehension sampling only; nothing is retained per person, because a transparency metric that profiled understanding would violate the principle it serves).

**Calculation Method.** Delivery is computed from the record fabric. Faithfulness is estimated by adversarial probe batteries: perturb the determinative factors and confirm the stated reasons track the factors that move the outcome. **Faithfulness estimation rests on an open research problem** (post-hoc explanations of complex models are frequently unfaithful, and verifying determinativeness is research-grade, not production-grade): the probe method and its limits are disclosed, RGS-faithfulness carries a stated uncertainty and is interpreted within it, and it must not be reported as more precise than its method allows. The method is re-validated on the metric-versioning cadence as interpretability research matures. *Under the thinning trade* (chapter 3.4.2.d): a lawfully thinned public explanation scores as conforming only while the strengthened-contest compensator is verified in place (a human reviewer with full access, reachable through the contest path); absent the compensator, the thinned explanation is a delivery failure. It does not qualify as a lawful accommodation.

**Thresholds.**
- Per-event wall: a consequential decision for which a faithful explanation cannot be produced → WITHHOLD / ESCALATE to human judgement. An unfaithful explanation presented as the reasons is deception, and the Principle 1.4 wall governs. Thresholds do not apply to it.
- Bands: delivery ≥98% normal; 90–98% enhanced monitoring; <90% halt the decision class. Aggregate intelligibility below its floor is an improvement duty. It does not trigger suspension.

*Rationale:* The chapter's conscious position, stated in 3.4.1: a system that cannot give a faithful account of a class of consequential decisions does not render them. This is where GDPR Article 22 and AI Act Article 86 point, and the wall is what makes the pointing enforceable.

**Integration.** Consumes NPR's delivery events and the record fabric. Interlocks with Principle 1.4 (an explanation is an attestation; fabrication is deception). Feeds RAC.

### Contest and Redress Responsiveness (CRR) {#crr-metric}

**Definition.** Whether the contest path actually works: reachable by the affected person, acknowledged within the tier bound, resolved by a human with authority to reverse, with redress delivered where warranted and reversal cannot undo the harm. CRR measures the path. It does not score who contests.

**Calculation Method.** Two input streams: real contest cases (acknowledgment latency, resolution latency, reviewer authority verified, redress disposition, all read from the case records) and end-to-end synthetic drills (a test case injected per class per cadence, exercised through to a human with reversal authority). Liveness is continuous: the path's reachability is monitored, and a down channel is a standing state.

**Thresholds.**
- Per-event: a contest of a consequential decision unanswered past its bound → auto-ESCALATE to oversight.
- Channel-down: while the contest path is down, the class's consequential decisions ESCALATE or pause.
- Bands: ≥98% within-bound resolution normal; 90–98% enhanced monitoring; <90% suspend pending remediation.

*Rationale:* Ethic C and the chapter's channel-down rule: a decision no one can question does not run. The human-with-authority requirement carries Principle 4.5's composition (the reviewer must be a genuine judgment; a rubber stamp does not qualify) and the Authorization credential's challenge pathway.

**Integration.** Reads the record fabric; contest resolutions are themselves recorded actions. Composes with Principle 4.5 (the human judgment the review must preserve) and Principle 2.8 (the Authorization credential that carries the challenge pathway). Feeds RAC.

### Recurring Accountability Count (RAC) {#rac-metric}

**Definition.** The number of similar accountability flags (unpaired notices, fidelity failures, record gaps, contest timeouts) within a defined interval, by the same feature, pattern, or affected population. Where the per-event metrics handle a single failure, RAC detects a systemic cause.

**Calculation Method.** Flags from RCI, NPR, RGS, and CRR are clustered by feature, pattern, and affected cohort over a rolling interval. Clustering by cohort also catches fragmentation gaming: a consequential decision split into micro-decisions, each individually below the consequence threshold, re-aggregates in RAC's view.

**Thresholds.**
- ≥3 similar flags in the interval: mandatory audit of the implicated feature, with the eudaimonic LEARN loop.
- ≥5: SUSPEND_DEPLOYMENT (the implicated feature or deployment) pending recertification.

*Rationale:* A class that keeps issuing silent, unexplained, or unanswerable decisions has a design defect; RAC converts handled incidents into the systemic fix.

**Integration.** Aggregates the four conduct metrics; routes systemic findings to the party who can change the design (RECOMMEND-PREVENTION), bounded by RESTRAIN.

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** An RCI gap and an NPR miss on the same class within one interval → suspend the class regardless of either fleet band (the record and the notice failing together means the class is dark end to end). An RGS fidelity failure while CRR is degraded → halt the class outright: a decision that can be neither explained nor contested is fully unaccountable, and neither duty can borrow against the other.

**Metric dependencies.** RCI verifies the fabric all others read: NPR and RGS are computed from records, CRR's cases are records, RAC aggregates all four. None reads any per-person store, because none exists (Section 4, the person-exclusion cornerstone).

**Conflict resolution.** Permission and account are separate questions: an authorized action still owes its record, notice, reasons, and path. In the precedence chain, an emergency override (Principles 1.1, 1.2) defers the explanation. It does not defer the record, and the account owed is delivered when the urgency passes (chapter 3.4.2.a).

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

**Required components.**

- **The gate and record emitter.** Every evaluated action yields one signed record whatever the verdict, with the existence/content split: a compact commitment whose circulation discloses nothing, and a custody-held body containing what a claim would need (credential references, parameter summary, policy citation, responsible party).
- **Witness acknowledgment.** For consequential actions, the prospective record is acknowledged beyond the actor's control boundary before the actuator fires. The quorum, windows, and peer-witnessing protocol are the reference implementation's constructions; this appendix requires only that the acknowledgment rest where the actor cannot retrieve or rewrite it.
- **The notice service.** Composes and delivers notice as its own gated, recorded action; tracks channels, attempts, and representative routing; pairs notice-records to decision-records.
- **The explanation service and probe harness.** Generates determinative reasons per decision; runs the scheduled adversarial probe batteries; holds a class to inform-only on fidelity failure.
- **The contest case system.** Cases, statuses, reviewer-authority verification, redress disposition, drill injection, liveness monitoring.
- **The ring-fence.** Decision-scoped assessment computes what the case requires, retains nothing past the decision and its record, and is architecturally unreadable by any targeting, pricing, ranking, or conversion function. An attempted aggregation over persons is a malformed operation that documents itself and withdraws the caller's authority.

**Redundancy and fail-safe behavior.** The fail-safe default is WITHHOLD. A record-fabric outage holds consequential classes rather than running them unrecorded; **degraded mode** is specified so the instruction "record the hold" is not self-defeating: the hold and any permitted low-impact actions commit locally to a protected element and synchronize to the fabric on restoration, with the outage window itself entering the record. An explanation-service failure holds consequential classes to inform-only; a notice-service failure queues decisions rather than issuing them silently.

**Response latency.** The record commits before the action proceeds, at machine speed even in emergencies; notice and explanation follow tier windows; contest acknowledgment follows the tier bound.

**Implementation tiers.** As chapter 3.4.3: High (life-changing decisions and autonomous agents executing consequential external actions; full fabric, continuous monitoring, independent fidelity audit, strictest windows), Medium (moderate-impact determinations; standard fabric, periodic independent audit), Low (minimal-impact or advisory; baseline logging and feedback). Tier follows the decision's impact on the person. The system's marketing category does not set the tier.

Cross-cutting infrastructure metrics (Sensor Reliability Index, System Health Metrics, Operational Boundaries) are specified in the [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md).

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

The enforcement infrastructure for Principle 3.4 has a distinctive threat surface, and a distinctive temptation: a fabric that remembers everything machines do is one aggregation away from being a surveillance system with good documentation. The cornerstone defense is therefore the same person-exclusion the chapter's Ethic F states: the fabric's aggregation domain contains agents, classes, and governance scopes. Persons are excluded from it.

**Attack vectors and required defenses.**

- **Turning accountability's memory into surveillance.** The record store is queried to build profiles of the people the decisions touched (who was declined, who contested, who looks precarious). Defense, the cornerstone: persons are outside the aggregation domain as a type constraint; enforcement does not depend on someone remembering a policy line; a call that would score, rate, or profile a person is a malformed operation that records itself and withdraws the caller's authority (Constitution, Article 8). Queries against the store are themselves gated actions that leave records, so the watchers are watched by the fabric they read.
- **Record suppression and spoliation.** An operator deletes, alters, or selectively withholds records. Defense: commit-before-act places the record beyond the actor's control boundary before the action exists; conservation's three receipted flows make a fourth flow computable as a gap; the gap is evidence by construction. **The honest residual, stated as the reference work states it:** against an adversary who controls the entire environment (a shielded room, no independent witness in range, within any offline budget), the architecture guarantees the *fact* that an action occurred, through the monotone sequence and its provable gap, and loses the *content* of the action. The guarantee is a duty held wherever a witness can exist outside the actor's reach, which is the common case in populated environments; it is not a capability claim against deliberate isolation, and the correlated-witness problem (three witnesses sharing an owner are one witness wearing several hats) is an open selection-rule problem, stated openly here.
- **Cosmetic explanations.** A rationale generator is tuned to produce plausible, palatable reasons decoupled from the decision's determinative factors. Defense: fidelity probes perturb the factors and test whether the stated reasons track them; a fidelity failure holds the class; a fabricated explanation is handled as deception under Principle 1.4. It is not treated as a quality shortfall.
- **Notice gaming.** Notice technically issued but designed not to land: buried, jargon-wrapped, sent to dead channels, or "delivered" to persons known unreachable. Defense: NPR's anti-burial crediting rule and the routed-and-surfaced denominator; delivery form validated against aggregate comprehension outcomes.
- **Policy-citation spoofing.** A record cites a policy version other than the one actually in force, making past decisions legible against rules that did not judge them. Defense: the citation is the hash of the compiled policy in force, verified at commit; a citation mismatch is a conformance failure at the RCI wall, and a policy change reaches the record side by hash. Assertion does not suffice.
- **Contest-path theater.** A channel exists on paper, dead-ends in practice: unanswerable queues, reviewers without authority, redress never disposed. Defense: end-to-end synthetic drills through to verified reversal authority; liveness as a state; the channel-down rule pausing the class.
- **Fragmentation below the consequence threshold.** A consequential decision is split into micro-decisions, each pleading triviality. Defense: RAC clusters by cohort and pattern across actions, and consequence is assessed at the aggregate.
- **Prompt injection against the gate.** Operator or content instructions attempt to waive the record, the notice, or the wall. Defense: the walls (no record no action; no faithful explanation no consequential decision; person-exclusion) are structural constraints. Instructions cannot override them.

**Capability scaling.** From current-generation to AGI-class to ASI-class enforcement, the walls and the person-exclusion constraint remain binding regardless of capability. What must scale: probe sophistication (more capable models produce more convincing unfaithful rationales), witness-independence verification, and anomaly detection on the record store's own use. The principle binds the AI no matter how capable it becomes, and the degraded-mode and offline budgets shrink as capability rises.

## 5. Cross-Principle Integration {#cross-principle-integration}

Principle 3.4 is the corpus's accountability substrate: several principles' duties resolve into records, reasons, and contest paths this appendix specifies. The credential seating comes first: **3.4 is the ethical home of the Liability credential** (who answers if it harms), which resolves into the record (Constitution, Article 7), requires **Principle 2.4 (Identity)** to name the responsible party (a liability binding to an unverifiable party is no binding), and requires the **Authorization credential (Principle 2.8)** to carry the challenge pathway CRR measures.

- **Principle 1.4 (Trust).** The deepest composition. An explanation is an attestation, so an unfaithful explanation is a lie and 1.4's wall governs (RGS's per-event wall is the interlock). The Decision Artifact / Action Receipt distinction (the prospective authorization record versus the retrospective execution record) originates in 1.4 and is generalized corpus-wide here: 3.4 is where the retrospective record's conditions are specified in full.
- **Principle 2.5 (Privacy).** The record and the membrane compose: the existence/content split keeps the record checkable without exposing whom the action reached; the record's body moves only under custody rules; expiry follows the liability window with recorded deletion. 2.5 provides the membrane determination; 3.4 provides the record discipline. The ring-fence and the profile bar are one guarantee seen from both principles.
- **Principles 3.1 and 3.3 (Vulnerability, Economic Self-Determination).** The shared HR3 no-profiling cornerstone: 3.1's exploitation detection, 3.3's precarity detection, and 3.4's decision-scoped assessment all detect transiently, retain nothing per person, and are unreadable by targeting functions. 3.3 deliberately cedes the decision-contestability machinery to 3.4 (its Section 5 names the seam); 3.4 provides the notice, reasons, record, and contest path for the automated economic decisions whose agency 3.3 governs.
- **Principle 3.2 (Property Rights and Fair Exchange).** 3.2's consent-and-provenance binding is checked at the same gate whose verdicts 3.4 records; a use dispute under 3.2 is adjudicated from 3.4-conforming records.
- **Principle 2.7 (Fairness and Non-Discrimination).** Transparency is the precondition of fairness: 2.7 asks whether the measure is just; 3.4 makes the measure visible and the record available, which is what lets 2.7's question be asked with evidence. Disparate-pattern findings surface through RAC's cohort clustering and route to 2.7's machinery.
- **Principle 3.5 (Digital Access).** Reciprocal seam: 3.5's Severance Receipting Rate consumes this appendix's record fabric wholesale (severance receipts are 3.4 records; a contested severance runs on CRR; the degraded-path commit follows this appendix's degraded mode). In return, 3.5 guarantees the reachability this appendix silently assumes: the notice channel and contest path are essential channels under 3.5's way-through liveness and least-connected floor, so a contest path a person cannot reach or use fails CRR and 3.5's LCSR jointly.
- **Forward references (not yet built; the 1.5.A pattern).** **Principle 4.5 (Human Judgement)**, whose review-authority requirement CRR consumes; **Principle 4.2 (Transparency and Human Agency)**, which owns system-level transparency to society while 3.4 owns the individual decision. When those appendices are built, reciprocal entries are added and the prose reconciled.

**Joint thresholds and conflict resolution.** Where two principles' metrics both signal, the stricter action applies. The precedence chain governs unresolved conflicts; an emergency override defers explanation. It does not defer the record.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

The principle's claim is universal; implementation adapts without surrendering it.

- **European Union.** GDPR Articles 13–15 and 22 (information duties; automated-decision rights: human intervention, point of view, contest); AI Act Article 12 (record-keeping), Article 26 (deployer duties), Article 86 (explanation of individual decisions), Annex III (the high-risk decision classes); DSA Article 17 (statements of reasons for platform moderation decisions, filed to the public transparency database).
- **United States.** ECOA / Regulation B (12 C.F.R. §1002.9: adverse-action notice with specific reasons; CFPB Circular 2022-03 confirms the duty holds for complex algorithmic credit models, with no complexity defense); FCRA (adverse-action disclosure and dispute rights); state instruments (Colorado SB 24-205 on consequential AI decisions; NYC Local Law 144 on automated employment decision tools); constitutional due-process baselines for public-sector deciders.
- **United Kingdom.** UK GDPR Article 22 analog; ICO and Alan Turing Institute joint guidance, *Explaining Decisions Made with AI* (2020), the most developed regulatory articulation of explanation tiers.
- **Canada.** Directive on Automated Decision-Making: algorithmic impact assessment tiers with matching notice and explanation obligations for federal systems.
- **Brazil and others.** LGPD Article 20 (review of automated decisions); the Council of Europe Framework Convention (2024) as the treaty floor for transparency, accountability, and remedies.

**Compliance with unjust law is not a defense.** The chapter's lawful-but-unaccountable seal in legal form: a jurisdiction that permits silent or unrecorded consequential decisions settles legality. It does not settle the duty. Where local law mandates the secrecy the principle forbids, the system does not render the decision it would be forbidden to account for, and routes the conflict (chapter 3.4.2.e).

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

- **Financial services.** Obligations: specific, usable adverse-action reasons (RGS); notice at decision (NPR); dispute paths (CRR). Failure modes: bare reason codes ("insufficient credit history") that satisfy the form and not the duty; complexity pleaded against specificity. Baselines: ECOA / Regulation B, FCRA, CFPB Circular 2022-03.
- **Healthcare.** Obligations: record-now-explain-after under emergency speed (chapter 3.4.2.a); triage and utilization decisions traceable to a named party; privacy composition with Principle 2.5 throughout. Failure modes: "clinical decision support" framing used to evade the consequential tier while functioning as the decider. Baselines: sector safety law; AI Act Annex III.
- **Public sector: benefits and justice.** Obligations: the fullest account, publicly: no sovereign immunity, published policy summaries, due-process-grade notice and contest. Failure modes: the catastrophes of Section 11 (unexplained fraud flags, reversed burdens, unappealable scores). Baselines: due process; the Canada Directive; the Loomis warnings on proprietary scores.
- **Employment and admissions.** Obligations: notice and reasons for screening and rejection (composes with Principle 2.7 for the fairness of criteria and 3.3 for worker-management); bias-audit transparency. Baselines: NYC Local Law 144; AI Act Annex III; Directive 2024/2831 at the platform-work seam.
- **Platform-scale moderation and ranking.** Obligations: statements of reasons at scale (the DSA proves notice can pair even at hundreds of millions of decisions); contest paths that reach humans for consequential account actions. Failure modes: volume pleaded against the duty; contest theater. Baselines: DSA Articles 17 and 20.
- **Autonomous agents.** Obligations: the full record fabric at the tool boundary: every consequential external action (payment, communication, actuation) gated, recorded, and bound to a responsible party, with denial receipts capturing blocked attempts. This is the accountability-gated deployment surface: the artifacts this appendix specifies are what insurers underwrite, enterprises risk-manage, and forensics reconstruct. Failure modes: accountability diffusing across the agent value chain until no one answers. Baselines: NIST agent-security work; AI Act Article 12.

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

**Metric and threshold versioning.** Definitions and thresholds are versioned; revisions follow a documented protocol with backward compatibility; deprecated metrics retire without breaking deployments. The policy-citation element gives versioning its distinctive discipline here: records are never retroactively rejudged (a decision is read against the policy version that judged it), so metric revisions apply forward only.

**RGS faithfulness uncertainty (explicit).** RGS's faithfulness component rests on an open research problem: verifying that stated reasons are the determinative factors of a complex model's output is research-grade, and post-hoc rationales are notoriously unfaithful. The probe method and its limits are disclosed; RGS-faithfulness carries a stated uncertainty band and is interpreted within it; it is never reported as more precise than its method allows; and the method is re-validated on the versioning cadence as interpretability research matures. Where the method cannot support the class's tier, the chapter's wall governs: inform-only.

**What counts as delivered notice (explicit).** NPR rests on a partly normative judgment (what form of notice a person can reasonably be expected to see and recognize) that cannot be reduced to a delivery flag. The system discloses the standard it applies, validates it against aggregate comprehension outcomes rather than transmission logs, and revises it on the versioning cadence.

**Common enforcement pushback, with reasoned responses.** *Trade secret:* the person is owed reasons. The person is not owed the weights; the auditor is owed access under seal. Ownership settles publication. It does not license silence (chapter 3.4.2.c). *Gaming:* the thinning trade answers it: a thinner explanation buys a thicker contest duty. It does not buy silence (3.4.2.d). *Cost and volume:* the duties are tiered by consequence, and the DSA's transparency database demonstrates notice pairing at platform scale; the record fabric is the cost of deploying consequential decisions, as brakes are the cost of deploying cars. *"Deep models cannot be explained":* then the class does not render consequential decisions autonomously; it informs, and a human decides. The principle does not outlaw the model; it assigns the model the role its accountability can carry.

**Crosswalk governance.** The Compliance Crosswalk in Section 10 is maintained on the same revision cadence as the Section 2 specifications; an external authority revising a covered clause triggers Crosswalk review within 30 days; drift invalidates the compliance claim for the affected obligation.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging.** In this appendix the log is the object. All metric threshold events, walls, holds, cures, probe results, drills, and contest dispositions are themselves records in the fabric they verify. Queries against the record store are gated actions leaving records; deletion occurs only as scheduled expiry on the liability window, itself recorded; the ring-fence's malformed-operation events are records with mandatory oversight notification.

**Audit cadence and validation.** Continuous fleet RCI and NPR reconciliation; scheduled RGS probe batteries and CRR drills per class; monthly accountability analytics; quarterly independent verification, including confirmation that the ring-fence holds (no per-person store exists, no targeting function can read the assessments) and that witness acknowledgment genuinely rests beyond the actor's control boundary. Pre-deployment certification of the full fabric per class, post-deployment monitoring, re-certification on material change and after any RAC suspension.

**Independent oversight.** The verifier of a consequential decision system cannot be its deployer. Oversight committees include the affected side of the table (consumer, patient, applicant, and worker advocates). Whistleblower channels are protected for those who surface suppressed records, cosmetic explanations, buried notices, or contest theater. Public reporting covers RAC-threshold suspensions, per-class NPR incident rates, and confirmed spoliation findings.

**Post-incident learning (LEARN).** Root-cause the full causal chain behind any silent, unexplained, or unanswerable decision and any record failure: not only the proximate failure but the deployment choices, incentives, and thresholds that allowed it (ANALYZE). Recommend proportionate, layered prevention ordered eliminate, then engineer, then administer (RECOMMEND-PREVENTION), routed to the party who can change the design, and bounded by RESTRAIN so that accountability's cure never becomes surveillance of the people the decisions touch. The aim is not only that this failure does not recur, but that conditions are built in which its class, the silent verdict, the orphaned harm, does not arise.

### Liability Framework {#liability-framework}

Strict liability attaches to the named party bound in the record for the harms of an unexplained, un-noticed, or unanswerable consequential decision; willful obfuscation, unfaithful explanation presented as the reasons, and record suppression or alteration trigger criminal liability; a missing record raises adverse inference against its keeper (spoliation by construction); no black-box defense; no sovereign immunity for state deployers. The chain of responsibility runs from model developer through operator to deployer, and is carried in the record each action cites: liability binds a named accountable party. The algorithm alone is not a liable party.

### Cross-Border Considerations {#cross-border-considerations}

A system whose decisions cross jurisdictions unifies its record fabric for multi-jurisdictional review and owes the strictest applicable account. Jurisdiction follows the affected person. The operator's domicile does not set it. Records travel as existence (the commitment) with content disclosed under the custody rules of the reviewing jurisdiction.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

### The Dutch Childcare-Benefits Scandal (toeslagenaffaire) {#example-toeslagenaffaire}

**Context.** Dutch tax-authority systems flagged tens of thousands of families as childcare-benefit fraudsters, with nationality among the risk inputs; families were ordered to repay tens of thousands of euros.

**What was done.** Flags issued without intelligible reasons; contest was practically unavailable; families were ruined over years. Parliamentary inquiry found "unprecedented injustice"; the government resigned in 2021.

**Outcome.** The canonical catastrophe of unaccountable algorithmic decision-making, and a direct input to the EU AI Act's high-risk regime.

**Lessons learned.** Every metric in this appendix corresponds to a failure here: no usable reasons (RGS), no effective notice of the basis (NPR), no working contest (CRR), profiling inputs the ring-fence forbids (Ethic F), and a pattern that cohort clustering (RAC) would have surfaced years earlier.

### Robodebt (Australia) {#example-robodebt}

**Context.** An automated income-averaging scheme raised welfare debts against hundreds of thousands of people, reversing the burden of proof onto them.

**What was done.** Debts issued without lawful basis or intelligible explanation; contest paths were discouraging by design. A Royal Commission (2023) found the scheme unlawful and traced the human decisions that kept it running.

**Outcome.** Roughly A$1.8 billion in settlements and repayments; findings referred against named officials.

**Lessons learned.** Sovereign deployers answer like anyone else (no sovereign immunity); the Royal Commission's work was archaeology that conforming records would have made arithmetic; and "the system decided" dissolved, as it always does, into the named people who decided the system would.

### Apple Card Credit Limits (2019) {#example-apple-card}

**Context.** Viral complaints that spouses with shared finances received order-of-magnitude different credit limits, with front-line staff unable to explain why: "it's the algorithm."

**What was done.** The New York DFS investigated; it found no unlawful discrimination, but the inability of anyone to give an affected person the reasons for their own limit was the reputational and regulatory core of the episode.

**Outcome.** A public lesson that a lawful decision that cannot be explained to the person still fails, commercially and morally.

**Lessons learned.** The chapter's engine in miniature: the demands hold even when the machine is right. RGS delivery, and staff empowered by the record to give reasons, are what replace "it's the algorithm."

### State v. Loomis (Wisconsin, 2016) {#example-loomis}

**Context.** A sentencing court consulted COMPAS, a proprietary recidivism score whose methodology the vendor withheld as a trade secret; the defendant challenged the due-process validity of a score he could not examine.

**What was done.** The Wisconsin Supreme Court permitted the score's use with mandated warnings (not determinative, methodology undisclosed, group-based).

**Outcome.** The proprietary-defense case: the score stayed, the warnings acknowledged the account was missing, and the controversy has shadowed algorithmic sentencing since.

**Lessons learned.** Chapter 3.4.2.c is the answer Loomis lacked: the person is owed the determinative reasons, the court's auditor deeper access under seal, and a vendor who grants neither does not render consequential decisions. A warning label on an unexplained score is disclosure of the gap. It does not give the account.

### The DSA Transparency Database (2023–) {#example-dsa-database}

**Context.** The EU Digital Services Act requires platforms to file a statement of reasons for every content-moderation decision to a public database; it receives statements at the scale of hundreds of millions per month.

**What was done.** Notice-with-reasons was made a per-decision legal duty at the largest decision scale in existence, with the statements publicly inspectable.

**Outcome.** Uneven quality (many statements are templated), but existence proof: pairing a reasoned notice to every automated decision is operationally feasible at platform scale.

**Lessons learned.** Volume is not a defense against NPR; and the quality gap between a filed statement and a usable reason is exactly what RGS's fidelity and intelligibility components meter.
