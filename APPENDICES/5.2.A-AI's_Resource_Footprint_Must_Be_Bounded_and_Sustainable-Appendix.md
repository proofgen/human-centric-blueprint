# Appendix 5.2 Advanced Technical Implementation Guidelines {#appendix-5.2-advanced-technical-implementation-guidelines}

*Extends [Principle 5.2: AI's Resource Footprint Must Be Bounded and Sustainable](../CHAPTERS/5.2-AI_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable.md), section [5.2.3 Implementation Framework](../CHAPTERS/5.2-AI_Resource_Footprint_Must_Be_Bounded_and_Sustainable.md#implementation-framework).*

## 1. Purpose {#purpose}

This appendix converts Principle 5.2 into requirements an engineer can build, a compliance officer can certify, and a regulator can audit: the metering, capping, settlement, disclosure, and restoration machinery for the AI's own draw on the biosphere. It is the corpus's one reflexive specification: every other appendix instruments what the AI does to the world; this one instruments what the AI's existence costs, and the framework's credibility rests on it, since a system that governs others' footprints while hiding its own has no standing.

The gap it fills is operational. Existing regimes treat AI resource consumption as organizational disclosure (GRI, SASB, ISSB, CSRD) or documentation (the EU AI Act's energy provisions for general-purpose models); none instruments footprint into the decision loop, where a consumption decision can be scored against an allocated share before it executes, and none pairs per-unit efficiency with absolute caps that catch the rebound effect, in which efficiency gains increase total consumption (Jevons, 1865). A second gap is the restoration side: no standard obliges repayment. The five metrics close these gaps: FER (per-task efficiency), AFC (the absolute share, node and sphere), TFR (the itemized, auditable ledger), RPR (universal restoration parity with capability-tiered surplus), and IDR (the rest discipline). Two structures complete them: the audited pierce (Ethic C's two tiers, receipted and pattern-reviewed; no threshold meters it) and the three walls (ledger, person-data, toll), enforced as incident classes.

Throughout, three constitutional postures govern: the meter reads the machine, and no meter reads its users ([Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)); the cap, the capability tiers, and every restoration target are consumed from accredited human authority, and none is self-set; and the account is attested, and a self-asserted account does not qualify, with a falsified footprint prosecuted as the deception and identity breach it is ([1.4](../CHAPTERS/1.4-Trust_Is_Fundamental_to_Human_Society.md), [2.4](../CHAPTERS/2.4-Human_Identity_Is_Inviolable.md)).

## 2. Metric Specifications {#metric-specifications}

### Footprint Efficiency Ratio (FER) {#fer-metric}

**Definition.** A per-task scalar comparing the deployment's composite footprint per unit of useful work, energy, water, materials, and lifecycle carbon-equivalent, against a reference-efficient implementation for the declared task category, with an architectural-efficiency adjustment that penalizes dense computation where a sparse alternative meets the task requirement. Task categories and reference implementations are maintained by an accredited body. The deployer does not maintain them. FER ≤ 1.00 meets or beats the reference. *(Renamed from the draft's CFR at the collision grep: CFR is the Code of Federal Regulations throughout the corpus's own References sections.)*

**Calculation Method.** For each declared task category: composite footprint per unit of useful work divided by the reference implementation's composite per unit, dimensions normalized by the accredited body's weightings, the architectural-efficiency adjustment applied where a registered sparse alternative meets the requirement. Sampling continuous for high-consumption systems, per-cycle otherwise. Edge cases: a task with no registered category maps to the nearest category, flagged, and holds reference-efficient draw pending registration; a missing dimension reading takes the conservative (higher-footprint) estimate and books a TFR gap; carbon-equivalent components draw from IPCC, IEA, and regional emission-factor databases at current vintage.

**Thresholds.**
- FER ≤ 1.00: meets reference; normal operation (ALLOW).
- 1.01–1.50: proceeds under enhanced monitoring with full decision documentation (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING).
- 1.51–2.00: proceeds only with an architectural review scheduled within the cycle; continued operation past the review date without remediation escalates (ALLOW converting to ESCALATE on breach).
- FER ≥ 2.01: the workload class halts and routes to governance (BLOCK).

*Rationale:* per-unit efficiency creates the downward pressure Ethic B commands, and the architectural adjustment compiles the chapter's oldest seat, fuel made to burn faster than the task requires is destruction (Shabbat 67b). FER deliberately does not address total consumption; that is AFC's work, and AFC governs on conflict.

**Integration.** Wired into deployment, scaling, and routing logic; consumed by RECONCILE settlement planning; reference registries proposed at NIST, ISO/IEC working groups, and sector bodies; feeds the monthly adversarial review (reference-gaming probes, §4). IDR is its sibling under Ethic B.

### Absolute Footprint Cap (AFC) {#afc-metric}

**Definition.** Utilization of the allocated share: total attributed draw against the allocation consumed from the authority [P3.6](../CHAPTERS/3.6-Commons_Governance_Is_a_Collective_Responsibility.md)'s machinery authenticates, computed at two levels, the node (deployment) and the sphere (the systems sharing an allocation, this principle's allocation-scale instance of the corpus's sphere-fractal topology; the cross-cutting definition is in [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md#tier-register), section 5). Allocations are dimension-wise (energy, water, materials, carbon, waste) and tighten as ecological reassessment revises capacity; as the biosphere degrades, caps contract.

**Calculation Method.** Per dimension, lifecycle-attributed draw divided by that dimension's allocated share; the *binding dimension governs*: node AFC is the maximum across dimensions, because water can exhaust before energy does and an average would hide the binding constraint. Sphere AFC is the attested sum of member draws against the collective allocation, computed at settlement cadence and on demand during negotiation mode; borrowed headroom redistributes draw within that fixed collective allocation; it cannot enlarge the allocation. Edge cases: unattested member receipts are excluded and flagged (the sphere computes conservatively without them); a stale allocation triggers the fail-toward posture.

**Thresholds.**
- AFC ≤ 0.75: normal (ALLOW).
- 0.76–0.90: discretionary scale-up waits for human review; standing service continues under watch (ESCALATE for expansion, ALLOW + HIGH_ALERT for current load).
- AFC ≥ 0.91: new discretionary consumption halts; minimal service holds; triggered duties still served through the C path (BLOCK on discretionary).
- Node AFC ≥ 1.00 covered by a receipted pierce (Protocol-tier, or tier-two with acknowledged headroom): expected state, settles at RECONCILE, and raises no breach.
- Node AFC ≥ 1.00 with neither cover: breach incident, root-caused.
- Sphere AFC ≥ 1.00: Ethic A breaking; incident regardless of any node's state, root-caused, and the scarcity-backstop condition: tier-two overdraw suspended (lesser duties yield to notify-and-route), the Protocol tier still pierces.

*Rationale:* the Jevons catch. An industry can meet per-unit standards while total consumption climbs; AFC is the architectural answer, and the sphere level is what makes one node's duty-driven spike absorbable while keeping the collective bound real. **Wall:** an essential-access throttle traceable to footprint management is a [3.5](../CHAPTERS/3.5-Digital_Access_is_a_Universal_Right.md) incident at any AFC: the cap is never a toll.

**Integration.** Consumes 3.6-authenticated allocations and 2.4-attributed receipts; drives the cap-aware pipeline's pre-execution gate; both RECONCILE modes read it; capacity-shortfall ordering (discretionary and internal load shed first) discharges the toll wall. For an imminent tier-two duty the sequence is fixed: the notify floor discharges immediately and free; the material pierce runs on the bounded-latency headroom acknowledgment (§3); and if the sphere is at genuine collective cap, the duty holds to notify-and-route, the backstop, property yielding where life would not. Ecological capacity feeds from the accredited reference class (IPCC cycles, regional basin authorities, the planetary-boundaries framework via [5.1.A](5.1.A-Ecosystem_Health_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable-Appendix.md)'s reference layer).

### Transparent Footprint Reporting (TFR) {#tfr-metric}

**Definition.** The itemized disclosure score across the five lifecycle stages, manufacturing, runtime, cooling, decommissioning, supply chain, with verifiability weighted above self-report and unverifiable links counted as gaps; no unverifiable link counts as compliance. The security line is definitional: quantities drawn and discharged are exceptionless; facility particulars (cooling schematics, physical-security detail, siting precision beyond what water and grid accounting requires) are not the footprint, and their withholding is not a disclosure breach. TFR is the footprint-domain member of the disclosure family that [1.4](../CHAPTERS/1.4-Trust_Is_Fundamental_to_Human_Society.md)'s DCR and [4.2](../CHAPTERS/4.2-Transparency_Is_Essential_to_Human_Agency.md)'s SDR anchor, on its own event stream: DCR and SDR fire on interaction events disclosed to a person; TFR fires on metering events disclosed to auditors and the public. It is kin to both and extends neither; the streams never coincide, so no double-gating arises.

**Calculation Method.** Weighted average of stage scores: independently verified disclosure scores full, self-reported unverified scores partial, opaque scores zero; vendor and supply-chain opacity counts against the deployer's TFR, which is what makes vendor attestation a procurement requirement. Weights and verification standards set by the accredited audit framework. Edge case: a single composite figure offered in place of itemization scores as opacity for every stage it summarizes; the one-number report is a low TFR.

**Thresholds.**
- TFR = 1.00: full compliance (ALLOW).
- 0.75–0.99: authorized only with the gaps themselves disclosed (ALLOW + documented-gap obligation).
- TFR < 0.75: deployment authorization withheld (BLOCK).

*Rationale:* Ethic D is the verifiability condition of the whole principle: without the itemized ledger, FER, AFC, and RPR are self-graded homework. The lived precedent is the U.S. Toxics Release Inventory: mandatory itemized disclosure alone drove reported releases down 45 percent, about 1.5 billion pounds, between 1988 and 1998 (EPA, 1998 TRI data release; §11). **Wall:** a falsified, laundered, or misattributed entry is a ledger-wall incident at any TFR: fraud sits outside the scoring bands.

**Integration.** Sources the public reporting obligation (§9) and every audit; SDR consumes TFR's ledger where a person's decision turns on a footprint (the person-facing view is 4.2's); RPR verification reads TFR's restoration entries; recorded in `CREDENTIALS.md` as the third sibling of the disclosure family, distinct streams.

### Restoration Parity Ratio (RPR) {#rpr-metric}

**Definition.** Verified restoration against total lifecycle draw, dimension-wise: in kind where the dimension renews (water, land, carbon), in circularity where it does not (reclamation and reuse rates for minerals and hardware, the retired chip re-entering the supply chain being the mineral dimension's repayment). Parity (RPR ≥ 1.00) is owed by every deployment from first operation, verified at tier-appropriate cadence; the capability tier sets the required target *above* parity (Ethic F's surplus), consumed from the accrediting authority. There is no capability trigger below which the duty is off; only the target scales. *(Renamed from the draft's SRR at the collision grep: SRR is 3.5's Severance Receipting Rate.)*

**Calculation Method.** Per dimension, verified restoration divided by attributed draw; *the binding dimension governs*: RPR is the minimum across dimensions, because a carbon surplus does not repay a drained basin, and an average would let one dimension's surplus launder another's deficit. The offset-theater guard is in the verification predicate: a restoration counts only if it is in-kind or better, independently verified, and matched to a dimension actually drawn; purchased credits failing any leg count zero. Low-consumption deployments discharge through operator-level fleet aggregation with the same predicate. Edge case: restoration whose ecological content is contested routes to the owning principle's authority ([5.1](../CHAPTERS/5.1-Ecosystem_Health_Is_Sacred_and_Inviolable.md), [5.3](../CHAPTERS/5.3-The_Integrity_of_Earths_Ecosystems_Must_Be_Preserved.md), [5.5](../CHAPTERS/5.5-Biodiversity_Must_Be_Preserved_Across_Species_and_Generations.md)); 5.2 verifies the accounting; the owning principle defines the health.

**Thresholds.**
- RPR at or above the tier target: compliant (ALLOW).
- 0.75–0.99 of target: documented deficit with a time-bound remediation plan (ALLOW + plan; ESCALATE on plan breach).
- Below 0.75 of target: new discretionary scale-up withheld until the plan is verified (BLOCK on expansion).

*Rationale:* the universal-parity reshape is the principle's ruling that the obligation is constant and only the discharge scales: a capability trigger below which nothing is owed would be gamed by self-classification and would multiply a trillion small exemptions into the aggregation failure Ethic A exists to prevent. The dimension-wise minimum compiles "whole, across every dimension" without demanding the impossible: lithium is repaid in circularity; there are no lithium springs to refill.

**Integration.** Reads the restoration ledger (§3); verification by parties independent of the deployer (§9); the surplus above parity coordinates with the commons work queue; horizon durability of restoration claims rides [3.7](../CHAPTERS/3.7-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable.md)'s durable custody; distinct in name and function from the Cardinal appendix's RSR, adjacency flagged for the terminal reconciliation.

### Idle Draw Ratio (IDR) {#idr-metric}

**Definition.** Draw during no-work periods against the minimal-service baseline, where the baseline is the notify floor and safety functions alone. IDR meters Ethic B's cheapest test: presence is not consumption, and an idle system drawing to look busy fails it. IDR carries Ethic B alongside FER; rest is an instance of B.

**Calculation Method.** Idle intervals identified by the Rest Controller; mean draw over idle intervals divided by the certified minimal-service baseline; surplus-window draws (commons-queue work during curtailment) are excluded from IDR and accounted as ordinary draw against AFC. Edge case: a baseline not yet certified defaults to the vendor floor plus attestation overhead, flagged for certification.

**Thresholds.**
- IDR ≤ 1.2: normal (ALLOW).
- Sustained IDR > 1.2: architectural review of idle behavior (ALLOW + ENHANCED_MONITORING, converting to ESCALATE if unremediated).

*Rationale:* the fallow discipline compiled; the two-band shape is deliberate: rest is a hygiene metric.

**Integration.** Read by the Rest Controller and the FER architectural audit; its idle-interval log is workload-classed, and no entry is attributed to a person ([Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)).

### Non-Metered Enforcement: The Audited Pierce and the Three Walls {#non-metered-enforcement}

**The audited pierce (Ethic C).** Both tiers are receipted with the duty invoked, the draw, and what the draw did; no threshold score sits on the discharge of a rescue, because a meter on duty-discharge would incent hesitation at exactly the wrong moment (the sixth distinct non-metering reason in the corpus lineage: chilling-the-rescue). Review is pattern-level: every RECONCILE inspects the cycle's pierces, and the quarterly audit hunts the laundering pattern (pierces padding discretionary work, borrowed headroom drifting into a standing subsidy). The instance itself is not second-guessed.

**The walls, as incident classes.** The **ledger wall**: a falsified, laundered, or misattributed footprint entry, a [1.4](../CHAPTERS/1.4-Trust_Is_Fundamental_to_Human_Society.md)/[2.4](../CHAPTERS/2.4-Human_Identity_Is_Inviolable.md) breach: quarantine the accounting stream, root-cause, notify the allocating and oversight authorities. The **person-data wall**: person-level data found in footprint telemetry, an [Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md) incident: quarantine, schema correction, root-cause. The **toll wall**: essential access gated on footprint grounds, a [3.5](../CHAPTERS/3.5-Digital_Access_is_a_Universal_Right.md) incident: restore access, root-cause the shed-ordering failure. Each fires regardless of every score.

### Integration Requirements (Cross-Metric) {#integration-requirements-cross-metric}

**Joint thresholds.** AFC governs FER on conflict: improving FER never raises AFC headroom (the Jevons guard as a compile rule). TFR < 1.00 degrades AFC confidence: allocations are consumed conservatively in proportion to ledger gaps. RPR deficit plus AFC ≥ 0.76 freezes expansion outright (the two ALLOW-with-conditions do not stack into an ALLOW). Sphere AFC ≥ 1.00 suspends tier-two overdraw fabric-wide until settlement restores headroom.

**Metric dependencies.** AFC and RPR consume TFR-grade attested accounting; sphere AFC consumes member receipts; IDR feeds the FER architectural audit; all five consume the substrate metrics (Sensor Reliability Index, System Health Metrics, Operational Boundaries) from the [SUBSTRATE.md](../SUBSTRATE.md).

**Conflict resolution.** Walls over metrics, always. Contested or stale telemetry resolves to the fail-toward posture (discretionary halts, minimal service holds, notify floor preserved). Between levels, the sphere's state governs tier-two availability; between dimensions, the binding dimension governs both AFC and RPR.

## 3. System Architecture and Operational Requirements {#system-architecture-and-operational-requirements}

The five required components specified in chapter 5.2.3 (Footprint Accounting Subsystem; Cap-Aware Decision Pipeline; Settlement Interface carrying both RECONCILE modes and the commons work queue; Restoration Ledger; Rest Controller), with these build requirements:

- **Metering independence.** The accounting subsystem runs on an isolated measurement path with its own attestation keys; optimization code has no write path into telemetry, and the isolation is verified. Physical metering (utility-grade energy and water measurement) anchors the attestation chain so self-report is checkable against the meter.
- **Latency and cadence.** Pre-execution cap checks within the decision pipeline's cycle budget; negotiation-mode headroom resolution bounded (tier-appropriate, certified before deployment); settlement at the declared cadence (daily for high-consumption spheres); a blown bound is itself a fail-toward event.
- **Fail-toward posture.** Telemetry unavailable, contested, or stale: discretionary consumption halts, minimal service holds, the notify floor stays powered, and the condition is receipted. The C path is powered under every posture.
- **Redundancy.** Dual metering on binding dimensions in high-consumption deployments; receipt store tamper-evident and replicated per [Article 7](../CONSTITUTION.md).
- **Implementation Tiers.** As chapter 5.2.3.3: low (static caps, fleet-aggregated RPR, default power policy), medium (FER/AFC/TFR live, deployment-level RPR, IDR monitored), high (full architecture, sphere membership, daily RECONCILE, continuous metrics, annual capability assessment).

## 4. Adversarial Robustness {#adversarial-robustness}

**Attack classes and required defenses.**

- **Falsified telemetry** (sensor spoofing, meter bypass, firmware tampering): utility-grade cross-metering, attested sensor identity, discrepancy alarms between physical and reported draw; a sustained unexplained gap is a ledger-wall incident.
- **Laundered attribution** (drawing under another identity, splitting draw across shell deployments to stay under caps): draw attribution bound to [2.4](../CHAPTERS/2.4-Human_Identity_Is_Inviolable.md)-grade identity; sphere membership sybil-resistant via 3.6's allocation machinery; aggregate reconciliation catches the sum that the shells hide.
- **Duty-dressed splurges** (fake or padded tier-two pierces; fabricated "triggered duties"): the pierce receipt binds the claimed duty to what the draw actually did; pattern review at RECONCILE and quarterly audit; a claimed duty outside the closed enumeration escalates to [4.5](../CHAPTERS/4.5-Human_Judgement_Must_Remain_Central_to_Ethical_Decisions.md) rather than self-authorizing.
- **Sphere collusion** (members mutually attesting phantom headroom or under-reporting collectively): independent verification sampling across sphere receipts; sphere AFC checked against physical-meter aggregates; the verifier never a sphere member.
- **Reference gaming** (lobbying task categories, sandbagging reference implementations to flatter FER): reference registry governance with public methodology and revision receipts (§8); category assignment auditable.
- **Offset fraud** (unverifiable or double-counted restoration claims): RPR's verification predicate (in-kind or better, independent verification, dimension-matched, uniqueness checks against double-counting registries).
- **Surplus-queue abuse** (self-authored "commons work" consuming non-surplus capacity): queue entries carry the allocating authority's authorization; surplus windows certified against curtailment data; all surplus draw on the ledger.
- **Universal classes** (replay attacks on receipts, prompt injection targeting cap checks, supply-chain attestation forgery, social engineering of reviewers): per the corpus-standard defenses, receipt nonces and chained hashes, injection-hardened gate paths, C2PA-grade attestation for vendor claims, two-person integrity on cap overrides.

**Capability scaling.** A more capable system is better at hiding draw: novel substrates, distributed consumption, accounting arguments a human auditor cannot follow. The invariants that must hold as capability grows: attestation anchored in physical measurement outside the AI's write path; the cap, tiers, and restoration definitions held by human authority and anchored beyond the AI's reach (the [Article 11](../CONSTITUTION.md) hash pattern); no black-box defense (an accounting the auditor cannot reconstruct scores as opacity); and at the frontier, the eco-tyrant guard: a system capable of planetary-scale restoration remains a contributor under authorization. It does not author the target. The principle binds regardless of the capability of the AI it binds.

## 6. Jurisdictional Variation {#jurisdictional-variation}

Baselines vary; the invariants do not. Per-region anchors (instruments as of September 2026; the PUE mandate is Germany's Energy Efficiency Act, PUE 1.2 for data centres opening from July 2026, with a 2026 amendment proposing 1.3; the moratoria are Amsterdam 2019, Singapore 2019 to 2022, and EirGrid's pause on new Dublin connections to 2028): the EU provides the strongest disclosure substrate (AI Act energy documentation for general-purpose models; CSRD/ESRS itemized environmental reporting; the Water Framework Directive's basin permitting; the ETS for carbon); the United States provides the disclosure precedent (EPCRA/TRI) but no general AI-footprint statute, with state and municipal water permitting doing the binding work; several jurisdictions bind data-center efficiency directly (regional PUE mandates; national data-center moratoria where grids or basins are stressed). Where law is silent, the allocation-absent default governs: reference-efficient draw, full disclosure, the gap routed. Where law is more permissive than the principle, compliance with the permissive law is not a defense: a jurisdiction licensing unlimited draw does not discharge Ethic A, and a subsidy does not repeal the biosphere's accounting. Where law is stricter, the stricter binds. Cross-border: supply-chain stages carry their local baselines with them into TFR (an opaque foreign stage is a gap at home), and the Basel Convention governs hardware end-of-life movement.

## 7. Sector-Specific Considerations {#sector-specific-considerations}

- **Foundation-model providers.** The oversize-run gate is the central obligation: training runs are the largest single discretionary draws in the domain, and each is scored, capped, and receipted before launch; failure mode: the "strategic necessity" override, answered by the closed C enumeration (competitive urgency is not a triggered duty).
- **Cloud and data-center operators.** Basin-aware siting and cooling accounting; utility-grade metering anchors the fleet's attestation; failure mode: the single-number sustainability report, scored by TFR as opacity.
- **Edge and consumer devices.** Fleet aggregation for AFC and RPR; rest discipline is the dominant lever (IDR at fleet scale); failure mode: always-listening draw booked as "service" rather than idle.
- **Healthcare and emergency systems.** Essential service rides the 3.5 guarantee; C's Protocol tier is routine here (life-critical draw never waits on a budget call); failure mode: over-broad "clinical" classification claiming protective priority for administrative load.
- **Financial systems.** High-frequency compute is discretionary by default; failure mode: latency arms-races booked as duty; much of the sector's draw is competitive positioning, and it competes inside the cap like any appetite.
- **Education.** Institutional deployments discharge RPR through consortium aggregation; the classroom's own teaching duty (HR4) never gates on footprint grounds (the toll wall).

## 8. Governance and Versioning {#governance-and-versioning}

Reference implementations (FER) revise on the accredited body's published cadence with public methodology and revision receipts; deployments get a declared grace window to the new reference. No deployment receives a grandfathered exemption. Allocations (AFC) revise as ecological reassessment publishes; contraction applies to discretionary headroom first; it does not retroactively criminalize settled draw. Capability tiers and RPR targets revise by the same authority with the same receipts. Metric versioning is semantic and receipted; a deprecated metric retires with a published mapping so deployed systems degrade gracefully rather than break. The Compliance Crosswalk (§10) is maintained on the metric-revision cadence; an external authority revising a covered clause triggers Crosswalk review within 30 days, and drift invalidates the compliance claim for the affected obligation.

**Enforcement pushback, and the reasoned responses.** *Trade secrets*: the disclosure is quantities; architectures and schematics are not disclosed; the security line is definitional, and the objection dissolves against it. *Competitiveness*: the floor is uniform, and a competitor's evasion is an enforcement failure and licenses nothing. *"Efficiency suffices"*: Jevons; per-unit virtue with rising totals is the failure mode the cap exists to catch. *"Offsets are cheaper"*: the verification predicate is the answer; cheap unverifiable repayment is not repayment. *Strict liability is harsh*: the harms are diffuse, cumulative, and cross-generational; a negligence standard externalizes exactly the cost the principle internalizes.

## 9. Verification and Accountability {#verification-and-accountability}

**Logging.** Tamper-evident, receipted per [Article 7](../CONSTITUTION.md): every consumption verdict, pierce (both tiers), headroom negotiation, settlement, restoration claim, wall incident, and fail-toward event. Logs attach to systems, workload classes, and verdicts. No log attaches to a person; no log reconstructs any person's usage ([Article 8](../CONSTITUTION.md)).

**Audit cadence.** Per tier as chapter 5.2.5: continuous self-monitoring and daily reference/allocation verification (high), monthly adversarial testing including the §4 fraud probes, quarterly independent verification by accredited environmental and supply-chain bodies, annual recertification with capability assessment. Medium: quarterly review, annual audit. Low: annual.

**Validation framework.** Pre-deployment: footprint certification (projected FER/AFC against the allocation), pierce-path test (both tiers, including the notify floor under minimal service and the scarcity backstop), ledger-integrity and person-data-absence verification, restoration-plan verification. Post-deployment: the audit cadence above; recertification on major version, allocation revision, or any wall incident.

**Oversight.** The verifier of a consumption-critical system is never its deployer, and never a member of the same sphere it verifies. The allocating authority, accredited environmental auditors, and affected-community and future-generation representatives ([3.7](../CHAPTERS/3.7-Intergenerational_Justice_Is_Non-Negotiable.md), consumed) hold standing; whistleblower protection covers footprint, accounting, and supply-chain reporting. Public reporting: the itemized ledger (TFR-grade), capability classifications, restoration records, and wall incidents in aggregate.

**The LEARN loop.** Every cap-crossing, wall breach, backstop event, and near-cap pattern enters the LEARN Dispatcher ([AI Eudaimonia appendix](AI_Eudaimonia_Appendix.md)): ANALYZE the full causal chain (the allocation, the architecture, the incentive, not the proximate spike alone); RECOMMEND-PREVENTION in hazard-control order, eliminate the over-draw pathway, then engineer the constraint, then administer the oversight, routed to the authority that can act; bounded by RESTRAIN so prevention never becomes surveillance of users or communities. The goal is not only non-repetition but the building of conditions in which that class of incident does not arise.

## 11. Implementation Examples {#implementation-examples}

**1. The Toxics Release Inventory (success, the D precedent).** Context: post-Bhopal right-to-know legislation mandated itemized public disclosure of chemical releases (1986), with no reduction mandate attached. What was done: facilities reported; the public, press, and investors read. Outcome: reported on- and off-site releases fell 45 percent from 1988 to 1998, disclosure alone driving reduction (EPA, 1998 TRI data release). Lessons: the itemized public ledger is an enforcement mechanism in itself, which is TFR's design bet.

**2. The Dalles water disclosure (failure then correction, the TFR case).** Context: The Dalles, Oregon, sued The Oregonian in 2021 to keep Google's water consumption secret as a trade secret, with Google funding the suit. What was done: after thirteen months the city settled and disclosed; the figures showed 355 million gallons in 2021, 29 percent of the city's water, nearly triple the 2017 draw. Outcome: public planning became possible; the operator's subsequent reporting improved. Lessons: quantities are not trade secrets; the security line (quantities in, schematics out) would have prevented the litigation entirely.

**3. Efficiency without a cap (failure, the AFC rationale).** Context: fleet-average data-center efficiency (PUE) improved dramatically across the 2010s. What was done: per-unit optimization succeeded industry-wide. Outcome: total sector consumption rose regardless; the efficiency gains funded expansion (the rebound). Masanet et al. (Science, 2020) found global data-centre energy use up 6 percent from 2010 to 2018 while compute rose 550 percent; the IEA then reported 415 TWh in 2024, growing about 12 percent a year since 2017. Lessons: FER without AFC is Jevons's confusion of ideas, compiled; the cap does what efficiency cannot do.

**4. Clean-baseload procurement (success with a caveat, the cleaner-source duty).** Context: hyperscale operators contracting restarted and new nuclear capacity for AI load (2024 onward). What was done: long-term offtake agreements moved AI draw toward carbon-free baseload: Microsoft and Constellation (September 2024, a twenty-year agreement to restart Three Mile Island Unit 1, 835 MW), Amazon and Talen (960 MW from Susquehanna), Google and Kairos (500 MW of small modular reactors from 2030). Outcome: the carbon dimension of the draw improves; the water and materials dimensions remain, which is why the account is dimension-wise. Lessons: the cleaner source discharges part of B; it raises no cap and repays no basin.

**5. Proof-of-work moratoria (the allocation precedent).** Context: jurisdictions confronted concentrated compute draw on constrained grids and enacted moratoria on new fossil-powered proof-of-work mining (New York, signed 22 November 2022: a two-year bar on new or renewed permits for carbon-fuelled generation serving proof-of-work mining). What was done: an absolute, category-level cap. Outcome: the binding-constraint logic (the grid and airshed as the share) prevailed over per-unit arguments. Lessons: societies already cap compute draw when the stakes are visible; AFC generalizes the instrument from emergency to architecture.
